### 语言点 1：`walk a fine line between A and B`

#### 1 — 基础陈述句
- **训练意图**：表达品牌在本地化和自身特色之间保持平衡。
- **学生答案**：`This brand must walk a fine line between adjusting the local market and keeping its characters, especially when running into issues.`
- **评价**：✅ `walk a fine line between A and B` 骨架正确，`run into issues` 也能主动复用。
- **不足**：`adjust the local market` 表示“调整市场”，不是“适应市场”；`characters` 多指人物/文字/性格，不适合表达品牌特征。
- **修正**：`This brand must walk a fine line between adapting to the local market and maintaining its distinctive character, especially when it runs into issues.`

#### 2 — 因果陈述句
- **训练意图**：方案到位后，品牌是否还需兼顾本地化和身份。
- **学生答案**：`The proposal is in place, the brand doesn't need to walk a fine line between localization and the brand identity.`
- **评价**：✅ 目标结构使用正确。
- **不足**：两个完整句子只用逗号连接，构成 comma splice；`the brand identity` 在泛指该品牌自身身份时通常直接说 `its brand identity`。
- **修正**：`The proposal is in place, so the brand no longer needs to walk a fine line between localization and its brand identity.`

#### 3 — 特殊疑问句
- **训练意图**：询问品牌如何兼顾两个目标。
- **学生答案**：`When the plan is in place, how does the brand walk a fine line between adapting to the local market and its brand identity?`
- **评价**：✅ 疑问句语序正确，`adapt to the local market` 已修复。
- **不足**：`between` 两边不平行：左边是动名词行为，右边只是名词。
- **修正**：`When the plan is in place, how does the brand walk a fine line between adapting to the local market and maintaining its brand identity?`

#### 4 — 反义疑问句
- **学生答案**：`The plan is in place and the brand is walking a fine line between adapting to the local market and maintaining its brand identity, isn't it?`
- **评价**：🏆 两个 `-ing` 结构平行，Tag `isn't it?` 与主语 `the brand`、谓语 `is walking` 匹配。
- **不足**：无关键错误。上下文若描述长期固定做法，可改一般现在时；若描述当前执行状态，进行时完全成立。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 5 — 选择疑问句
- **学生答案**：`When issues occur, does the brand walk a fine line between adapting to the local market and maintaining its brand identity, or just follow the trend?`
- **评价**：✅ 目标结构、疑问句和两个选项的逻辑都清楚。
- **不足**：无关键语法错误；更严格的平行结构可补出主语 `does it just follow...`。
- **修正**：`When issues occur, does the brand walk a fine line between adapting to the local market and maintaining its brand identity, or does it just follow the trend?`

#### 6 — Type 3 条件句
- **学生答案**：`If the brand had not walked a fine line between adapting to the local market and maintaining its brand identity, would the project have run into issues?`
- **评价**：🏆 `had not walked` + `would ... have run` 的过去虚拟结构正确，目标短语完整。
- **不足**：无关键错误。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 7 — Quick Fire 自由表达
- **学生答案**：`Well, I think we need to walk a fine line between maintaining our brand identity and adapting to the local market.`
- **评价**：🏆 自然、完整，两个动名词并列平行。
- **不足**：无。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 8 — Quick Fire 扩展表达
- **学生答案**：`Well, I'll step in, but I will not be in favor of any of them. And I'll walk a fine line between quick action and overacting.`
- **评价**：✅ 成功复用了 `step in`、`be in favor of` 和目标结构。
- **不足**：`overacting` 通常指表演过火；这里的“反应过度”应为 `overreacting`。`not ... any of them` 可以理解，但两个方案时 `won't favor either option` 更自然。
- **修正**：`Well, I'll step in, but I won't favor either option. I'll walk a fine line between acting quickly and overreacting.`

### 语言点 2：`localize`（使内容、产品或材料适应当地市场）

#### 9 — 转折陈述句
- **学生答案**：`The plan is in place, but after entering the new market, we still need to localize.`
- **评价**：✅ `localize` 可在宾语由上下文明确时省略宾语，`enter the market` 搭配正确。
- **不足**：`the new market` 需要前文已明确是哪一个市场；泛指进入新市场时用 `a new market`。
- **修正**：`The plan is in place, but after entering a new market, we still need to localize.`

#### 10 — 否定陈述句
- **学生答案**：`The material is in place. We don't need to localize it any more.`
- **评价**：✅ 代词 `it` 与单数/不可数 `material` 一致，`localize it` 搭配正确。
- **不足**：英式 `any more` 和美式 `anymore` 都可；需根据语境确认 `material` 是一份/整体材料，而不是多份具体物料。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 11 — 情境疑问句
- **学生答案**：`With the campaign running into issues, should we localize all the materials?`
- **评价**：🏆 `with + 名词 + doing` 结构正确，`run into issues` 和 `localize all the materials` 都自然。
- **不足**：无关键错误。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 12 — 反义疑问句
- **学生答案**：`With the campaign running to issues, we still need to localize, don't we?`
- **评价**：✅ Tag `don't we?` 正确，`still` 也被保留。
- **不足**：固定搭配是 `run into issues`，不能说 `run to issues`。
- **修正**：`With the campaign running into issues, we still need to localize, don't we?`

#### 13 — 选择疑问句第一次尝试
- **学生答案**：`When the plan is in place, do we just localize materials or all?`
- **评价**：✅ 已建立选择问句框架。
- **不足**：`materials or all` 比较对象不完整，`all` 后需要明确是 `all of them`；如果对比“部分还是全部”，应显式写出 `some`。
- **修正**：`When the plan is in place, do we localize only some of the materials or all of them?`

#### 14 — 选择疑问句修正版
- **学生答案**：`With the plan in place, do we just localize the materials or the entire business?`
- **评价**：🏆 `With the plan in place` 简洁自然，两个选择对象明确。
- **不足**：`localize the entire business` 语义较宽，但如果题意确实是调整整个业务模式，就可以成立。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 15 — Type 3 条件句
- **学生答案**：`If the team had not stepped in in time, would the materials have been localized?`
- **评价**：🏆 `had not stepped in` + `would ... have been localized` 的条件句和被动语态都正确；连续两个 `in` 各有功能，不算错误。
- **不足**：无关键错误。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 16 — panic 结构错误
- **学生答案**：`Well, there's no need to be a panic for us, even if the materials have not been localized.`
- **评价**：✅ `even if` 从句逻辑清楚，`materials have not been localized` 正确。
- **不足**：`panic` 不能在这里作为“我们成为的一阵恐慌”；`there is no need` 后可接 `for us to panic` 或名词 `for panic`。
- **修正**：`Well, there's no need for us to panic, even if the materials haven't been localized.`

#### 17 — panic 修正版
- **学生答案**：`Well, there's no need for us to panic. Even though the materials have not been localized, we can start to localize them now.`
- **评价**：🏆 `there's no need for us to panic` 已修复，复数 `materials / them` 一致。
- **不足**：`start localizing them` 通常比 `start to localize them` 更顺口，但两者都正确。
- **自然版本**：`Well, there's no need for us to panic. Even though the materials haven't been localized, we can start localizing them now.`

#### 18 — Quick Fire 长句整合
- **学生答案**：`Well, in order to be prepared for our action to go into a new market, I think we need to localize what we have, such as materials. And I think we need to walk a fine line between adapting to the local market and maintaining our brand identity. By the way, the backup plan is in place.`
- **评价**：✅ 能串联 `localize`、`walk a fine line`、`brand identity` 和 `in place`，逻辑完整。
- **不足**：`be prepared for our action to go into` 过度名词化且不自然；`what we have, such as materials` 太模糊；正式表达不建议用 `And` 另起一句。
- **修正**：`Well, to prepare for our entry into a new market, I think we need to localize what we already have, such as our marketing materials. We also need to walk a fine line between adapting to the local market and maintaining our brand identity. By the way, the backup plan is already in place.`

### 语言点 3：`localize ... so much better`

#### 19 — 名词化表达
- **学生答案**：`With the process in place, the localization is so much better.`
- **评价**：✅ 句子语法基本成立，可以理解为“本地化成果好得多”。
- **不足**：本轮目标是动词 `localize`；使用名词 `the localization` 回避了目标结构，而且“好得多”的比较基准未给出。
- **修正**：`With the process in place, we can localize the content so much better.`

#### 20 — 目标结构修正版
- **学生答案**：`With the process in place, we can localize the content so much better.`
- **评价**：🏆 `with + 名词 + in place`、`localize the content` 和 `so much better` 全部正确。
- **不足**：无。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 21 — `Just because` 让步结构
- **学生答案**：`Just because the process is in place, it doesn't mean that the team localize so much better.`
- **评价**：✅ `Just because A, it doesn't mean B` 逻辑正确。
- **不足**：`the team` 是单数集体主语时谓语应为 `localizes`；更重要的是语义需要表达能力或结果，宜用 `can localize the content`。目标宾语 `the content` 被漏掉。
- **修正**：`Just because the process is in place, it doesn't mean that the team can localize the content so much better.`

#### 22 — 一般过去时疑问句
- **学生答案**：`With the new process in place, did the team localize the contents so much better?`
- **评价**：✅ `did + 主语 + 原形` 的疑问句语序正确。
- **不足**：泛指营销/文本内容通常用不可数 `content`，不是 `contents`；`contents` 更常指目录、容器内的东西或书刊各部分。
- **修正**：`With the new process in place, did the team localize the content so much better?`

#### 23 — Tag Question
- **学生答案**：`After the expert stepped in, we localized the content so much better, didn't we?`
- **评价**：🏆 `stepped in`、过去时 `localized` 和 Tag `didn't we?` 完全匹配。
- **不足**：无关键错误。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 24 — 原因选择问句（名词作主语）
- **学生答案**：`Did the localization become so much better because of the process or because the expert stepped in?`
- **评价**：✅ `because of + 名词` 与 `because + 从句` 区分正确。
- **不足**：`the localization became better` 可以指成果，但本轮仍绕开了目标动词 `localize`。
- **修正**：`Did we localize the content so much better because of the process or because the expert stepped in?`

#### 25 — 原因选择问句（目标动词）
- **学生答案**：`Did we localize the content so much better because the process was in place or the expert stepped in?`
- **评价**：✅ 已主动回到 `localize the content so much better`。
- **不足**：第二个选项前补 `because` 会使长句结构更清楚，虽然省略后仍可理解。
- **修正**：`Did we localize the content so much better because the process was in place or because the expert stepped in?`

#### 26 — Type 3 条件问句第一次尝试
- **学生答案**：`If the consultant had not stepped in, would have we localized the content so much better?`
- **评价**：✅ If 从句 `had not stepped in` 正确。
- **不足**：疑问句中助动词必须放主语前，但完成结构的顺序是 `would + subject + have + pp`，不是 `would have + subject`。
- **修正**：`If the consultant had not stepped in, would we have localized the content so much better?`

#### 27 — Type 3 条件问句修正版
- **学生答案**：`If the backup process had not been in place, would we have localized the content so much better?`
- **评价**：🏆 `had not been in place` + `would we have localized` 结构完全正确。
- **不足**：无。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 28 — `with` 结构误用
- **学生答案**：`With running into issues, we localized the content so much better.`
- **评价**：⚠️ 想用 `with` 建立背景，方向可以理解。
- **不足**：`with` 后缺少动作执行者，不能直接说 `with running into issues`；而且“遇到问题”不自然地直接导致“做得更好”。
- **修正**：`After running into issues, we ended up localizing the content so much better.`

#### 29 — 时间/结果关系修正版
- **学生答案**：`After running into issues, we ended up localizing the content so much better.`
- **评价**：🏆 `After + doing` 主语一致，`ended up + doing` 使用自然，因果被弱化为经历后的结果，更符合逻辑。
- **不足**：无关键错误。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

### 语言点 4：`in a way` / `in some way`

#### 30 — `in some way` 初次产出
- **学生答案**：`With the process in place, foreign brands can be like local brands in some way.`
- **评价**：✅ `in some way` 可以表示“在某个尚未说明的方面/以某种方式”。
- **不足**：`be like local brands` 比较笼统；如果说话者已知评价角度是“更像本地品牌”，`in a way` 更像评论视角，但 `in some way` 并非错误。
- **修正**：`With the process in place, foreign brands can, in some way, become more like local brands.`

#### 31 — 已知角度的评注
- **学生答案**：`In a way, the fact that the process is in place also constrains us.`
- **评价**：🏆 `In a way` 正确修饰整句，表达“从某个角度看”；`the fact that...` 作主语语法正确。
- **不足**：无关键错误。若想更简洁，可把从句名词化为 `the process being in place`。
- **自然版本**：`In a way, the process being in place also constrains us.`

#### 32 — 重新评价事件
- **学生答案**：`In a way, this delay isn't a disappointment.`
- **评价**：🏆 简洁自然，准确表达“从某种角度看，这次延误并非坏事”。
- **不足**：无。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 33 — 未知方式疑问句
- **学生答案**：`In some way, did the expert intervention solve the issue?`
- **评价**：✅ `in some way` 能表达“是否以某种方式起了作用”。
- **不足**：句首位置略显生硬；`the expert intervention` 可用，但 `the expert's intervention` 更明确。
- **修正**：`Did the expert's intervention solve the issue in some way?`

#### 34 — 辨析选择回答
- **学生答案**：`In a way.`
- **评价**：✅ 在已知说话者要给出一个评价角度的上下文中，选择正确。
- **不足**：这是简短选择回答，不是完整翻译句；保留以记录辨析过程。
- **完整示例**：`In a way, the delay helped us.`

#### 35 — `in a way` 插入位置
- **学生答案**：`Do you agree, in a way, the backup plan avoids a moment of panic?`
- **评价**：✅ 已理解 `in a way` 可作插入评注。
- **不足**：`agree` 后接完整从句需要 `that`；当前两个逗号把 `in a way` 放在 `agree` 和宾语从句之间，结构不自然。
- **修正**：`Do you agree that, in a way, the backup plan prevents a moment of panic?`

#### 36 — `in a way` + intervention
- **学生答案**：`Do you agree that in a way the technical group's intervention saved the project?`
- **评价**：✅ `agree that + 从句` 已修复，`in a way` 可修饰后面的判断。
- **不足**：`technical group` 不如 `technical team` 常见；插入语加逗号后更易读。
- **修正**：`Do you agree that, in a way, the technical team's intervention saved the project?`

#### 37 — 程度性判断
- **学生答案**：`In a way, the backup process is in place, isn't it?`
- **评价**：✅ Tag `isn't it?` 正确。
- **不足**：`in place` 通常描述是否已经建立/就绪，偏事实状态；用 `in a way` 弱化成“某种意义上已到位”需要具体上下文，否则显得含糊。
- **修正**：`In a sense, the backup process is already in place, isn't it?`

#### 38 — 选择问句
- **学生答案**：`In a way, is the delay or running into issues more helpful?`
- **评价**：✅ 能把两个事件作为比较对象。
- **不足**：`the delay` 是名词，`running into issues` 是动名词事件，形式不完全对称；`helpful` 需要隐含“对谁/对什么有帮助”。
- **修正**：`In a way, which was more helpful to the team: the delay or the issues we ran into?`

#### 39 — 比较项名词化第一次尝试
- **学生答案**：`In a way, which is more important? The process is in place or the manager's intervention.`
- **评价**：✅ 比较逻辑明确。
- **不足**：问号过早结束句子；`the process is in place` 是从句，`the manager's intervention` 是名词短语，比较项结构不平行。
- **修正**：`In a way, which is more important: the process being in place or the manager's intervention?`

#### 40 — 名词化比较
- **学生答案**：`In a way, which is more key, the experts' intervention or the backup plan being in place?`
- **评价**：✅ 已成功使用 `the backup plan being in place` 作为名词化比较项。
- **不足**：`more key` 不自然，通常说 `more important`；若指一位专家，用 `the expert's intervention`。
- **修正**：`In a way, which is more important, the expert's intervention or the backup plan being in place?`

#### 41 — 过去时间比较
- **学生答案**：`In a way, which was more important, the expert's intervention last week or the backup plan being in place?`
- **评价**：🏆 过去时 `was` 与 `last week` 匹配，两个比较项均为名词化结构。
- **不足**：为使两个选项的时间都清楚，可写 `the fact that the backup plan was in place`，但原句可成立。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 42 — Type 3 条件问句
- **学生答案**：`In a way, if the expert had not stepped in, would the delay have helped us?`
- **评价**：🏆 `had not stepped in` + `would ... have helped` 完全正确，`in a way` 对整句作视角评注。
- **不足**：无关键错误。
- **自然版本**：原句可直接使用。

#### 43 — `prevent ... from doing`
- **学生答案**：`In a way, it prevented the team from having a moment of panic.`
- **评价**：🏆 `prevented + 人 + from doing` 结构准确，历史短语 `a moment of panic` 完整保留 `of`。
- **不足**：`it` 必须有清晰前文指代；独立使用时应改成具体主语。
- **修正**：`In a way, the backup plan prevented the team from having a moment of panic.`

#### 44 — 偏好与代词指代
- **学生答案**：`Well, these issues show the preferences of the local users. In a way, it helped the team optimize the content.`
- **评价**：✅ `the preferences of local users` 和 `optimize the content` 语义清楚。

## 逐句记录后的集中诊断

### 🔴 最需要优先修复的问题

1. **Comma Splice 复发且有重复提交证据**
   - 出现于 #2、#52、#53、#54、#58、#67。

   - #26：`would have we localized` ❌ → `would we have localized`。

| `distinct` 和 `distinctive` | `distinct` 强调“不同、可区分”；`distinctive` 强调“有鲜明特色、容易识别”。品牌特征通常用 `distinctive`。 |
| `run into` 的主语 | 人、团队、项目、品牌等都可以作主语，只要语义上是“遭遇问题”。 |
| `in a way` 和 `in some way` | `in a way` 通常给出说话者已知的评价角度；`in some way` 常表示方式尚不明确或“以某种方式”。具体选择依赖上下文。 |
| `prevent` 和 `avoid` | `prevent + 事件/人 + from doing` 强调阻止结果发生；`avoid + 名词/doing` 强调主动避开。备选方案阻止恐慌发生时，`prevent panic` 更直接。 |
| `and` 能否表示因果 | `and` 本身主要表示顺承；事件先后及语境可让读者推断因果，但强因果宜用 `so`、`thereby` 等。 |
| `the process being in place` / `the manager stepping in` | 把完整事件 `the process is in place` / `the manager steps in` 名词化，用作比较项、主语或介词宾语。 |
| `a user's preference(s)` | 单数可指一个具体偏好或总体倾向；复数指同一用户在多个方面的偏好。`local users' preferences` 指一群用户的多项偏好。 |
| `content` 可数与不可数 | 不可数时指信息/内容整体；复数 `contents` 多指容器内容物、目录或书刊各部分，不宜泛指普通营销内容。 |
| `entry` in `market entry` | 指“进入市场”这一行动/阶段，不是入口。 |
| `there may be no way` 和 `there may not be any way` | 两者都正确；`no way` 往往更直接、更强，`not ... any way` 更中性。本次训练要求保留后者。 |
| `material` 和 `materials` | 不可数单数表示材料/素材的整体概念；复数表示海报、手册、文案等多份具体物料。 |
| `manuals` 和 `brochures` | `manuals` 是说明/操作手册；`brochures` 是宣传册。中文“手册”必须结合用途判断。 |

## 历史语言点复用

- `step in`：多次正确作不及物短语使用，可恢复为 🟢。
- `run into issues`：绝大多数使用正确；出现一次 `running to issues` 后，后续多次修复并稳定使用。
- `in place`：在 process、backup plan、security measures 等语境中多次正确使用，维持 🟢。
- `brand identity`：与 `maintain`、`balance`、`localize` 的搭配已能主动组织。

## 下次训练安排

1. 开场做 2 题修饰词完整性 Flash Correct，强制保留 `on time / still / all / yet`。
2. 做 2 题现在完成时被动的 Tag：`have/has been + pp` → `haven't/hasn't + pronoun`。
3. 对比训练 `marketing material has/it` 与 `marketing materials have/they`，加入 `some/all/not all`。
4. 无提示复测 `there may not be any way to do`，不得改写成近义结构。
5. 加 2 题 comma splice 修复，要求分别使用 `so` 和句号/分号。
6. 隔场抽查 `trickier / the trickiest`、`would we have + pp`、`get/invite sb to step in`。

## 出题约束（根据学生反馈）

- 中文题干控制在约 20—25 个汉字内。
- 涉及时态、说话者立场、固定做法或未来操作时，必须给足上下文，不让多个答案在信息不足时同样成立。
- 中文提示要自然，并明确关键语义（如 `all`、`still`、`on time`），避免学生因题意歧义被迫猜测。
