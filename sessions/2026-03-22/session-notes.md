## 闪电纠偏记录

### 纠偏题 1: `yesterday` + 时态
- **中文**: "那个关于 API 的遗留 Bug 在昨天的代码评审中并没有被指派（assign）给任何人。"
- **学生答案**: `The API bug wasn't assigned to anyone in yesterday's code review meeting.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 精准使用了一般过去时被动语态 `wasn't assigned`，完美避开了 `hasn't been` 的陷阱。**`yesterday + has been` 的顽固问题首次在闪电纠偏中零回退！**

### 纠偏题 2: `supposed` 拼写 + 语法
- **中文**: "按照排期，你今天本来应该（be supposed to）在调查（look into）那个线上故障的。"
- **学生答案**: `According to the schedule, you were supposed to look into the production issue.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `supposed` 拼写（末尾 **d**）正确无误。`were supposed to` 精准表达了"原本应该……但实际并非如此"的职场语气。

### 纠偏题 3: `shipped` 拼写 + Mixed Conditional
- **中文**: "要是当初那个功能被早点发布（ship），我们现在就不需要做这些额外的工作了。"
- **学生答案**: `If the feature could have been shipped earlier, we would not have to do extra bits of work now.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `shipped`（双写 **p**）拼写正确。Mixed Conditional `could have been shipped` (过去虚拟) + `would not have to ... now` (现在虚拟) 逻辑丝滑。`earlier` 使用正确（非 `early`）。`extra bits of work` 跨模块复用出色。

### 纠偏题 4: 条件句时态匹配
- **中文**: "如果这周我们不清理积压任务（backlog），下周我们就会遇到麻烦。"
- **学生答案**: `If we don't clean up the backlog this week, we will have some problems next week.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `don't` + `will` 的真实条件句时态完美匹配，成功避开了 `don't + would` 的混搭陷阱。

> **闪电纠偏总结**: 🏆🏆🏆🏆 **4/4 全胜！** 这是历史上第一次闪电纠偏全对。上节课暴露的四个顽固缺口（`yesterday + tense`、`supposed` 拼写、`shipped` 拼写、条件句匹配）全部零回退。标志着学生已进入高阶肌肉记忆阶段。

## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 1: an evolution of (nomenclature) over time（随时间推移的（术语）演变）

#### Round 1 — 带否定的陈述句 (Negative Statement)
- **中文**: "这种敏捷流程其实并不是在一夜之间就设计好的，而是随着时间的推移（an evolution of ... over time）一个逐渐演变的过程。"
- **学生答案**: `This agile process was not built just overnight; It's an evolution over time.`
- **评价**:
  - 🏆 `not built just overnight` 表达非常到位，极具职场应对的稳重感。
  - ✅ `an evolution over time` 精准概括了核心意思。
  - ✅ `was not built` (被动语态) 使用完全正确。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句 (Special Question)
- **中文**: "既然（Now that）这种反馈机制一直在变，那么在过去三年中，我们这个开发团队的命名到底经历（be）了怎样的一种随着时间而产生的演变（an evolution of ... over time）呢？"
- **学生答案**: `now that the feedback mechanism is always changing, what naming exactly in this development team has gone through over time in the past 3 years?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `Now that` 使用精准。
  - ✅ 核心逻辑清晰。
  - **地道性建议**: 课文中提到"命名/术语"时用了一个非常高级的词 **`nomenclature`**。在 IT 讨论命名规范时更专业。
  - **句式结构建议**: "经历演变" 最地道的表达通常是 **`What exactly has the evolution of [something] been?`** 或 **`What kind of evolution has [something] gone through?`**

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句 (Tag Question)
- **中文**: "那个系统的术语演变（an evolution of ... over time）其实一直都是在后台（in the background）进行的，对吧？"
- **学生答案**: `The evolution of the naming in the system has been in the background, hasn't it?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **Tag Question 满分！** `has been ... hasn't it?` 极性匹配稳如泰山。`in the background` 跨模块复用自然。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句 (Choice Question)
- **中文**: "你是准备要做一次彻底的重写（rewrite），还是觉得目前这种由于时间流逝自发产生的术语演变（an evolution of nomenclature over time）其实已经足够用了呢？"
- **学生答案**: `would you guess it's more appropriate to have the individual head up the evolution over time or think of another approach?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ 平行结构 `to have ... head up` 与 `(to) think of another approach` 构成了很好的平行对照。
  - ✅ `would you guess` 作为整个句子的"委婉引导"，非常自然。
  - ✅ **`appropriate`** 用在职场排期的场合里非常有说服力。

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气 (Mixed Conditional)
- **中文**: "要是一开始我们就没有经历这种随时间产生的术语演变（an evolution of nomenclature over time），我们现在肯定已经陷入（stuck in）巨大的混乱之中了。"
- **学生答案**: `If we had not gone through the evolution of nomenclature over time at first, we would definitely have stuck in this great chaos.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ If 从句 `had not gone through` 完全正确。
  - **🔴 Mixed Conditional 主句时态偏差**: 原题说的是"现在肯定已经陷入"（**现在**的状态），应为 Type 2 的 **`would definitely be stuck`**，而非 Type 3 的 `would have stuck`。
  - **修正**: `If we had not gone through the evolution of nomenclature over time, we **would definitely be stuck** in this chaos.`

### 语言点 2: I would guess（我个人觉得/我估计）

#### Round 1 — 带否定的陈述句 (Negative Statement)
- **中文**: "我个人的感觉是，这种术语演变（evolution of nomenclature）其实并不会在短时间内（any time soon）就自然而然地发生。"
- **学生答案**: `I'd guess the evolution of nomenclature does not actually happen in a short time, naturally.`
- **评价**:
  - 🏆 `I'd guess` 开场语气到位，一下子那种"职场资深开发"在做架构预估的感觉就出来了。
  - ✅ **`naturally`** (自然而然地) 放在句末的使用非常地道。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句 (Special Question)
- **中文**: "既然（Now that）这个开发文档的术语（nomenclature）存在混乱，到底什么时候这种由于时间产生的演变（evolution over time）才会真正发生呢？你个人觉得到底（would you guess）是几周还是几个月？"
- **学生答案**: `Now that the nomenclature in the development document is messy, when exactly will the evolution over time will happen? Would you guess a couple of weeks or several months?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `Now that` 使用精准。
  - ✅ `Would you guess` 的语序使用正确。
  - **🔴 重复助动词**: `when exactly **will** the evolution... **will** happen?` — 只需要第一个 `will` 就可以了。
  - **修正**: `...when exactly **will** the evolution over time happen?`

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句 (Tag Question)
- **中文**: "如果要让那个特定的新人（individual）去全权负责（head up）这次随时间产生的术语演变（evolution over time），我猜（I'd guess）他肯定会有压力的，对吧？"
- **学生答案**: `if the individual is assigned to head up this evolution of nomenclature over time, I'd guess he'll be stressed out, won't he?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **跨模块大串联满分！**
  - ✅ `individual` + `head up` + `nomenclature` + `evolution over time` 四块积木拼合得严丝合缝。
  - ✅ Tag Question 逻辑完美：观点帽（I guess）→ Tag 针对从句中的主体（he'll）→ `won't he?` 极性匹配满分。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句 (Choice Question)
- **中文**: "你到底（would you guess）是觉得让那个特定的人（individual）去负责（head up）这场随时间产生的演变（evolution over time）更合适，还是觉得另外想个办法会更好呢？"
- **学生答案**: `would you guess it's more appropriate to have the individual head up the evolution over time or think of another approach?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **平行结构（Parallel Structure）处理得很好！**
  - ✅ `to have ... head up` 与 `(to) think of another approach` 构成了很好的平行对照。
  - ✅ `would you guess` 作为整个句子的"委婉引导"，非常自然。
  - ✅ **`appropriate`** 用在职场排期的场合里非常有说服力。

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气 (Mixed Conditional)
- **中文**: "要是我当时没猜错（hadn't guessed wrong）的话，那个特定的人（individual）现在肯定已经在全权负责（head up）这场随时间发生的术语演变（evolution of nomenclature over time）了。"
- **学生答案**: `if I hadn't guessed wrong at that time, the individual would definitely head up the evolution of nomenclature over time.`
- **评价**:
  - 🏆 Mixed Conditional 精准捕捉到了"过去假设（hadn't guessed）"与"现在的推断（would definitely head up）"的组合。
  - **地道性建议**: 针对"现在已经在负责"这个**进行状态**，用 **`would be heading up`** 会比 `would definitely head up` 更有画面感。

### 47BEC Role-play: Jira 架构治理讨论会

- **场景**: 作为 Senior SE，与产品经理 Nancy 讨论 Nomenclature（术语）混乱的问题。Nancy 抱怨客户对 Requirement 和 Issue 分不清。
- **学生原文**:
  > Well, actually this is the legacy system and the evolution of nomenclature over time built up the current situation.
  > So if you want to clarify all these item names in our system, I think you need to put up an requirement. And then we can put it back from our backlog and it was supposed to be finished by next month, but I see it's urgent to finish it as soon as possible.
  > I'd guess if you put it into the current iteration, it'll be shipped by next week.
  > So I think let's look into it after this meeting, because this meeting was supposed to talk about the future plan for our new product.
- **核心语言点集成**:
  - ✅ `evolution of nomenclature over time`: 放在句首解释原因，非常自然。
  - ✅ `backlog`: 提到"从积压任务中拿出来"，语境极佳。
  - ✅ `I'd guess`: 给出了精准的排期预估（shipped by next week），留了余地。
  - ✅ `look into`: 放在会后（after the meeting）处理紧急问题，非常符合会议逻辑。
  - ✅ `be supposed to`: 两次使用，分别表达了"原计划"和"会议的主题"，满分应用。
- **职场软化词使用**:
  - ✅ `Well, actually...` — 开场自然。
  - ✅ `I'd guess` — 委婉给出排期估计。
  - ✅ `I think let's...` — 柔化建议。
- **不足与纠错**:

| 原始表达 | 问题 | 修正/地道表达 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `an requirement` | **🔴 冠词错误 (a vs an)**。`r` 是辅音开头用 `a`，不是 `an` | **`a requirement`** |
| `put up a requirement` | **搭配不精准**。`put up` 偏向"张贴/搭建"，提交需求用 `put in` 更准确 | **`put in a requirement`** 或 **`raise a request`** |
| `put it back from our backlog` | **语义歧义**。`put back from` 容易理解为"放回去推迟"，而原意是"拿出来" | **`pull it from our backlog`** |

- **表现评价**: 🏆 逻辑层次清晰（解释现状→提出需求→给出排期→建议后续动作→守住会议主轴），五个语言点全部自然植入。尤其是两次使用 `be supposed to`（一次说原计划，一次守住会议边界），展示了极高的职场沟通掌控力。

- **评价**: 🏆 **Tag Question 匹配成功！** `You want ... don't you?` 的反义疑问句构造毫无破绽。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句 (Choice Question)
- **中文**: "你是打算现在就带我们过一遍（walk through）这些架构细节，还是想把它们全部搁置（table）到明天再说（for tomorrow）？"

- **评价**: 🏆 **平行结构（Parallel Structure）满分！** `Do you want to [walk us through ...] or [table them ...]` 动词原形的对照非常利落。

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气 (Mixed Conditional)
- **中文**: "如果当初我们没有把那个关于积压任务（backlog）的议题搁置（table）这么久，现在肯定早就（already）把这些架构细节（architecture details）处理好了。"

### 语言点 5: separate（单独的/另行的/分开的）

#### 概念辨析: `separate` vs `another`
- **学生的问题**: "这里的 separate 可以改为用 another 吗？"
- **给出的解释**:
  - `another call`: 指"再来一场电话"。重点是**数量**（又一个），语气比较随意。
  - `separate call`: 指"一场**单独的、另辟出来的**电话"。重点是**独立性**（专门为这个议题开一个小房间）。
- **理解程度**: 🏆 学生理解后在后续造句中准确运用了 `separate`。

#### Round 1 — 带否定的陈述句 (Negative Statement)
- **中文**: "既然（Now that）时间快不够了，我们其实不应该（should not）在这里带大家过一遍（walk through）这些关于术语演变（evolution of nomenclature）的细节，我们要另外安排一个（separate）15 分钟的电话会议。"
- **学生答案**: `now that we're running out of time, we should not actually walk us through all these details of the evolution of nomenclature, we should make a separate 15-minute call.`
- **评价**:
  - 🏆 **`running out of time`** 用得非常有水准。这在会议中形容"时间不够了"是最地道的主动表达。
  - ✅ `evolution of nomenclature` 已经彻底成为"肌肉记忆"。
  - **地道性建议**: `make a separate call` 已经很棒，但在正式排程里母语者更爱用 **`schedule a separate call`**。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句 (Special Question)
- **中文**: "既然（Now that）由于幻灯片太模糊（blurry），我们不得不把这个议题暂时搁置（table），那么具体（exactly）是哪个部分我们要另约一个（separate）电话会议来带大家过一遍（walk through）呢？"
- **学生答案**: `Now that the slides are blurry, we have to table this topic. Which part exactly should we schedule a separate call to walk us through?`
- **评价**:
  - 🏆 `which part exactly` 位置精准。
  - ✅ 非常有逻辑地分成了两句：先交待背景（blurry → table），再抛出核心提问（exactly → separate call），职场叙述节奏利落。
  - ✅ `blurry` 和 `table` 的精准嵌入。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句 (Tag Question)
- **中文**: "那个关于系统术语演变（evolution of nomenclature）的讨论，其实是你想要单独开个会议（separate call）来带大家过一遍（walk through）的议题，对吧？"
- **学生答案**: `You want to actually schedule a separate call to walk us through the discussion about the system's evolution of nomenclature, don't you?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **Tag Question 匹配成功！** `You want ... don't you?` 的反义疑问句构造稳固。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句 (Choice Question)
- **中文**: "你是打算单独安排（schedule a separate session）一次带大家过一遍（walk through）这些细节，还是想直接把它们写进会后的总结（key takeaways）里发在会议频道（meeting chat）里？"
- **学生答案**: `Do you want to schedule a separate session to walk us through all the details or directly write the key takeaways in the summary and then send it in the meeting chat?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **平行结构（Parallel Structure）满分！** `Do you want to [schedule ...] or [write ...]` 动词原形的对照非常利落。`schedule a separate session`、`walk us through`、`key takeaways` 和 `meeting chat` 把 `48BEC` 的核心精华打包在一个盒子里。

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气 (Mixed Conditional)
- **中文**: "要是一开始我们没有另约会议（separate call）来带大家过一遍（walk through）这些复杂的术语演变（evolution of nomenclature），我们现在肯定已经被大量的细节（details）和麻烦（issues）给淹没了。"
- **学生答案**: `If we had not scheduled a separate call to walk us through all this complicated evolution of nomenclature, we would definitely be stuck in a lot of details and issues.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **Mixed Conditional 处理得非常好**。`had not scheduled` (过去虚拟) + `would definitely be stuck` (现在结果)，时态匹配精准。

## 重点纠错与不足总结 (Knowledge Gaps)

| 涉及知识点 | 原始错误/不足 | 纠正/改进方向 | 严重程度 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🔴 Mixed Conditional 主句时态偏差** | `would definitely have stuck in chaos` | "现在的状态" → 用 **Type 2** 的 `would (definitely) be stuck`，而非 `would have stuck` | **高** (偶发，非系统性) |
| **🔴 重复助动词** | `when exactly will ... will happen?` | 特殊疑问句中助动词只写一次：`when exactly **will** ... happen?` | **高** (新发现) |
| **🔴 `We walked us through` 主宾冲突** | `we had not walked us through` | 主语是 `We` 时，宾语不能也是 `us`。改为 `walk the team through` 或直接 `walk through` | **中** (出现 2 次) |
| **🔴 `a` vs `an` 冠词** | `an requirement` | `r` 是辅音开头 → 用 **`a`** requirement，不是 `an` | **中** (基础语法) |
| **`it is limited to our time`** | `it is limited to our time` | 主语搞反了。母语者直接说 **`our time is limited`** | **中** (表达不地道) |
| **`put up` vs `put in` (提交需求)** | `put up a requirement` | "提交需求"用 **`put in a requirement`** 或 **`raise a request`** | **中** (搭配不精准) |
| **`put it back from` 语义歧义** | `put it back from our backlog` | "从积压任务中提出来" = **`pull it from our backlog`**；`put back` 容易理解为"放回去/推迟" | **中** (语义歧义) |
| **`until all the people here` 缺谓语** | `until all the people here` | 从句必须完整：**`until everyone is here`** | **中** (句子不完整) |
| **`take a bit long` vs `take too long`** | `take a bit long` 语气偏轻 | 表达"耗时太久"的压力感用 **`take too long`** 更有力度 | **低** (地道性差异) |
