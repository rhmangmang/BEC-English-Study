## 隔夜抽查记录（5/6→5/8）

### 纠偏题 1 — `aware` + `of` + `looks like`
- **中文**: "看起来他完全不知道这个决定背后的风险是什么。"
- **学生答案**: `It looks like he is not aware of the risks behind this decision at all.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `looks like` 直接接从句（没有 `that`），`aware of` 到位，`at all` 捕捉了强调语气。
- **结论**: 🟢 隔夜固化确认

### 纠偏题 2 — `let alone`
- **中文**: "我连用中文都说不清楚这个架构，更别说用英文跟团队同频交流了。"
- **学生答案**: `I cannot express the architecture even in Chinese, let alonespeak their language with the team in English.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `let alone speak their language` — 后接原形短语，**隔夜固化确认** 🏆
  - ❌ `let alonespeak` — 拼写间距（`let alone speak`）
  - ⚠️ `express the architecture` — `express` 偏"表达情感"，说清楚架构用 `explain` / `describe` 更准确
- **结论**: 🟢 隔夜固化确认（`let alone` 结构正确），拼写小问题需注意

### 纠偏题 3 — 条件句 + `so + adj`
- **中文**: "如果你用这么复杂的术语来解释，新同事就不会觉得这个系统简单了。"
- **学生答案**: `If you had explained it in such a complicated term, the new colleague would not have felt the system was so easy.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `so easy` — 没有 `so much`，**隔夜修复确认** 🏆
  - ⚠️ 条件句方向过深 — 用了 Type 3（`If you had explained`），但中文没有给出过去时间信号，偏向一般规律/建议 → 用 Type 1 或 2 更自然
  - ⚠️ `such a complicated term` → `such complicated terms`
- ****整体隔夜抽查结果**: 5/6 中 4 题满分，条件句选择仍需巩固。

## 概念辨析记录

### 话题 1: `was` vs `is` 在虚拟嵌套句（来自 71BEC 延续）
- **学生提问**: `I think what would most likely have happened was that...` 为什么 `was` 不用虚拟，且能不能换成 `is`
- **核心讲解**:
  - `was` 是"支架"——负责连接虚拟假设和它的结果，本身不需要虚拟
  - `was`（母语者大多数用）vs `is`（少数用，强调"结论到现在也成立"）
  - `I think`（现在判断过去假设）vs `I thought`（过去判断过去假设）
- **掌握情况**: 🟢

### 话题 2: 修改 GEMINI.md 指令
- **学生指出**: "如果句式池中的句式都可以用，那就都用"
- **修改内容**: 
  1. 第 113 行：从"2-4 轮皆可"改为"覆盖所有适用的句式，不设轮数上限"
  2. 第 122 行：从"每次选 2-4 个即可"改为"按适用性逐一覆盖"

### 话题 3: `a ground rule`（学生主动追问）
- **学生提问**: "什么意思？"
- **核心讲解**: = 一条基本规则/约法三章。原指棒球场地规则，引申为一开始定好的规矩。`set a ground rule` > `make a rule`
- **掌握情况**: 🟢 当轮 Quick Fire 即复用 🏆

### 话题 4: `stage` vs `phase`（学生主动追问）
- **核心区别**: `stage` = 节点/步骤（不重叠）；`phase` = 时期/状态（可重叠）
- **掌握情况**: 🟢

### 话题 5: `for` vs `to` + 人（学生主动追问）
- **核心规律**: `to + 人` = 主观感受；`for + 人` = 客观影响/受益
- **掌握情况**: 🟢

### 话题 6: `get out for dinner` vs `go out for dinner`（学生主动追问有什么问题）
- **核心区别**: `get out` = 带"逃离/解脱"意味；`go out` = 中性"出去"。聚餐场景用 `go out`
- **掌握情况**: 🟢

## 造句训练记录

### 72BEC 语言点 1: `build into`（把 A 纳入/融入 B）

> 课文原文：*building that into everyone's schedule would probably be a good thing*

#### Round 1 — 肯定陈述句
- **中文**: "我们需要把社交活动固定纳入每个新员工的入职流程里。"
- **学生答案**: `There needs for us to build social activities into every employee's onboarding process.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `build social activities into... onboarding process` — 核心搭配完全正确 🏆
  - ❌ `There needs for us to` — `There needs to be` 和 `We need to` 混搭在一起。`There needs to be` 后不能接 `for sb to do`
- **精修**: `We need to build social activities into every new employee's onboarding process.`

#### Round 2 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "不能只是临时约一顿饭，而是要把社交环节固定纳入入职日程中。"
- **学生答案**: `We should not just arrange a meal. However, we need to build social stages into onboarding process.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `build... into` — 核心短语再次正确
  - ❌ `social stages` — `stages` = 阶段/舞台 → `social activities` / `social elements`
  - ⚠️ `arrange a meal` — 临时约用 `grab a meal` / `throw together a dinner`
- **精修**: `We shouldn't just grab a meal once in a while. We need to build social activities into the onboarding process.`
- **学生追问** `once in a while` 意思——讲解为"偶尔/时不时"

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: "我们已经把社交活动纳入了入职流程，对吧？"
- **学生答案**: `We have built social activities into the onboarding process, haven't we?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** Tag 极性、助动词全部正确

#### Round 4 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: "你们是怎么把社交活动纳入远程入职流程的？"
- **学生答案**: `How did you build social activities into the onboarding process?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 5 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: "你是想把社交时间纳入正常的日程安排里，还是纳入入职流程里？"
- **学生答案**: `Do you want to build social activities into the normal schedule or the onboarding process?`
- **评价**: ✅ 结构正确

#### Round 6 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: "如果我们当初把社交时间纳入了入职流程，新人就不会那么难融入了。"
- **学生答案**: `If we had built social activities into the onboarding process, newcomers would not have trouble fitting in.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `If we had built` — Type 3 从句正确，`build into` 核心短语正确
  - ❌ 结果句时态不匹配 — Type 3 从句 + Type 2 结果句 → `would have had trouble`
- **精修**: `If we had built social activities into the onboarding process, newcomers would not have had trouble fitting in.`

#### Quick Fire
- **场景**: 发现团队周五有固定社交时间
- **学生答案**: `No wonder the atmosphere is good.`
- **评价**: ✅ 场景回应自然。但导师误判要求学生融入 `build into`（Quick Fire 不应强制植入目标语言点），学生纠正后承认错误。
- **结论**: Quick Fire 中 `No wonder` 完全正确，导师反馈偏差已更正 🏆

**语言点 1 总结**:

| 句式 | 结果 |
|:---|:---:|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | ✅ 核心搭配正确；`There needs for us to` 结构混搭需注意 |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | ✅ `social stages` 用词错误 |
| 🟢 反义疑问 | ✅ 满分 |
| 🟢 特殊疑问 | ✅ 满分 |
| 🟢 选择疑问 | ✅ 正确 |
| 🟢 虚拟语气 | ⚠️ 结果句时态偏差，已修正 |

### 72BEC 语言点 2: `talk shop`（聊工作/聊本行）

> 课文原文：*instead of talking shop, we could talk, you know, just about life and everything.*

#### Round 1 — 肯定陈述句
- **中文**: "团队聚餐的时候，大家老是聊工作，都没机会聊聊生活。"
- **学生答案**: `When having a team together, everybody always talks shop and don't get to talk about life and everything.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `talk shop` — 核心短语正确！`shop` 没有加 `s` 🏆
  - ❌ `when having a team together` — "聚餐/聚会" → `When the team gets together for dinner`
  - ❌ `everybody... don't` — `everybody` 是单数 → `doesn't`
- **精修**: `When the team gets together for dinner, everybody always talks shop and doesn't get to talk about life and everything.`

#### Round 2 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "我们约法三章：聚餐的时候可以闲聊，但不许聊工作。"
- **学生答案**: `Let's make a deal: don't talk shop when the team gets together for dinner but we can talk about everything else.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `talk shop` — 核心正确 🏆
  - ❌ `Let's make a deal` — "约法三章"不是做交易 → `Let's set a ground rule`
  - ⚠️ 句式人称不一致（`don't... but we can`）
- **精修**: `Let's set a ground rule: when the team gets together for dinner, no talking shop — we can talk about everything else.`

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: "老板不在的时候你们总会聊几句工作相关的事，对吧？"
- **学生答案**: `When the boss is out, you guys always talk shop, don't you?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** Tag 助动词、极性全部正确

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: "你们聚餐的时候是聊生活还是聊工作？"
- **学生答案**: `Do you talk shop or talk about life when you get together for dinner?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: "如果聚餐的时候大家都不聊工作，氛围会好得多。"
- **学生答案**: `If no one talked shop when getting together for dinner, the atmosphere would be better.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** Type 2 从句 + 结果句完美匹配

#### Quick Fire
- **场景**: 新团队团建，老同事说"我们这边有个规矩：聚餐不聊工作"
- **学生答案**: `We have a ground rule here. No talking shop when getting together for dinner, just talking about life.`
- **评价**: 🏆🏆 **满分！** `No talking shop`（刚精修过的结构当场复用）+ `a ground rule`（刚问过意思立即用上）

**语言点 2 总结**:

| 句式 | 结果 |
|:---|:---:|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | ✅ `team together` 表达需注意 |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | ✅ `make a deal` → `set a ground rule` |
| 🟢 反义疑问 | ✅ 满分 |
| 🟢 选择疑问 | ✅ 满分 |
| 🟢 虚拟语气 | ✅ 满分 |
| 🟢 Quick Fire | 🏏 两处新学点当场复用 |

### 72BEC 语言点 3: `be past something`（熬过某阶段/不再纠结）

> 课文原文：*I'm almost past that a little bit right now.*

#### 概念辨析：表达"经过某个地方"
- **学生提问**: "那如果要表达经过某个地方怎么表达?"
- **核心讲解**: `walk/go/drive/run + past`（动作动词 + past）vs `be + past`（系动词 + past = 状态）。过去时/现在时搭配。
- **掌握情况**: 🟢

#### Round 1 — 肯定陈述句
- **中文**: "刚来的前两周我确实有点懵，但现在我已经过了那个阶段了。"
- **学生答案**: `For the first two weeks, I was actually a little bit confused, but now I'm past that.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 时态切换干净，核心短语正确

#### Round 2 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "我们还没熬过这个阶段，不能现在就放松。"
- **学生答案**: `We have not past this phrase yet, so we cannot let it up.`
- **评价**:
  - ❌ `have not past` — `past` 不是动词，不能接在 `have` 后面 → `are not past`
  - ❌ `this phrase` — `phrase` = 词组/短语 → `stage` / `phase`
  - ❌ `let it up` — `let up` 不及物、不加宾语 → `let our guard down`
- **精修**: `We're not past this stage yet, so we can't let our guard down.`

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: "你已经熬过了最难的那个适应阶段了，对吧？"
- **学生答案**: `You're past the hardest stage, aren't you?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: "你现在是已经熬过了适应期，还是还在摸索阶段？"
- **学生答案**: `Are you past the adjusting stage or still in figuring stage?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `Are you past the adjusting stage` — 正确
  - ❌ `in figuring stage` — 缺冠词 + `figuring` 非标准形容词 → `still in the adjustment phase` / `still figuring it out`
- **精修**: `Are you past the adjustment phase, or are you still figuring it out?`

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: "如果我已经过了那个阶段，我就不会还这么紧张了。"
- **学生答案**: `If I were past the phase, I would not still be nervous.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `If I were past the phase` — Type 2 从句正确
  - ⚠️ `would not still be` — `still` 位置可优化，且 `anymore` 更适合此类"过了阶段就不再"的语境
- **精修**: `If I were past that phase, I wouldn't be this nervous anymore.`

**语言点 3 总结**:

| 句式 | 结果 |
|:---|:---:|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | ✅ 满分 |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | ✅ `past` 不是动词 → `are not past` |
| 🟢 反义疑问 | ✅ 满分 |
| 🟢 选择疑问 | ✅ `figuring stage` 表达需注意 |
| 🟢 虚拟语气 | ✅ 正确；`still` → `anymore` 更自然 |

## 话题构建训练：Onboarding 与 Mentoring

### 背景
学生选择“话题构建训练”，背景：老员工带新人，教他团队聚餐不聊工作的规矩。

### 第一步：中文 Brainstorm
- **学生中文观点**（原文照录）：
  > "现如今，工作和生活平衡，是我们这一代人谈的最经常谈的一件事，他可以很好的提升我们的生活的幸福感。
  > 因此呢我们团队设定了一个规则，也就是在不工作的时候。就不要聊工作而聊你的生活上的事情，大家互相交流。
  > 作为新人呢，在你入职的前几周，在工作的时候，那必须得尽可能的吸收我们的工作流程。
  > 那在工作之外呢，我们尽可能不要聊工作，这样子也能提升或带给新人更好的体验。"

- **导师反馈**: ✅ 逻辑链清晰（为什么定规则→规则内容→工作时做什么→工作外做什么→整体体验）。建议：观点 1 可以从"团队为什么定这个规则"直接切入，省去"工作和生活平衡"的宏大铺垫。

### 第二步：表达骨架供给
- **第一版骨架**: 导师初次给出了完整句子而非骨架模板，学生指出问题（"我看你给的不是骨架，而是所有的表达内容吧"）
- **修正后骨架**:
  - 观点 1: `___ is something ___ talks about a lot. It can actually improve your ___.`
  - 观点 2: `So we set a ground rule: when ___, we don't ___. We ___ instead.`
  - 观点 3: `During ___, just focus on ___ as much as possible.`
  - 观点 4: `And outside of ___, we try not to ___. That way, ___ feels better for you.`

### 第三步：有脚手架输出
- **学生英文**:
  > *Well, work-life balance is something our generation talks about a lot. It can actually improve your well-being. So we set a ground rule when we get out for dinner, we don't talk shop. Instead, we talk something else. However, during onboarding, just focus on consuming knowledge as much as possible. And outside of work, we try not to talk work. That way, it feels better for you.*

- **评价**:

| 骨架 | 使用 |
|:---|:---:|
| 观点 1 | ✅ 两个骨架都用上 |
| 观点 2 | ✅ `ground rule` / `when... don't... instead` 用上 |
| 观点 3 | ✅ **`consuming knowledge` 主动复用 70BEC 旧点** 🏆 |
| 观点 4 | ✅ 用上 |

**精修点**:

| 原文 | 修正 |
|:---|:---|
| `when we get out for dinner` | `when we go out for dinner` |
| `we set a ground rule... we don't` | `we set a ground rule: when... we don't` |
| `talk something else` | `talk **about** something else` |
| `However` | 删掉（并列不转折） |
| `talk work` | `talk shop`（刚学过的） |

### 第四步：撤骨架重说
- **学生英文**:
  > *Well, work-life balance is something our generation talks about a lot. We believe it can actually improve our well-being. So we set a ground rule: we don't talk shop, when we get out for dinner, we talk about something else. And during everyone's onboarding, I believe the best thing they need to do is to consume knowledge as much as possible. And when outside of work, we don't talk shop. That way, the whole onboarding experience feels better for you.*

- **评价**:

| 对比 | 骨架版 | 撤骨架版 |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| 观点 1 | ✅ 保留 + 加了 `We believe` 🏆 |
| 规则表述 | ⚠️ `when... don't...` 语序倒过来了 | |
| `consume knowledge` | ✅ **再次主动复用** 🏆 | |
| `ground rule` | ✅ 保留 | |

**残留问题**:
- `get out for dinner` 仍未修正为 `go out for dinner`（导师反馈中修正了但学生撤骨架时依然用了 `get out`）
- `when we don't talk shop, when we get out for dinner` 条件后置导致语序别扭

### 话题构建训练总结

| 维度 | 评价 |
|:---|:---:|
| 中文内容质量 | 🏆 逻辑链清晰 |

| 骨架内化 | 🏆`consume knowledge` 两次复用、`ground rule` 主动用上 |
| 残留问题 | `get out for dinner` 顽固、条件句语序、`talk work` 而非 `talk shop` |

## 综合重点纠错与不足总结

| 涉及知识点 | 原始错误/不足 | 纠正/改进方向 | 严重程度 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`There needs for us to do`** | `There needs to be` 和 `We need to` 混搭 | 固定用法：`We need to do` | **中**（结构混淆） |
