## 1. 🎯 定向纠偏 (Flash Correct)

### 类别一：不规则动词过去分词（2026-05-16 复发）
**选题原因**：距上次修复 20 天，最久未练的类别。

- **Round 1**: 她已经花了三个小时准备这次报告。
  - 学生：`She has spent three hours preparing this report.`
  - 评价：✅ 满分。`spent` 过去分词正确 ✅

- **Round 2**: 我以前从来没有开车去过那个办公室。
  - 学生：`I have never drove the car to that office.`
  - 评价：❌ **关键性错误**。`drove` → `driven`（`drive-drove-driven`）。`have` 后必须接过去分词。更地道可省略 `the car` → `I've never driven to that office.`

- **Round 3**: 他说他已经写了代码，但还没有测试。
  - 学生：`He said he had written code, but didn't test it.`
  - 评价：✅ `written` 过去分词正确 ✅。冠词遗漏 `the code`（特指）；时态一致性 `hadn't tested` 比 `didn't test` 更自然。

**纠偏小结**：2.5/3。`spent`/`written` 正确 ✅，`driven` 仍为 `drove` ❌ → d drove 已当轮指正。过去分词类别修复进度维持。

### 类别二：间接疑问句语序（2026-05-05，间隔较久）

- **Round 1**: 我不知道他什么时候能到。
  - 学生：`I'm not sure when he can arrive.`
  - 评价：✅ `when he can arrive` 陈述语序正确。

- **Round 2**: 你能不能告诉我这个漏洞有多严重？
  - 学生：`Can you tell me how serious this bug can cause?`
  - 评价：❌ `cause` 用错。`how serious` 问的是"多严重"，不是"导致什么"。应为 `Can you tell me how serious this bug is?`

- **Round 3**: 你能解释一下为什么要做这个改动吗？
  - 学生：`Could you explain why you made this change?`
  - 评价：✅ `why you made this change` 陈述语序正确。

**纠偏小结**：2.5/3。`cause` 用词错误（非语序错误）。

### 类别三：`discuss` 及物性（2026-05-30 新发现）

### LP 1: `stage/step in + doing`（做某事的第 X 步/阶段）— 新学 🆕

**来源**：19BEC — `the first stage in selecting art`
**含义**：`[序数词] + stage/step + in + 动名词`，表"在某个过程中的第 X 步/阶段"
**训练句式**：5/6（虚拟语气跳过——`in + doing` 不适合假设场景）

#### Round 1 (肯定陈述句)
- 中文：在制定项目计划时，第一步是明确你的目标。
- 学生：`the first step in building a project plan is identify your goal.`
- 评价：✅ `step in + doing` → `step in building` 正确 ✅。❌ `is identify` → `is **to** identify`（系动词后不能直接跟原形动词）。应为 `The first step in building a project plan is to identify your goals.`

#### Round 2 (否定陈述句) — 初版
- 中文：测试不是了解用户需求的最后一步，而是第一步。
- 学生：`The first step in testing is to know users' requirements. It's not the last step.`
- 评价：❌ 用了两个句子，违反了"一个否定句"要求。目标应为 `Testing is not the last step in understanding user needs, but the first one.`

**否定句重出**：
- 中文：解释你的需求并不是谈判的第一步，而是准备。
- 学生：`Explaining your needs is not the first step in negotiation, but the preparation.`
- 评价：✅ 否定句+`step in` 结构正确 ✅。`the preparation` → `preparation`（抽象概念一般不加冠词），更佳：`but preparing`（保持动名词平行）。

#### Round 3 (特殊疑问句) ❌ **漏用目标结构**
- 中文：在项目管理中，哪一步对于确保质量最关键？
- 学生：`Which step in project management is key to quality?`
- 评价：❌ 未命中目标。`in project management` = "在项目管理领域"，不是 `in + doing` 结构。应为 `Which step **in ensuring quality** is the most critical?`

#### Round 4 (反义疑问句) ❌ **再次漏用目标结构**
- 中文：制定计划是项目管理中最关键的一步，不是吗？
- 学生：`Building plans is the most important step in project management, isn't it?`
- 评价：❌ 再次漏用 `in + doing`。`step in project management` 不是目标结构。应为 `...the most important step **in planning a project**, isn't it?`

#### Round 5 (选择疑问句)
- 中文：在解决问题时，第一步是分析根源，还是直接去找解决方案？
- 学生：`Is the first step in solving problems to analyze root causes or to directly find solutions?`
- 评价：✅ **满分**。`the first step in solving problems` — `in + doing` 结构正确，`or` 平行结构工整。

#### Quick Fire
- 场景：Your team started using a new PM tool. Colleague asks about the first thing to do.
- 学生：`Well, the first step in understanding workflow creation is for you to read all the documents.`
- 评价：✅ `step in understanding workflow creation` 目标结构正确。`for you to read` 略显指令式，`is to read through` 更自然。

**LP 状态**：🟡 复用中。肯定/否定/选择疑问 ✅ 掌握较好；特殊疑问句和反义疑问句 2 次漏用目标结构，需要强化 `in + 动名词` 的主动运用意识。

### LP 2: `need/want/require + doing`（主动表被动）— 新学 🆕

**来源**：19BEC — `Art needs to be placed` ↔ `Art needs placing`
**含义**：`[物] + need/want/require + doing`，动名词主动形式表被动含义。仅适用于 need/want/require 三词。
**训练句式**：5/6（虚拟语气跳过）

#### Round 1 (肯定陈述句)
- 中文：这堵墙需要重新粉刷。
- 学生：`this wall needs repainting.`
- 评价：✅ **满分**。`needs repainting` 主动表被动，正确且自然。

#### Round 2 (否定陈述句)
- 中文：这个漏洞不需要立即修复，下一版再说。
- 学生：`This bug doesn't need fixing immediately. We can wait until next version.`
- 评价：✅ `doesn't need fixing` 否定结构正确。第一句准确命中目标结构。

#### Round 3 (一般疑问句) ❌ **漏用目标结构**
- 中文：这套系统需要多长时间来升级？
- 学生：`How long will the system need to update?`
- 评价：❌ 用了 `need to update`（`need + to do`），不是 `need + doing`（主动表被动）。`needs to update` = 系统主动去更新；`needs updating` = 系统需要被更新。
- **重出 1 轮**：这个报告还需要审核吗？
  - 学生：`Does the report still need reviewing?`
  - 评价：✅ **修复成功**。`needs...need reviewing`（助动词后 `need` 原形 ✅）— 目标结构用上了。严格说这是一般疑问句，可覆盖特殊疑问句的缺位。

#### Round 4 (反义疑问句)
- 中文：这个流程需要优化，不是吗？
- 学生：`The workflow needs optimizing, doesn't it?`
- 评价：✅ **满分**。`needs optimizing` 目标结构 ✅，`doesn't it?` Tag 极性正确。

#### Round 5 (选择疑问句)
- 中文：这个模块是需要重写还是只需要打补丁？
- 学生：`Does this module needs rewriting or just patching?`
- 评价：⚠️ **关键性错误（助动词+动词形态）**。`Does` 已经是第三人称单数助动词，后面动词应用原形 `need` → `Does this module **need** rewriting or just patching?` 其他部分 `rewriting`/`patching` 均正确 ✅。虽属情态动词+原形缺口（非 `need doing` 本身错误），但影响句子正确性。

#### Quick Fire
- 场景：Colleague says deployment pipeline is outdated and causing issues.
- 学生：`Well, I think the deployment pipeline needs fixing because it causes issues more frequently recently.`
- 评价：✅ `needs fixing` 正确，场景运用自然。`more frequently recently` 略显冗余，`lately` 或 `recently` 选一个即可。

**LP 状态**：🟡 复用中。肯定/否定/反义 ✅ 掌握良好；疑问句 `need to do` 替代 `need doing` 一次、`Does needs` 形态错误一次。核心语义理解到位，句式稳定性需巩固。

### 3. 🎭 Micro Role-play（整合 LP 1-2）

**场景**：你是 IT 项目经理。团队刚上线了新的工单管理系统。你在周会上做简短介绍：① 使用新系统的第一步是理解它的工作流程；② 需要被验证的是系统的稳定性。

**学生产出**：
> Well, thank you all for joining us today and I'd like to say thank you all for creating such a wonderful system. And in order to make sure that everyone will use the system smoothly, I'd like to explain its workflow and let you understand the process of the system. So the first step in understanding its workflow is to read through all the documents and what needs verifying is use cases is not the stability of the system, so the system needs verifying, great, I think.

**目标短语覆盖**：
| 短语 | 是否使用 | 用法评价 |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| `step in + doing` | ✅ | `the first step in understanding its workflow` — 结构正确 |
| `need + doing` | ✅ | `the system needs verifying` — 正确（但中间句式混乱）|

**评价**：✅ 两个目标短语都用了。问题在于：
- 第二句 `what needs verifying is use cases is not the stability` — **两个 `is` 冲突**，语法混乱。应为 `What needs verifying is the **use cases**, not just stability.`
- 开头太长，`I'd like to say thank you all for creating` 可简化成 `Thanks for building this system.`
- `wonderful` 在这个语境下略显夸张

**Micro RP 通过**（目标短语覆盖 ✅，句式流畅度需提升）。

### LP 3: `it is` 口语插入 wh- 从句 — 新学 🆕

**来源**：19BEC — `what kind of work **it is** you do`
**含义**：`wh- 词 + **it is** + 主语 + 谓语`(`that` 可省略)，`it is...that...` 强调句型的口语变形，去掉 `that` 后变成自然的舒缓语气插入结构。
**训练句式**：4/6（虚拟语气跳过——不适合假设场景；疑问句语序持续纠正后掌握）

#### Round 1 (肯定陈述句) ❌ **漏用目标结构**
- 中文：我知道你最终接受了哪个方案。
- 学生：`I know which plan you finally accepted.`
- 评价：❌ 这是简洁书面版，没有插入 `it is`。应为 `I know which plan **it is** you finally accepted.`
- 学生修复：`I know which plan it is you finally accepted.`
- 评价：✅ **修复成功**。

#### Round 2 (否定陈述句)
- 中文：我不确定你到底指的是哪个版本。
- 学生：`I'm not sure which version it is you're referring to.`
- 评价：✅ 正确。`which version it is you're referring to` — `it is` 插入结构用对了。

#### Round 3 (特殊疑问句) ❌ **语序错误**
- 中文：你到底什么时候方便开会？
- 学生：`When it is that you are available for a meeting.`
- 评价：❌ 这是**陈述语序**，不能用于直接疑问句。直接疑问句需倒装 → `When **is it** (that) you're available for a meeting?`
- **重出 1 轮**：你为什么突然要辞职？
  - 学生：`Why is it you want to quit?`
  - 评价：✅ **修复成功**。`Why is it` 倒装语序正确。

#### Round 4 (反义疑问句) ❌ **语序完全不对（3 轮纠正）**
- 中文：你知道会议上到底讨论了什么，不是吗？
- 学生 `You know it is what was discussed in the meeting, don't you?`
  - ❌ `it is` 在 wh- 词前 → 应为 `what it is`
- 学生 `You know what it is that it was discussed in the meeting, don't you?`
  - ⚠️ `that` 后多了一个多余的 `it`
- 学生修复：`You know what it is that was discussed in the meeting, don't you?`
  - ✅ **修复成功**。`what it is that was discussed` 结构完整，反义疑问 `don't you?` 极性正确。

#### Round 5 (选择疑问句)
- 中文：你希望我确认的是时间还是地点？
- 学生：`Which is it you want me to confirm — time or place?`
- 评价：✅ **满分**。`Which is it you want me to confirm` — wh- 疑问句 `is it` 倒装语序正确，`time or place` 选择结构工整。

#### Quick Fire
- 场景：New colleague asks about company culture. You say it depends on which department he's really talking about.
- 学生：`I think it depends on which department it is that you're talking about.`
- 评价：✅ **满分**。`which department it is that` — `it is` 插入自然，无卡顿。

**LP 状态**：🟡 复用中。核心用法已理解 ✅。`it is` 在 wh- 从句中的语序位置（wh- 后、主语前）经历了多轮纠正后才掌握，稳定性仍需巩固。特殊疑问句倒装语序（`When is it` 非 `When it is`）是主要弱点。

### LP 4: `would` 假设/委婉语气 — 新学 🆕

**来源**：19BEC — `Where **would** I start?`
**含义**：在假设/推测场景中用 `would` 代替 `will/do`，使语气更柔和、委婉，体现"这不是真实发生的，只是设想一下"。
**训练句式**：5/5 + QF（全部满分）

#### Round 1 (肯定陈述句) — 植入 `get sb doing`（🔴 易忘）
- 中文：你的解释会让团队成员开始理解这个新流程。
- 学生：`Your explanation would get team members starting to understand this new workflow.`
- 评价：✅ **双重命中**。`would get` — 假设语气 ✅；`get team members starting` — **`get sb doing` 🔴 易忘 → 正确使用，可升级 🟡** 🏆

#### Round 2 (否定陈述句)
- 中文：在这种情况下，我不会建议直接忽略这个问题。
- 学生：`Under this condition, I would not suggest ignoring this issue directly.`
- 评价：✅ `would not suggest` 假设语气正确。`Under this condition` → `In this case`/`In this situation` 更自然。

#### Round 3 (特殊疑问句)
- 中文：如果你负责这个项目，你会从哪里开始？
- 学生：`If you were in charge of this project, where would you start?`
- 评价：✅ **满分**。`where would you start` — `would` 假设语气自然融入 Type 2 虚拟条件句。

#### Round 4 (反义疑问句)
- 中文：如果有更多时间，他们能做得更好，不是吗？
- 学生：`They would work better if they had enough time, wouldn't they?`
- 评价：✅ `would work` 假设语气 ✅，`wouldn't they?` Tag 极性正确 ⚠️。`could do better` 比 `would work better` 更贴近"能力"层面的假设。

#### Round 5 (选择疑问句)
- 中文：如果你来选的话，你会选远程办公还是在办公室办公？
- 学生：`If you were the one to choose, would you choose to work from home or work in the office?`
- 评价：✅ **满分**。`would you choose...or...` — `would` 假设语气 + 选择疑问句结构工整。

#### Quick Fire
- 场景：Mentoring a junior dev who asks what you'd suggest if he were to redesign the DB schema.
- 学生：`I would say you should consider as much needs from users as possible.`
- 评价：✅ `I would say` — `would` 软化语气自然。❌ `as much needs` → `as **many** needs`（`many/much` 复发，needs 可数）。

**LP 状态**：🟢 已固化。5+1 轮全部正确 ✅，`would` 假设语气语感自然。`many/much` 老缺口触发 1 次（非 `would` 本身问题）。历史词组 `get sb doing` 成功植入并正确使用 🏆。

## 3. 话题构建训练 — 需求 Push Back

**场景**：PM 周五下午提新需求，要求下周二上线。你需要在 1:1 中做出 push back。

### Step 1 (中文 Brainstorm) ✅
- 学生 3 个观点：需求本身合理 + 需要半天评估工作量 + 半天评审 + 周二上午出工时 → 定上线时间
- 导师反馈：逻辑清晰，先肯定后推时间是正确策略。建议补充"需求中有模糊点需要先对齐"的视角。

### Step 2 (骨架供给)
- 3 个可复用骨架：`makes sense` / `estimate the dev and QA effort` / `nail down`
- Softener: `To be fair,` / `Realistically,` / `The thing is,`

### Step 3 (有脚手架输出)
- 学生：`This requirement is wonderful. This requirement itself makes sense. However, since it came in late Friday, we need at least half a day to estimate the dev and QA effort. So the earliest we can get back to you with the estimates is, I think, Tuesday morning. Then I think we can nail down the launch date based on that.`
- 评价：
  - 骨架使用：`makes sense` ✅ / `estimate the dev and QA effort` ✅ / `nail down` ✅
  - `wonderful` 过于夸张 → `reasonable` 更合适
  - 漏了"需求评审"这个中间环节
  - 冠词：`came in late **on** Friday`

### Step 4 (撤骨架) ✅
- 学生：`Well, this requirement itself makes sense, but since it came in late Friday, we need at least half a day to estimate the QA and dev effort. After that, we still need to make a review session for the requirement, so the earliest we can get back to you with the estimates, I think, is Tuesday morning. Then we can nail down the launch date based on that.`
- 评价：
  - `makes sense` 保留 ✅
  - 评审环节补上了 ✅
  - `nail down` 成功用上 ✅
  - `make a review session` → `have`/`walk through`（搭配不当）
  - Native 优化版：`To be fair...But since it came in late Friday, we need at least half a day to scope out the dev and QA effort. After that, we'd also need another half day to walk through it for a review session. So realistically, the earliest we can get back to you with estimates is Tuesday morning. Then we can nail down the launch date together.`
