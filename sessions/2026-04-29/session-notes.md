## 闪电纠偏记录 (巩固昨日缺口)

### 纠偏点: `have come` (PP), `trouble` (Uncountable), `notify` vs `notice`

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: “我们其实还没想出一个好的解决方案，所以请先不要通知每一个受影响的团队。”
- **学生答案**: `In fact, we have not come up with a good solution. So do not notify every affected team.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 成功识别 `have come` (PP) 和 `notify`。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: “针对那个 Bug，你到底想出了什么办法？它是不是给其他团队带来了不少麻烦？”
- **学生答案**: `Regarding the bug, what approaches exactly have you come up with? did it bring a lot of trouble to the other teams?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `a lot of trouble` (不可数) 掌握非常稳。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: “他刚才注意到了那个错误，但他没法立刻想出什么好主意，不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `He noticed the mistake. However, he cannot came up with a good idea, didn't he？`
- **评价**: 🔴 **双重回退**。`cannot came` -> `cannot come` (情态后接原形)；`didn't he` -> `can he` (Tag 与主句助动词及极性需匹配)。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: “你是打算现在通知每个人那项关于上线方案的变动，还是想等我们想出更好的主意？”
- **学生答案**: `Do you want to notify everyone about the change to the rollout plan or wait until we come up with a better idea？`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 5 — 混合虚拟语气
- **中文**: “要是当初他注意到了那个错误，咱们现在也就不用想出这么多补救措施了，那会省去不少麻烦。”
- **学生答案**: `If he had noticed the mistake, we would not have to come up with so many measures to fix that. It would save a lot of trouble.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 逻辑严密，`trouble` 极稳。

## 造句训练记录 (65BEC)

### 语言点 1: Interrupt (礼貌打断)

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: “在主席（Chair）还没说完之前，我是不会随便插话（interrupt）的。”
- **学生答案**: `I will not interrupt before the chair finishes speaking.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: “在这种正式场合，你觉得到底该如何礼貌地插话（interrupt）呢？”
- **学生答案**: `In formal situations, what exactly do you think is the polite way to interrupt?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: “我们刚才不应该在那个敏感的环节打断（interrupt）他，不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `We should not interrupt him in the allergic stage, shouldn't we?`
- **评价**: 🔴 **错误**。`allergic` (过敏的) -> `sensitive` (敏感的)；Tag 极性回退 `should we?`

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: “你是想现在插话补充一个细节，还是想等他汇报完？”
- **学生答案**: `Do you want to interrupt to add a detail or wait until he finishes?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: “如果昨天你在那个环节没有插话，结果应该会大不相同。”
- **学生答案**: `If you had not interrupted in that stage yesterday, the result would have been different.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

### 语言点 2: Both of which (且两者都...)

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: “我们目前有两个上线方案，但这两者都不包含 UI 的改动。”
- **学生答案**: `Currently, we have two rollout plans, both of which do not entail UI changes, however.`
- **评价**: ✅ `rollout plan` 和 `entail` 准确。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: “既然这两份提交文件（submissions）都和博物馆有关联（both of which ... linked to），你觉得咱们到底该优先处理哪一个？”
- **学生答案**: `Since these two submissions, both of which are linked to the museum, which one exactly do you think we should first deal with?`
- **评价**: ⚠️ **结构断裂**。`Since` 引导的从句缺少谓语动词。纠正为 `Since we have ...`.

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: “那儿有两份提交材料（submissions），且这两者都和你有关（both of which），不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `There are two submissions, both of which are linked to you, aren't they?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `both of which` 嵌套成功。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: “你是打算自己处理这两份提交文件——且这两者其实都挺复杂的——还是想指派给别人？”
- **学生答案**: `Do you want to handle these two submissions, both of which are quite a bit complicated, or do you want to delegate them to others？`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: “如果当时有那么两份提交文件——且这两者都和我有关联——那我当时就该报备（declare）利益冲突了。”
- **学生答案**: `If there had been two submissions, both of which were linked to me, then I should have declared a conflict of interest.`
- **评价**: ✅ `should have declared` 命中核心。

## 场景化模拟实战 (Scenario Role-play)

### 场景: 专家委员会报备利益冲突
- **学生原文**:
  > May I interrupt for a moment? I realize that I have two submissions, both of which are linked to my previous company. So I'd like to say that if it's appropriate for me to declare a conflict of interest right now. Because I want to make sure that it's okay before we enter the deliberations. Both of the submissions are on my desk right now, if you'd like to look through them, I will go fetch them.

- **评价与不足**:
  - ✅ `May I interrupt`, `both of which are linked to`, `declare a conflict of interest`, `enter the deliberations` 四大核心点完美串联！
  - ✅ 复用了 `appropriate for me` 和 `look through` 等旧词，语感极佳。
  - 🔴 **地道性**: `I'd like to declare ..., if I may` 是更高端的收尾方式。
  - 🔴 **细节**: `on my desks` (复发) -> `on my desk`。
