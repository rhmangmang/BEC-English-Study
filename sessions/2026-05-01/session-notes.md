## 造句训练记录 (67BEC)

### 语言点 1: `noise`（讨论、反馈、动静 — 不可数名词）

课文原文：`I saw a little bit of noise around it, which was good.` — 我看到大家有一些讨论和反馈，这挺好的。

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "那个方案发到群里之后其实并没有引起什么讨论。"
- **学生答案**: `After the proposal had been sent, it didn't create any noise.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`noise` 不可数** — 与 `any` 搭配正确，核心用法命中 🏆
  - ✅ **`it didn't create`** — 时态正确
  - ⚠️ **漏译 "群里"**: 地点（Slack/公司群）未译出，加上更完整：`After the proposal was posted in Slack, it didn't create any noise.`

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: "对于那个新功能，客户那边到底产生了什么样的反馈？"
- **学生答案**: `What feedback did the client give on the new feature?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ 语法正确
  - ❌ **未使用核心语言点 `noise`** — 句子本身没问题，`feedback` 也很自然，但训练目标是让你用 `noise` 来表达"反馈/讨论"。
- **预期答案**: `What kind of noise exactly did the client generate around the new feature?`

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: "那个公告在团队里引起了不少讨论，不是吗？"
- **学生答案**: `The notice generated quite a bit noise in the team, didn't it?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`noise` 使用正确** — 不可数，无冠词
  - ✅ **Tag 极性** — 主句肯定 → `didn't it?` 正确 🏆
  - ❌ **`quite a bit noise`** — 固定搭配漏了 `of`：应为 `quite a bit **of** noise`
- **修正**: `The notice generated quite a bit of noise in the team, didn't it?`

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: "你觉得这个活动在客户那边是引起了好的反响，还是没什么动静？"
- **学生答案**: `Do you think this activity caused positive noise on the client side or it didn't cause any noise?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`positive noise`** — 表达"反响"使用正确
  - ❌ **平行结构不工整** — `caused positive noise` (动+宾) vs `it didn't cause any noise` (主+动+宾)。`or` 两侧结构应一致。
- **修正**: `Did this activity cause positive noise on the client side or **no noise at all**?`

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: "如果当时那个活动在客户那边引起了一些讨论，我们就能早点知道他们的真实想法了。"
- **学生答案**: `If that activity had caused some noise on the client side, we would have already known their true idea.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **Type 3 虚拟语气** — `had caused... would have known` 完美 🏆
  - ✅ **`some noise`** — 不可数用法正确
  - ❌ **`their true idea`** — `idea` 不贴切。"真实想法/看法"应是 `what they really thought` 或 `their true thoughts / opinions`
- **修正**: `If that activity had caused some noise on the client side, we would have already known what they really thought.`

### 语言点 2: `in a nutshell / the nutshell is…`（简而言之、核心是……）

课文原文：`You know, the nutshell here is that as we've kind of restructured and tried different things.`

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "简而言之，新系统上线后并没有带来预期中的效率提升。"
- **学生答案**: `In a nutshell, it didn't bring any expected improvement in efficiency after it was launched.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`In a nutshell`** — 固定搭配使用正确，冠词 `a` 无误 🏆
  - ⚠️ **`any expected improvement`** — "预期中的"指那个特定的预期值，用 `the` 更精准：`**the** expected improvement`

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: "简而言之，你觉得这个项目延期的主要原因到底是什么？"
- **学生答案**: `In a nutshell, what exactly is the main reason that caused the delay of this project?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`In a nutshell`** — 固定搭配正确 🏆
  - ✅ **`what exactly`** — Exactly 位置精准 🏆
  - ⚠️ **`reason that caused`** — 稍显冗余，更简洁：`In a nutshell, what exactly is the main reason **for the delay** of this project?`

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: "说白了，他那番话的核心意思就是团队需要更多支持，不是吗？"
- **学生答案**: `the nutshell is that what he said is that the team needs support, does it?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`the nutshell is that`** — `the` 特指总结，使用正确 🏆
  - ❌ **Tag 极性/助动词** — 主句主语 `the nutshell`，系动词 `is` → Tag 应为 **`isn't it?`**，不是 `does it?`
  - ❌ **句子结构臃肿** — 双重 `that`：`the nutshell is that what he said is that...` → 简化：`In a nutshell, what he said is that the team needs more support, **isn't it**?`
- **修正**: `In a nutshell, what he said is that the team needs more support, **isn't it**?`

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: "简而言之，你是觉得预算不够，还是人手不够？"
- **学生答案**: `In a nutshell, do you think the budget is not enough or the workforce is not enough?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`In a nutshell`** — 正确 🏆
  - ✅ **选择疑问句结构** — 平行完整
  - ⚠️ **`not enough` 用法** — `not enough` 做表语偏生硬。更自然：`we don't have enough budget or headcount`
- **修正**: `In a nutshell, do you think **we don't have enough** budget or enough workforce?`

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: "简而言之，如果当初我们把需求敲定得更清楚，现在就不会这么被动了。"
- **学生答案**: `In a nutshell, if we had made the requirements more clear, we would not be so nervous.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`In a nutshell`** — 正确 🏆
  - ✅ **虚拟语气** — `If had made` (Type 3 过去条件) + `would not be` (现在结果) 时态匹配完美 🏆
  - ❌ **`nervous` 语义不匹配** — "被动"不是"紧张/焦虑"，而是状态被动：`in a passive position` / `on the back foot`
- **修正**: `In a nutshell, if we had nailed down the requirements more clearly, we would not be in such a passive position now.`

### 语言点 3: `be nailed down`（被敲定、被确定）

课文原文：`the support that we need isn't as nailed down as it needs to be.`

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "项目的上线日期到现在还没有敲定。"
- **学生答案**: `the launch date of the project has not been nailed down as of now.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`be nailed down`** — 核心短语精准 🏆
  - ✅ **`has not been nailed down`** — 被动+完成时正确
  - ⚠️ **`as of now`** — 偏"从此刻起往后"，而"到现在"表达"直到现在还没"。更地道：`has not been nailed down **yet**`
- **修正**: `The launch date of the project has not been nailed down yet.`

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: "哪些具体的交付成果到现在还没敲定？"
- **学生答案**: `Which specific deliverables have not been nailed down yet?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 正确使用了 `yet` 表达"到现在为止"，`be nailed down` 使用精准，特殊疑问句语序工整。**同时理解 `yet` 在否定句中表"到目前为止"的用法。**

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: "这次活动的预算还没敲定，不是吗？"
- **学生答案**: `The budget of this event has not been nailed down yet, has it?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `yet` 句末位置地道，Tag 极性 `has not → has it?` 正确 🏆

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: "你觉得这个方案是已经敲定了，还是还有一些细节没定？"
- **学生答案**: `Do you think this proposal has been nailed down or are there some details that have not been nailed down yet?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ 两处 `be nailed down` 使用正确 🏆
  - ⚠️ **结构冗长** — `or` 两侧用了两个不同句式。更简洁：`Do you think this proposal **has been nailed down** or **still has some details to be finalized**?`

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: "如果当初预算在上个月就敲定了，我们现在就可以启动活动了。"
- **学生答案**: `If the budget had been finalized last month, we could launch the event now.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **Type 3 + Mixed 虚拟语气** — `If had been... could... now` 时态匹配完美 🏆
  - ❌ **未使用核心短语 `nailed down`** — 用了 `finalized`，意思不错但训练目标是课文原词 `nailed down`
  - ⚠️ **`could launch`** — 语气偏"可能"，"就可以……了"用 `would be able to` 更贴切
- **修正**: `If the budget had been **nailed down** last month, we **would be able to** kick off the event now.`

### 语言点 4: `not as + adj/pp + as it needs to be`（还没达到应有的……程度）

课文原句：`the support that we need isn't as nailed down as it needs to be.`

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "新来的那个工程师的技能水平还没达到我们需要的标准。"
- **学生答案**: `The technical skills of the new engineer are not as good as it needs to be.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`not as...as it needs to be`** — 核心句式正确 🏆
  - ❌ **主谓不一致** — `technical skills` (复数) → 代词应为 `they` 而非 `it`：`as **they** need to be`
- **修正**: `The technical skills of the new engineer are not as good as **they** need to be.`

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: "流程的哪个环节还没有达到它应有的标准化程度？"
- **学生答案**: `Which part of the process is not as standardized as it needs to be?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 核心句式 `not as...as it needs to be` 使用精准，`standardized` 完美匹配课文例子。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: "我们的测试覆盖率还没达到应有的水平，不是吗？"
- **学生答案**: `Our test coverage is not as high as it needs to be, is it?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `not as high as it needs to be` 完美，Tag 极性 `is not → is it?` 正确 🏆

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: "你觉得是系统的稳定性没达标，还是性能没达标？"
- **学生答案**: `Do you think the stability of the system or the performance of the system is not good as it needs to be?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ 核心句式感良好
  - ❌ **漏了第一个 `as`** — 结构是 `not **as** + adj + **as** it needs to be`，第一个 `as` 不可省
- **修正**: `Do you think the stability or the performance of the system is **not as good as it needs to be**?`

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: "如果当初文档没有达到应有的完善程度，我们就不应该发布它。"
- **学生答案**: `If the document had not been as completed as it needs to be, we should not have released it`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **Type 3 虚拟语气** — 骨架清晰
  - ❌ **`as completed as`** — `completed` (已被完成) ≠ "完善" (`complete`，形容词)。此处应为 `as **complete** as`
  - ❌ **时态不一致** — 主句过去虚拟，比较从句也应回退：`as it **needed** to be`
  - ✅ **`should not have released`** — "本不该做"的语气精准 🏆
- **修正**: `If the document had not been as **complete** as it **needed** to be, we should not have released it.`

- **学生答案**: `They went with proposal A in the end, didn't they?`

### 语言点 6: `run the campaigns`（执行营销活动）

课文原文：`your campaign manager does the campaigns for that event` → 纠正为 `runs the campaigns`

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "那个团队现在没有足够的资源来执行这些营销活动。"
- **学生答案**: `The team didn't have enough resources to run the campaigns.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`run the campaigns`** — 核心短语正确，`run` 比 `do` 地道 🏆
  - ⚠️ **时态** — 中文"现在没有"暗示当前状态，应用一般现在时 `doesn't have`，而非 `didn't have`（过去）

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: "这次发布活动到底是由哪个团队来执行的？"
- **学生答案**: `Which team will run the launch campaign?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: "你的活动经理会负责执行这些营销活动，不是吗？"
- **学生答案**: `Your campaign manager will run these campaigns, won't he?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** Tag 极性 `will... won't he?` 完美。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: "这次的产品发布活动是由 PMM 来执行，还是由你的活动经理来执行？"
- **学生答案**: `Will this product launch campaign be ran by PMM or your campaign manager?`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`run by`** — 被动语态结构正确
  - ✅ **平行结构** — `by PMM or by your campaign manager` 工整
  - ❌ **`be ran`** — `run` 的过去分词是 **`run`**（run-ran-**run**），不是 `ran`
- **修正**: `Will this product launch campaign be **run** by the PMM or by your campaign manager?`

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句
- **中文**: "如果当初让市场团队来执行这个活动，效果应该会好得多。"
- **学生答案**: `If we had let the marketing team run this campaign at that time, the results would be much better.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`run this campaign`** — 核心短语正确 🏆
  - ✅ **条件句** — `If we had let...` Type 3 正确
  - ⚠️ **结果句时态** — `would be`（现在结果），但"当初"强调过去结果，应用 `would have been much better`（Type 3）
- **修正**: `If we had let the marketing team run this campaign at that time, the results would **have been** much better.`

## 场景化模拟实战 (Scenario Role-play)

### 场景: Go-to-Market 策略对齐会
- **任务**: 作为 PMM 向 CMO 汇报产品发布活动筹备情况。

- **学生原文**:
  > Well, to be honest with you. We have not prepared anything ever since we launched the product campaign and I've also heard a lot of noise around it. Some are good and some are not that good. In a nutshell, we need to improve our product as we know, because it's not as good as it needs to be. And after three weeks, we decided to go with the proposal that you came up with a few days ago. And we decided to run the campaigns tomorrow morning. What do think? I would like to hear your advice.

- **核心语言点集成**:

| 语言点 | 使用情况 |
| :--- | :--- |
| `noise` | ✅ `a lot of noise around it` |
| `In a nutshell` | ✅ `In a nutshell, we need to improve our product` |
| `not as...as it needs to be` | ✅ `it's not as good as it needs to be` |
| `go with` | ✅ `we decided to go with the proposal` |
| `run the campaigns` | ✅ `we decided to run the campaigns tomorrow` |
| `be nailed down` | ❌ 未使用 |

- **不足与纠错**:

| 原始表达 | 问题 | 修正 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `ever since we launched the product campaign` | 逻辑矛盾：`launched` (已发布) 但 `haven't prepared anything` (啥也没准备) | `ever since we started planning the product campaign` |
| `after three weeks` | "三周后"时间归属不清晰 | `after three weeks of discussion` |
| `What do think?` | 漏了 `you` | `What do **you** think?` |

- **表现评价**: 🏆 5/6 个核心语言点全部自然植入，串联丝滑。`Well, to be honest with you` 开场地道，`I would like to hear your advice` 展现对话感。全场即兴表达流利度高。
