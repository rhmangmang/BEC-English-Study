## 📚 造句训练详情

### 语言点 1: `conference vs meeting` (49BEC)

**来源**: `welcome to today's video conference` — 学生问 conference 和 meeting 区别
**核心**: meeting = 日常小会；conference = 正式大会
**状态**: 🟡 复用中

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 下周有一个国际会议，我得准备一份演示文稿。 | `There will be an international conference next week. I have to prepare the presentation.` | ⚠️ | `the presentation`→`a presentation`；`have to`→`need to` |
| R2 | 选择疑问 | 你觉得这算是日常会议还是正式大会？ | `Do you think it is a daily meeting or a formal conference?` | 🏆 | 间接疑问句语序零回退；区分精准 |
| R3 | 否定陈述 | 这不是一个普通的团队周会，这是一个行业峰会，有500多人参加。 | `This is not a normal team weekly meeting; it's a industry conference. there will be more than 500 people taking part in.` | 🔴 | `a industry`→`an industry`(冠词)；`team weekly`→`weekly team`(形容词顺序)；`taking part in`→`attending/taking part`(句末in多余) |
| R3+ | 加练否定 | 这不是面试，只是一次非正式的同步聊天。 | `This is not an interview; it's just a casual sync up chat.` | 🏆 | `an interview`✅ 冠词修复；`sync up chat`→`sync-up`(连字符) |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 这种行业峰会每年都办，对吧？ | `This kind of industry conference is held every year, isn't it?` | 🏆 | 极性/时态/代词全部正确 |
| R5 | 特殊疑问 | 你们公司每年参加多少场行业峰会？ | `How many industry conferences will your company take part in every year?` | 🔴 | `will`→`does`（每年=习惯→一般现在时） |
| R5+ | 加练特殊 | 为什么这次视频会议改成了面对面会议？ | `Why did you change this video meeting to a face-to -face one?` | 🏆 | `did...change` 过去时修复；`face-to -face`→`face-to-face` |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | — | 跳过（句式不适用说明后用户同意） | — | — |
| QF | 场景回应 | 两个会议邀请：明早stand-up vs 下月伦敦大会 | `I will attend the team's stand up tomorrow. however, I'm not sure I'll take part in the industry conference in England.` | ⚠️ | 区分意识 ✅；`team's stand up`→`team stand-up`；`tomorrow. however` 小写；`take part in`→`attend` 更简洁 |

**覆盖句式**: 5/6（虚拟语气跳过，已向用户说明原因）

**植入历史词组**: `walk through`(R2复用时用了), `take part in`(R5), `not really like`(未直接植入)

### 语言点 2: `do a check-in` (49BEC)

**来源**: `Let's do a quick check-in` — 会议开场确认/同步
**核心**: 会议场景=设备确认；工作场景=同步状态；`check in with sb on sth`
**状态**: 🟡 复用中

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 在项目开始之前，我们先快速同步一下各自的进度。 | `Before the project starts, let's do a quick check - in.` | ⚠️ | `check - in`→`check-in`（连字符无空格） |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 我们还没来得及做设备检查，先别开始正式内容。 | `Do not start the formal contact before we finish the check in with devices.` | ⚠️ | `contact`→`content`；`check in with devices`❌→`device check-in`（`check in with sb`不能接device） |
| R3 | 特殊疑问 | 我们通常用什么工具来做会议签到和状态确认？ | `What tools do you usually use to do the meeting check-in and the status check-in?` | ✅ | `status check-in` 稍冗余 |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 你还没跟他同步过项目进展，对吧？ | `You have not synced the project updates, have you?` | 🔴 | **目标结构未使用**！`synced`→应植入`checked in with him on` |
| R4+ | 加练反义 | 你还没跟他同步过项目的交付时间，是吧？ | `You have not checked in with him on the project delivery time, have you?` | 🏆 | `checked in with sb on sth` 三要素完整命中 |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 你们是会前做签到还是会后做总结？ | `Do you do the check-in before the meeting or do the summary after the meeting?` | ✅ | `summary`→`recap`，整体平行结构正确 |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | 要是我们会议开始前做了签到检查，就不会浪费10分钟了。 | `If we had done a check-in before the meeting started, we would not have wasted 10 minutes.` | 🏆 | Type 3 公式精准 |
| QF | 场景回应 | 新项目启动，周四15分钟碰一下确认进度 | `Let's spend 15 minutes on Thursday to do a quick  check-in and make sure everyone is all set.` | 🏆 | 满分；`all set` 非常地道；`quick  check-in`多了一个空格 |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖

**植入历史词组**: `walk through`(概念讲解时提到), `take notes on`(未植入), `not really like`(未植入)

### 语言点 4: `apologies for` (50BEC)

**来源**: `Apologies for the delay.` — 固定口语道歉短句
**核心**: 复数 `Apologies` = 固定习语；单数不能裸奔，须 `My apology` 或 `I apologize`
**状态**: 🟡 复用中

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 抱歉回复晚了，我昨天在外面开会。 | `Apologies for late reply.I was in a meeting yesterday.` | ⚠️ | 缺冠词 `the late reply` |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 不用为这个技术小问题道歉，它不影响整体进度。 | `Don't apologize for this technical little glitch. It doesn't affect the whole progress.` | 🔴 | **用了动词而非目标名词短语**；形容词顺序；`whole progress`→`overall progress` |
| R2+ | 加练否定 | 会议迟到了几分钟，不用太正式道歉，直接坐下就好。 | `Don't apologize for being late for the meeting. Just sit down, just take a seat.` | 🔴 | 再次用动词形式；两个`just`重复 |
| R2++ | 二次加练 | 迟到几分钟而已，不用道歉，赶紧坐下吧。 | `No need for apologies for being late. just sit down.` | 🏆 | **名词短句命中**！`just`小写→大写 |
| R3 | 特殊疑问 | 你为什么要为设备故障道歉？这不是你的错。 | `Why did you apologize for the device glitch? It's not your fault.` | ✅ | 问句用动词形式自然，接受 |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 你为这事已经道歉过了，对吧？ | `You have apologized for this, haven't you?` | 🏆 | 满分 |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 你是想口头道歉还是发一封道歉邮件？ | `Do you want to apologize orally or sent an email for your apologies?` | 🔴 | `sent`→`send` **平行结构错误**；`orally`过于正式→`in person` |
| R5+ | 加练选择 | 你是这次当面道歉还是回去之后发一封正式的道歉信？ | `Will you apologize in person, or send a formal apology letter when you get back?` | 🏆 | 平行结构 `apologize`/`send` 修复；整体母语者水准 |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | 要是你当时道了歉，事情不会闹这么大。 | `If you had apologized at that time, the thing would not have been so serious.` | 🏆 | Type 3 精准；`the thing`→`things`/`it` |
| QF | 场景回应 | 迟到5分钟进Zoom，随口道歉不搞太正式 | `Apologies for my late.` | ⚠️ | `Apologies for`✅ 短语命中；`my late`❌→`being late`（late是形容词不能作名词） |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖

**概念辨析穿插**: `apology`单数为什么不能裸奔（可数名词需要限定词）+ `late`形容词vs名词

### 语言点 5: `technical glitch` (50BEC)

**来源**: `I had a quick technical glitch with my microphone.`
**核心**: glitch = 小技术故障/小毛病（很快修好、不严重），区别于 problem/issue
**状态**: 🟡 复用中

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 我的麦克风出了个小问题，稍等几秒钟。 | `There's a little glitch with my microphone. Please wait a second.` | 🏆 | 满分；上轮用`hitch`，本轮精准换`glitch` |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 这个系统从上线以来没出过任何小故障。 | `There never happened any glitches ever since the system was launched.` | 🔴 | `There never happened` 结构不自然；`ever` + `never` 冲突 |
| R2+ | 加练否定 | 网络一直到现在都没出过小毛病，非常稳定。 | `There's no glitch with the network until now. it's very stable.` | 🔴 | 时态冲突：`There's`(一般现在) + `until now`(要求完成时)；`it's`→`It's` |
| R2++ | 二次加练 | 系统这几个月一直没问题，很稳定。 | `The system has been working properly for these months. It's very stable.` | 🔴 | **绕开目标词** `glitch`，用了正向表达 `working properly` |
| R2+++ | 三次加练 | 系统这几个月没出过任何小故障，很稳定。 | `There haven't been any glitches with the system these past few months.It is very stable.` | 🏆 | **三次修复达成**！完成体+否定+glitch 三合一 |
| R3 | 特殊疑问 | 你上次遇到网络小故障是什么时候？ | `When did you run into the network glitch last time?` | ⚠️ | `the network glitch`→`a network glitch`(泛指)；`last time`句末突兀；`run into`历史复用 🌟 |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 你之前遇到过这种系统小毛病，对吧？ | `You have run into this kind of system glitch, haven't you?` | 🏆 | 满分；`this kind of` + `run into` 双历史复用 |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 这是一个系统小故障还是网络问题？ | `Is it a system glitch or a network issue?` | 🏆 | 满分 |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | 要是当时系统出了小故障，我们就不会按时完成了。 | `If there had been a glitch with the system, we would not have been asked to finish on time.` | ⚠️ | Type 3 ✅；`be asked to finish`→`have finished`（多加"被要求"，语义偏差） |
| QF | 场景回应 | 演示时屏幕共享卡住，解释不是大问题 | `Well, this is not a issue; it's just a technical glitch. let me do a quick check-in.` | ⚠️ | `a issue`→`an issue` 冠词；`do a quick check-in` 跨session复用电击 🌟 |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖

**概念辨析穿插**: `glitch` vs `hitch` vs `problem` vs `issue` + `last time`句末位置解释（定语从句成分分析） + `in advance` vs `ahead of time`

**中间穿插 student concept questions**: `hitch vs glitch 区别`、`为什么last time放句末突兀`、`you ran into a network glitch 在定语从句中做什么成分`、`the last time在从句中做什么成分(时间状语)`、`in advance vs ahead of time`

### 语言点 6: `convene` (51BEC)

**来源**: `Behavior Analysis Regulatory Board Meeting is convening at 11:35am`
**核心**: convene = 正式召开/召集；人作主语(主动) / 会议作主语(正式)
**状态**: 🟡 复用中

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 委员会下周一正式召开，所有成员必须到场。 | `The commission meeting is convening next Monday. All members must be present.` | ⚠️ | `commission`→`committee`（内部常设机构≠政府任命的调查委员会） |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 董事会今天不召开，改到下周三了。 | `The board meeting won't convene today; it has been changed to next Wednesday.` | ⚠️ | `changed`→`moved/pushed to` 更自然 |
| R3 | 特殊疑问 | 委员会具体什么时候正式召开？ | `When will the committee meeting convene?` | 🏆 | 满分 |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 董事会在今天上午召开，对吧？ | `The board meeting will be convening in the morning today, won't it?` | ⚠️ | `will be convening`偏重→`is convening this morning`；`in the morning today`冗余→`this morning` |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 委员会是这周开还是下周开？ | `Is the committee meeting convening this week or next week?` | 🏆 | 满分 |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | 要是委员会昨天就召开了，我们就能提前拿到决议了。 | `If the committee meeting had been convened yesterday,we would have gotten the decision in advance.` | 🏆 | Type 3 被动完美；`gotten`→`got`(国际通用)；`in advance` ✅ |
| QF | 场景回应 | 通知团队会议日期已定 | `The date for convening the meeting has been finalized; please notify everyone.` | 🏆 | `convening` 作动名词，满分 |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖

**概念辨析穿插**: `hold vs held vs host` / `hold a meeting vs host a meeting`（组织者≠主办方≠主持人）/ `host vs chair`（活动主持人≠会议主席）/ `in advance vs ahead of time` / `activity vs event`（日历上的事 vs 具体行为）/ `live-streamed vs streamed live`

### 语言点 7: `be being + pp` 被动进行态 (51BEC)

**来源**: `This meeting is being held in person and through Teams`
**核心**: `be being + pp` = 现在进行时被动 = "此刻正在被…"；区别 `is held`(常态) vs `is being held`(此刻)
**状态**: 🟡 复用中（吻合 knowledge-gaps "被动进行态意识弱"缺口）

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 会议此刻正以线上和线下两种方式同步进行。 | `The meeting is going both in online and offline way at the same time.` | 🔴 | **目标结构未使用**！`is going`→`is being held` |
| R1重 | 重做肯定 | 发布会此刻正在三楼宴会厅举行。 | `The media meeting is being held on the third floor right now.` | ⚠️ | 结构命中 ✅；`media meeting`→`press conference` |
| R1再 | 再重做 | (同上) | `The press conference is being held in the banquet hall on the third floor right now.` | 🏆 | 满分 |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 这次会议没有被录制，不用担心说错话。 | `The meeting is not being recorded, so don't worry that you will say something wrong.` | ⚠️ | 结构正确 ✅；`don't worry that`→`don't worry about saying` |
| R3 | 特殊疑问 | 会议正在用哪个平台录制？ | `Which platform is the meeting being recorded on?` | 🏆 | 满分，悬空介词 `on` 处理得当 |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 这个会议正在被直播，对吧？ | `This meeting is being live, isn't it?` | 🔴 | `live`不是过去分词！`being`后必须接pp→`is being live-streamed` |
| R4+ | 加练反义 | 这个活动正在被全程录制，对吧？ | `This activity is being recorded all time, isn't it?` | 🏆 | 结构修复 ✅；`all time`→`the whole time`；`activity`→`event` |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 会议正在被录制还是直播？ | `Is this meeting being recorded or live streamed?` | ✅ | 结构正确；`live streamed`→`live-streamed`(连字符) |
| R6 | 虚拟Mixed | 要是会议当时被全程录下来了，我们现在就能回去查那段讨论了。 | `If the meeting had been recorded the whole time, we would be able to look up the discussion in it.` | 🏆 | Mixed Conditional 精准；Type 3 被动进行态在实际中不自然→接受 `had been recorded`；`look up`→`go back to/review` |
| QF | 场景回应 | 老板问"会开着摄像头吗？有在录吗？" | `Well, this meeting is actually being recorded, and it's camera open.` | ⚠️ | `is being recorded`✅；`it's camera open`❌→`the camera is on`（中式直译） |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖

**概念辨析穿插**: `hold/held/host` 三词区分 + `host vs chair` + `activity vs event`语感 + `live-streamed vs streamed live`
