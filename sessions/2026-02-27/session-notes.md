### 语言点 1: get passed to (被转交/移交给)
- **来源**: 30BEC-23s 原文 "the tickets then get passed to the IT team"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "This issue didn't get passed to the technical team yesterday" — 时态与被动结合完美 🏆
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "To which team do the tickets in the system usually get passed?" — **介词前置高级结构**应用自如 🌟
  3. ✅ 反义疑问句: "This complaint letter from a client will get passed to the legal department, won't it?" — 极性切换精准 🏆
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "Did these new requirements get passed to the development team or the design team?" — 平行结构严谨 ⚖️
  5. ⚠️ 虚拟语气 (Type 3): "If the ticket had not got passed to the IT team, the customer would have already complained" — 🔴 纠正了 `would have been complained` (不及物动词多余被动)；补齐了漏译的“昨天/早就” 🏆

### 语言点 2: move on to (推进到下个阶段/环节)
- **来源**: 30BEC-23s 原文 "they test it and they move on"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "Because of the serious bug, we cannot move on to the release phase" — 职场场景词汇准确 🏆
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "After testing this feature, which phase will we move on to?" — 动名词用法自然 ✅
  3. ✅ 反义疑问句: "The team will move on to the next iteration tomorrow, won't they?" — 🔴 **突破性掌握**：环节名词（next iteration）前必须加 `the` 🏆；集合名词 `team` 对应 `they` 亮点 🌟
  4. ⚠️ 选择疑问句: "Have they moved on to the code review phase, or are they still fixing those bugs?" — 🔴 **时态校准**：精准区分“已经推进 (完成时)”与“还在修改 (进行时)” ⚖️
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Type 3): "If it hadn't been for the customer's complaints, they would not have moved on to the development of the next version last week" — **`If it hadn't been for` 高级句式**首秀成功 🏆🏆🏆

### 语言点 3: in the background (在后台/幕后)
- **来源**: 30BEC-23s 原文 "in the background"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "Our system doesn't secretly send all the data to the background" — 🔴 修正了 `to the background` 为状语 `in the background` 更地道 🏆
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "Why are these tasks always secretly running in the background?" — 时态语序满分 🌟
  3. ✅ 反义疑问句: "The update program usually runs silently in the background, doesn't it?" — 助动词 `doesn't` 匹配精准 🏆
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "Is he going to do this secretly in the background or openly?" — 🔴 纠正口语 `gonna` 为职场 `going to`；`in public` 优化为 `openly` ⚖️
  5. ⚠️ 虚拟语气 (Type 3): "If the program had run in the background, our system would not have crashed at that time" — 🔴 **动词拼写纠偏**：`had ran` → `had run` (run-ran-run) 🏆

## 重点错题记录

| 涉及知识点 | 原始错误句子 | 纠正后的正确表达 | 错误类型 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **不及物动词** | would have **been complained** | would have **complained** | `complain` 是主动动作，不能加 `been` |
| **虚词/程度词** | (漏译“昨天/去年”) | ...yesterday / ...already | 翻译扫描漏掉修饰成分 |
| **不规则动词** | had **ran** | had **run** | `run-ran-run` 过去分词拼写错误 |
| **时态敏感度** | **Do** they move on... or **still fix** | **Have** they moved on... or **are they still fixing** | 忽略了“已经(完成)”与“还在(进行)”的时态暗示 |
| **冠词规律** | ...to **next** iteration | ...to **the** next iteration | 环节/步骤名词前必须加 `the` |

## 涵盖的关键概念
- **`next` 冠词双轨制**: 实体环节加 `the`，时间标签（以当前为准）不加 `the`。
- **`in the background`**: 作为动作状态状语，而非简单的目的地。
- **Type 3 虚拟语气**: 针对已发生的过去事实进行完全反向假设。
- **不及物动词的主动性**: 职场动作如 `complain` 需避免过度被动化。
