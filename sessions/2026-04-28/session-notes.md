## 闪电纠偏记录 (综合巩固)

### 纠偏点: `related TO`, `take IT over`, `put effort INTO`

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: “由于目前这个 Bug 并不直接和底层架构相关，所以我们其实不用在这个问题上投入太多精力。”
- **学生答案**: `Because this bug isn't related to the underlying architecture, we don't need to put a lot of effort into it.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 成功识别 `related to` 和 `put effort into it`。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: “到底为什么要指派（assign）他来接管这个项目？这难道和他上个月的表现有关系吗？”
- **学生答案**: `Why exactly was he assigned to take the project over, is it related to his performance last month?`
- **评价**: ✅ `exactly` 位置完美；`take the project over` 名词在中间正确。
- **建议**: 当指代项目时，若用代词 `it`，必须说 `take it over`。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: “既然你明年要离开，你以后肯定会把这些任务移交给我的，不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `Since you're gonna leave next year, you'll definitely pass these tasks to me, won't you?`
- **评价**: ✅ Tag `won't you` 正确。
- **纠偏**: 建议使用更具“交接感”的 **`hand them over`**，强化代词插中间的肌肉记忆。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: “你觉得那个问题是直接和系统性能（system performance）相关的，还是说我们根本没必要在上面投入任何精力？”
- **学生答案**: `Do you think this issue is related to the system's performance or we don't need to put any effort into it?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `related to` 与 `put effort into it` 连续正确。

#### Round 5 — 混合虚拟语气
- **中文**: “要是当初他能早点把那个项目移交给我的话，我们现在也就不会碰到这么多与合规性（compliance）相关的麻烦了。”
- **学生答案**: `If he had handed the project over to me earlier, we would not encounter so many issues related to compliance.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **完美通关！** 混合虚拟骨架稳健；`hand...over` 与 `related to` 配合丝滑。

## 造句训练记录 (64BEC)

### 语言点 1: sooner rather than later (宜早不宜迟 / 尽快)

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: “如果我们不尽快引入这些改变的话，眼下的风险是没法消除的。”
- **学生答案**: `If we don't introduce these changes sooner than later, we cannot eliminate the current risks.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **完美实用！** `sooner than later` 放置句尾非常自然。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: “为了避免用户混淆，你觉得我们到底为什么要尽快推行这套新的上线方案（rollout plan）呢？”
- **学生答案**: `In order not to introduce more confusion to users, why exactly do you think we should release the rollout plan sooner than later?`
- **评价**: ✅ `exactly` 位置极其稳固。
- **纠偏**: 建议 `rollout plan` 搭配 `implement`，`release` 稍显业余。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: “既然现在所有东西都统称为任务（tasks），我们肯定需要尽快把这个新术语（term）介绍给团队，不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `Now that all these things are called tasks, we should introduce this new term to the team sooner than later, shouldn't we?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 逻辑严密。
- **提醒**: ⚠️ `things` 建议升级为 `items`。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: “你觉得咱们是真的需要尽快解决文档（docs）里的混淆问题，还是说这其实没那么紧急？”
- **学生答案**: `Do you think we really need to solve the confusion problems in the docs sooner than later or it is not so urgent?`
- **评价**: ✅ 逻辑清晰。建议用 `address the confusion` 替代 `solve problems`。

#### Round 5 — 混合虚拟语气
- **中文**: “要是当初我们能尽快把这个新功能集成（integrate）进去的话，咱们现在就不用指望（rely on）用户自己去翻文档了。”
- **学生答案**: `If we could have integrated this new feature sooner than later at that time, we would not need to rely on users to look up in the doc.`
- **评价**: ✅ `rely on sb to do` 结构调用成功。
- **不足**: `in the doc` 应为复数 `in the docs`。

### 语言点 2: entail (包含 / 涉及)

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: “其实这个新的上线方案（rollout plan）并不包含太多的技术改动。”
- **学生答案**: `In fact, this new rollout plan doesn't entail too many technical changes.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 准确区分 `entail` 与 `include`。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: “如果我们现在决定要改这个术语（term），那到底会涉及到哪些具体的内容呢？”
- **学生答案**: `If we decide to change the term now, what exactly will be entailed?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **高阶表达！** 熟练运用 `be entailed` 被动语态。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: “这个岗位（role）并不包含很多出差（business trips），不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `This role doesn't entail too many business trips, does it?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 动词否定与 Tag 匹配极快。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: “你认为这个方案是必然会包含（entail）对 UI 的改动，还是说它仅仅涉及到文档（docs）更新？”
- **学生答案**: `Do you think this plan will definitely entail the changes to the UI or it is just related to the updates of the docs?`
- **评价**: ✅ `related to` 再次复用成功。
- **建议**: `UI changes` 和 `doc updates` 更简洁。

#### Round 5 — 混合虚拟语气
- **中文**: “如果当初那个新岗位（role）不包含这么多出差（business trips）的话，他现在肯定还在咱们团队待着呢。”
- **学生答案**: `If the role had not entailed so many business trips, he would definitely still be in our team.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **完美复刻！** 时态逻辑与近义词辨析双满分。

### 语言点 3: rely on somebody to do something (指望 / 依赖某人去做某事)

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: “我们不能指望（rely on）每个用户都去仔细阅读这套复杂的文档（docs）。”
- **学生答案**: `We cannot rely on every user to carefully read these complicated docs.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 准确使用 `to do` 结构。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: “既然文档（docs）里写得不清不楚，你觉得用户到底为什么要指望（rely on）咱们去帮他们解决每一个小故障（glitch）呢？”
- **学生答案**: `Since the docs do not express clearly, why do you think users rely on us to solve every glitch?`
- **评价**: 🔴 **漏译“到底”** (exactly)；**中式描述**：docs 不用 `express clearly`。
- **修正**: `Since the docs are unclear, why exactly ...?`

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: “为了确保上线顺利，咱们不能只指望（rely on）研发团队（R&D team）去发现所有潜在（potential）的 Bug，不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `In order to make sure everything's okay with the launch, we cannot rely on the R & D team to find all the potential bugs, can we?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** Tag Question 无瑕疵。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: “你是觉得咱们应该指望（rely on）用户主动去报告这些小故障（glitches），还是说咱们应该主动去处理它们？”
- **学生答案**: `Do you think we should rely on users voluntarily report these glitches...`
- **评价**: 🔴 **结构性漏洞**：漏掉了关键的 **`to`**。
- **修正**: `rely on users **to** voluntarily report...`

#### Round 5 — 混合虚拟语气
- **中文**: “如果当初我们能指望（rely on）研发团队（R&D team）按时解决那个 Bug 的话，我们现在也就没必要投入这么多精力去准备这套复杂的上线方案（rollout plan）了。”
- **学生答案**: `If we could have relied on the R&D team to solve the bug on time, we would not have to put a lot of effort into preparing this complicated rollout plan.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **神级表现！** 四个核心点完美融合，`into preparing` 动名词用法正确。

### 语言点 4: rollout plan (上线推广方案 / 分步落地计划)

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: “其实我们目前并没有一套非常成熟（mature）的上线推广方案（rollout plan）。”
- **学生答案**: `Actually, we don't have a mature rollout plan now.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **简洁准确！**

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: “到底是谁负责（be in charge of）制定（develop）这部分新功能的上线推广方案（rollout plan）呢？”
- **学生答案**: `Who exactly is in charge of developing the rollout plan of this new feature?`
- **评价**: ✅ `exactly` 位置稳固。
- **建议**: `for this feature` 比 `of` 更具目的感（今日深度解析内容）。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **中文**: “为了避免混淆（confusing users），他们明天肯定会开始执行（execute）那个针对新术语的上线推广方案（rollout plan），不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `In order to avoid confusing users, they would definitely execute the rollout plan for the new term, wouldn't they?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `rollout plan for` 活学活用。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **中文**: “你觉得咱们是真的需要一套正式的上线推广方案（rollout plan），还是说只需要发一封简单的邮件告诉大家这件事？”
- **学生答案**: `Do you think we really need a formal rollout plan or we just send a simple email...`
- **评价**: 🔴 **通知动词误区**：`notice everyone` -> **`notify/inform everyone`**。
- **注意**: `notice` 是“注意到”，不是“通知”。

#### Round 5 — 混合虚拟语气
- **中文**: “如果当初我们能有一套更好的针对新功能的上线推广方案（rollout plan）的话，咱们现在也就不用指望（rely on）每个同事去单独（individually）找我核实（double-check）细节了。”
- **学生答案**: `if we could have had a better rollout plan for the new feature, we would not have to rely on every user to individually double-check details with me.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **完美终结！** 全部结构、介词、代词位置、时态逻辑全对。

## 场景化模拟实战 (Scenario Role-play)

### 场景: 关于 Jira 术语变更 (Task -> Work Item) 的高层沟通
- **学生原文**:
  > Well, now we have an issue about the nomenclature and we all know naming is a big thing in a big company. Because it can cause confusion and it can be a big barrier across different departments. So it's very important to align everyone on the nomenclature sooner than later. Well, the word tasks has a lot of meaning now. And we just rely on somebody to double-check what we mean in the meeting afterwards. So it brings a lot of troubles and takes a lot of time to align what we talk about. With this, we have came up with a feature. This is our rollout plan for that feature, and everyone can check it.

- **评价与不足**:
  - ✅ `sooner than later`, `rely on sb to double-check`, `rollout plan for` 调用成功，语感流畅。
  - 🔴 **`have CAME up with`**: 过去分词回退 (`come-came-come`)。
  - 🔴 **`a lot of troubles`**: `trouble` 指抽象概念时通常不可数。
  - 🔴 **`issue ABOUT`**: 建议用 `issue WITH`。
  - 🔴 **`naming is a big THING`**: 词汇过于基础，建议用 `vital challenge/critical matter`。
  - 🔴 **`missed EXTAIL`**: 虽然理解，但在自由写作中仍倾向于使用 `include` 或绕过。

## 重点纠错与不足总结 (Knowledge Gaps)

| 涉及知识点 | 原始错误/不足 | 纠正/改进方向 | 严重程度 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🔴 have COME up with** | `have came up with` | 牢记 come 的三态转换，`have` 后必接 pp。 | **高 (回退)** |
| **🔴 trouble (uncountable)** | `a lot of troubles` | 表示“麻烦”这一概念时，不可加 `s`。 | **中** |
| **🔴 notify vs notice** | `notice everyone` | 通知某人：`notify/inform`；`notice` 是注意到。 | **中** |
| **🔴 rely on sb TO do** | `rely on users report` | 必须加 `to`，这是固定搭配。 | **中 (Round 4 遗漏)** |
| **🔴 issue WITH** | `issue about` | 习惯性表达“某事有毛病”用 `with`。 | **低 (地道性)** |
| **thing/stuff 依赖** | `big thing` | 升级为 `critical matter / essential task`。 | **中 (持续监控)** |
