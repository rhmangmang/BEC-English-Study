## 闪电纠偏记录 (Flash Correct)

### 纠偏题 1: `at the same time` 介词与 `discuss`
- **中文**: “与此同时（at the same time），我们必须讨论（discuss）入职流程（onboarding process）之类的事情。”
- **学生答案**: `At the same time, we must discuss onboarding process and all that.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **完美修复！** 彻底改掉了 *in the same time* 的错误。`discuss` 后面没有加 *about*，及物动词属性掌握稳固。
- **小瑕疵**: `onboarding process` 前缺定冠词 `the`。

### 纠偏题 2: `by the day` 与 `walk through`
- **中文**: “即使当时局势一天比一天变糟（by the day），他也没带我详细梳理（walk through）那两个特定（specific）的迭代。”
- **学生答案**: `Even though the situation was getting worse by the day, he didn't walk me through those two specific iterations.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **全对！** `Even though` (事实) 与 `was getting worse` (动态) 处理得非常地道。

## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 1: a nod to ... (向...致敬 / 带有...的影子)

#### Round 1 — 带否定的陈述句 (Negative Statement)
- **中文**: “这个新产品的设计其实并没有向我们的传统工艺致敬，它完全是基于现代风格的。”
- **学生答案**: `this new product design doesn't actually give a nod to our craftsmanship. It is totally based on modern style.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **高分起手！** 灵活变换为动词词组 `give a nod to`，掌握了 `craftsmanship` 不可数属性。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句 (Special Question)
- **中文**: “既然这款香水的定位是如此现代，为什么它的包装还会专门向那个特定的传统时期致敬呢？”
- **学生答案**: `since the goal of the fragrance is modern oriented, why does its packaging specifically give a nod to the specific period?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **避开陷阱！** `specifically` (adv) 与 `specific` (adj) 区分完美。
- **不足**: `modern-oriented` 需加连字符。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句 (Tag Question)
- **中文**: “即使你们当时面临着压力，你们在这个特定版本中的设计也向我们的品牌传统致敬了，不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `even though you were faced with stress at that time, the design of this specific version still gave a nod to our brand legacy, didn't it?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **教科书级别！** `gave... didn't it` 极性完全匹配，`legacy` 词汇选择极具质感。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句 (Choice Question)
- **中文**: “由于目前发生的一切，你是想让这个新标志向我们的过去致敬，还是想让它保持前瞻性并融入现代环境？”
- **学生答案**: `With everything happening, do you want this new logo to give a nod to our past or do you still want it to keep forward-looking and fit in the modern environment?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **惊艳串联！** 成功融入 `With everything happening`, `forward-looking` 和 `fit in`。

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句 (Subjunctive - Mixed Conditional)
- **中文**: “如果这个品牌当初没有向我们的传统工艺致敬，它现在也就无法在更深的层次上打动中国消费者了。”
- **学生答案**: `If this brand had not given a nod to our traditional craftsmanship, it would not connect with Chinese consumers on another level.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **完美通关！** `had not given` (过去) -> `would not connect` (现在) 逻辑极其稳固。

### 语言点 2: forward-looking to ... (着眼于 / 面向)

#### Round 1 — 带否定的陈述句 (Negative Statement)
- **中文**: “这个项目目前并没有着眼于全球扩张，它更关注本地市场。”
- **学生答案**: `This project doesn't forward looking to the global expansion, it is more focused on the local market.`
- **评价**: **🔴 语法地雷**：`forward looking` 是形容词属性，不能接在 `doesn't` 后，需用 `is not`。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句 (Special Question)
- **中文**: “既然目前发生了这么多事，为什么你们的战略还会专门着眼于那些特定的细分市场？”
- **学生答案**: `With everything happening, why are your strategy still specifically forward looking to those specific market segments?`
- **评价**: **🔴 主谓不一致**：`strategy` (单数) 误用了 `are`。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句 (Tag Question)
- **中文**: “你的职业规划非常有前瞻性，不仅向传统领域致敬，还着眼于在世界舞台上的发展，不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `Your career plan is forward looking, it is not only a nod to the traditional industries, but also forward looking to the advancement in the world stage, isn't it?`
- **评价**: ✅ `is... isn't it` 匹配正确。
- **地道建议**: `advancement to the world stage` 比 `in` 更具动力感。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句 (Choice Question)
- **中文**: “由于目前发生的种种，你们的新品牌是着眼于现代风格，还是仅仅通过使用传统工艺来向过去致敬？”
- **学生答案**: `With everything happening, is your new brand forward looking to the modern style or a nod to the past just by using traditional craftsmanship?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** 平行结构清晰。

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气句 (Subjunctive - Mixed Conditional)
- **中文**: “如果那些外国品牌当初不仅仅是依赖它们的名气，而是更着眼于当代中国的样子，它们现在也就不必如此焦虑该如何融入了。”
- **学生答案**: `If those foreign brands had not just relied on their fame but had been more forward looking to what Chinese is today, they would not be upset how to fit in.`
- **评价**: ✅ 逻辑完全正确。
- **细节**: `what China is today` 而非 `Chinese`；`upset about how to`。

## 场景化模拟实战 (Scenario Role-play)
- **场景**: 董事会解释品牌战略 (为什么要兼顾传统与前瞻)
- **学生原文**:
  > Well, with everything happening, we should not only rely on our brand's fame but also show our forward-lookinging, and give a nod to traditions, so we can connect with consumers on a deep level and to fit in well.
- **评价**: 
  - ✅ 职场语气自然，框架逻辑清晰。
  - **🔴 词形错误**: `forward-lookinging` 误叠后缀。
  - ⚠️ `on a deep level` 词汇偏简，建议 `on another level` 以体现深度。
  - ⚠️ `to fit in` 平行结构冗余。
