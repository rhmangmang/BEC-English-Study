## 复习纠偏（开场 4 题）

### 第 1 题 — `go out for dinner`（顽固纠偏）
- **中文**: "如果这周没那么忙，我就带家人出去吃饭了。"
- **学生答案**: `If you're not busy this week, I'll go out for dinner with my family.`
- **评价**: ✅ `go out for dinner` 未犯 `get out` 错误 🏆；❌ 主语 `you` 与中文"我"不一致 → 应为 `I`；⚠️ 真实条件 `will` 可以接受，但"没那么忙"暗示实际很忙，Type 2 虚拟更贴切
- **精修**: `If I weren't so busy this week, I'd take my family out for dinner.`

### 第 2 题 — 间接疑问句语序
- **中文**: "你能告诉我这个项目什么时候截止吗？"
- **学生答案**: `Could you tell me what's the deadline for this project?`
- **评价**: ❌ `what's the deadline` → 间接疑问句需用陈述语序 `what the deadline is`
- **精修**: `Could you tell me what the deadline is for this project?`

### 第 3 题 — Type 3 条件句
- **中文**: "要是我当时知道有这个会议，我就提前准备了。"
- **学生答案**: `If I had known this meeting, I would have prepared it in advance.`
- **评价**: ✅ Type 3 结构 `had...would have` 满分 🏆；❌ `known this meeting`（不"认识会议"）→ `known about this meeting`（知道有此会）；❌ `prepared it`（准备"它"）→ `prepared for it`（为会议做准备）
- **知识点延伸**: `know sth` vs `know about sth` 辨析；`prepare sth` vs `prepare for sth` 辨析

### 第 4 题 — `quote unquote` 位置
- **中文**: "这就是你说的那个所谓的'紧急问题'吗？"
- **学生答案**: `Is this the emergency issue you said, quote unquote?`
- **评价**: ❌ `quote unquote` 再次跑到句尾 → 应紧贴被加引号的词 `Is this your, quote unquote, emergency issue?`
- **结论**: 🟠 位置漏洞仍未固化，Tag 极性之外的另一个复发点

## 73BEC 语言点 6：`That'd be...`（委婉肯定/设想评价）

> 课文原文：*Maybe we'll do an at-home happy hour. That'd be good.*

### Round 1 — 肯定陈述句
- **中文**: "我们可以在周五下午开个轻松的复盘会，这样挺好的。"
- **学生答案**: `We could have an easy review meeting next Friday. That'd be good.`
- **评价**: 🏆 `That'd be good` 结构正确；`easy` → `casual/light`（氛围轻松）；`next Friday` 时间指代修正
- **精修**: `We could have a casual review meeting on Friday afternoon. That'd be good.`

### Round 2 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "单独为这个小问题开个会不太好。"
- **学生答案**: `That wouldn't be good just to have a meeting for this little issue.`
- **评价**: ⚠️ 核心结构 `That wouldn't be` 正确；句子结构稍绕 → `Having a meeting just for this minor issue? That wouldn't be necessary.`
- **精修**: `Having a meeting just for this minor issue? That wouldn't be necessary.`

### Round 3 — 特殊疑问句
- **中文**: "你觉得哪个方案比较好？"
- **学生答案**: `Which option do you think would be better?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `do you think` 插入语位置正确，外企会议常见句型

### 总结

| 句式 | 结果 |
|:---|:---:|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | ✅ 满分 |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | ✅ 结构正确 |
| 🟢 特殊疑问 | ✅ 满分 🏆 |

## Micro Role-play（73BEC 整合）

### 场景
HR 恭喜你转正，问你入职体验。需用 `appreciate sb doing`, `As..., we'll...`, `thrive through`, `throughout`, `quote unquote`, `That'd be...`。

### Round 1
- **学生答案**: 
  > *Well, we'd appreciate you helping us a lot for the past few years. As the team grows, I think there will be a lot of things that we need to make it better. And newcomers who thrive through the onboarding process say it's a wonderful experience for them throughout the whole process. The quote unquote challenge for them is not a big deal. I think that would be the best thing our team have*
- **评价**: ⚠️ 角色意识偏移（应该是"我"转正后自己说的感受，不是替 newcomers 说）；`we'd` → `I really appreciate`；`The quote unquote challenge` → `The challenge, quote unquote,`（位置正确但放前面了）；`our team have` → `has`

### Round 2
- **学生答案**:
  > *Well, I'd appreciate you and your team helping me a lot for the past three months. As the team grows, I think we'll do our best to improve the existing things and I'm so glad that I've thrived through the whole process. And throughout the onboarding process, I feel like I'm a better man now, and the biggest challenge, quote unquote, is not a big deal for me. and I think I will write some suggestions for the upcoming newcomers I think that would be good for them.*
- **评价**: 🏆🏆🏆
  - `appreciate sb doing` ✅ `appreciate you...helping me`
  - `As..., we'll...` ✅ `As the team grows, we'll do our best`
  - `thrive through` ✅ `thrived through the whole process` 🏆
  - `throughout` ✅ `throughout the onboarding process`
  - `quote unquote` ✅ **位置正确！** `the biggest challenge, quote unquote`（Round 1 纠偏后修复 🏆）
  - `That'd be...` ✅ `that would be good for them`
- **精修点**: `I'd appreciate` → `I really appreciate`（直接陈述不用 would）；`a better man` → `grown a lot`（职场成长）

## 01BEC.md 遗留语言点训练（9 个知识点）

### LP 1: `go into work` vs `go to work`

> 课文：*Sometimes I still go into work to meet clients and so on.*

**概念理解**: ✅ 学生理解精准——`go into work` = 去公司地点，`go to work` = 去上班状态

| 轮次 | 中文 | 学生答案 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | 我一般周一到周四在家办公，但周五去公司 | `I usually work from home Monday to Thursday, but I go into work on Fridays.` | 🏆 满分 |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | 这周我不去公司，全部在家办公 | `I will not go into work this week. I'll work from home this week.` | ✅ 核心正确，`this week` 重复；精修合并 |
| 🟢 Quick Fire | 同事邀你去公司开会，你明天有约不上门 | `I'm gonna work from home, because I got an appointment.` | ⚠️ 未主动使用 `go into work`；`got` → `have`/`'ve got` |

### LP 2: `office job` vs `remote job`

> 课文：*Was that why you gave up your office job?*

**概念理解**: ✅ 学生理解精准——"线下办公" vs "远程办公"

| 轮次 | 中文 | 学生答案 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | 我之前一直都是做办公室工作，但去年我转成了远程工作 | `I had gotten an office job previously. However, I went remote working last year.` | ⚠️ `had gotten a job` = "找到过一份工作"而非习惯状态；`went remote working` → `switched to remote work` / `went remote` |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | 这份工作不是办公室工作，是远程职位 | `This job isn't an office job. It's a remote one.` | 🏆 满分 |
| 🟢 Quick Fire | 朋友问你新工作是 office job 还是 remote job | `Well, I used to go into work every day. But now, I can work from home.` | ⚠️ 场景理解偏差——回答描述了模式而非类型，未使用 `office job`/`remote job` |

### LP 3: `office space` 可数性

> 课文：*It means the company saves money on office space.*

**概念理解**: ✅ 学生知道大部分情况不可数，泛指"办公场地"

| 轮次 | 中文 | 学生答案 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | 这家公司在办公场地方面省了很多钱 | `This company has saved a lot of money on office space.` | 🏆 满分 |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | 这栋楼没有多余的办公空间了 | `There's no much office space in this building.` | ❌ **`no much` 错误** — `no` + `much` 不共存，应改为 `There isn't much office space available` |
| 🟢 Quick Fire | 老板问新楼办公空间够不够 | `Well, I don't think there's much office space available for us because we have too many people.` | 🏆 **`no much` → `not much` 修复成功！** |

### LP 4: `have + 时间 + off`（休息某时段）

> 课文：*Do you ever have a day where you decide to have the morning off...?*

| 轮次 | 中文 | 学生答案 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | 我这周五下午休息 | `I'll have the afternoon off this Friday.` | 🏆 满分 |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | 这周我不休假，下周再休两天 | `I won't take any day off this week. I will have two days off next week.` | ✅ 核心正确；`take...off` 也是地道表达 |
| 🟢 反义疑问句 | 你下周一下午休息，对吧？ | `You'll have the afternoon off next Monday, won't you.` | 🏆 满分（标点用 `?`，但语法正确）|
| 🟢 Quick Fire | 同事问下周什么时候有空开会，你周三下午休假 | `I'll have the afternoon off Wednesday, So we have to have a meeting this morning or on Thursday.` | ⚠️ `Wednesday` 前缺 `on`，或更自然用 `have Wednesday afternoon off`；`this morning` 应为 `Wednesday morning`（未来） |

**概念延伸 — `where` vs `when`**: 讨论 `a day where` vs `a day when` — 两者都正确但 `when` 更规范，`where` 把 day 看作场景，口语常见。

**概念延伸 — 一般现在时表将来**: `I have Wednesday off`（日程已定）vs `I'll have Wednesday off`（计划/想法）。一般现在时表达"日历上已确定"的安排，并非所有动词都能这样用——主要是 `have`/`start`/`leave`/`arrive`/`begin` 等。

### LP 5: `invite sb over`

> 课文：*I can't really invite them over to my house.*

**概念理解**: `over` = "到我这里来"的空间方向感，`invite sb to` 只是指向目的地

| 轮次 | 中文 | 学生答案 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | 我周末邀请了几个同事来我家吃饭 | `I invited a couple of colleagues over to my house for dinner at weekends.` | ✅ 结构正确；`at weekends`（习惯性）与 `invited`（一次性）时态矛盾 → `last weekend` |
| 🟢 时态讨论 | 表达"上周邀请同事这周来家吃饭" | 讨论 `this weekend` 指代方向 | ✅ 结论正确：`I invited...for dinner this weekend.`（`this weekend` 修饰 dinner，不是 invited） |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | 这周我不邀请任何人来我家 | `I won't invite anyone over to my house this week.` | 🏆 满分 |
| 🟢 Quick Fire | 周末安排，邀朋友来烧烤 | `I'm gonna invite some colleagues and friends over to my house to have barbecues. Do you want to come over?` | ✅ `invite sb over` + **主动用上 `come over`** 🏆；`barbecues` 复数 → 单个活动用 `a barbecue` |

### LP 6: `ever` 在疑问句（习惯 vs 过去）

> 课文：*Do you ever have a day where you decide to have the morning off...?*

**概念辨析**:
- `Do you ever...` — 时间范围=从过去到现在（阶段未结束），问习惯性/偶发性经历
- `Did you ever...` — 时间范围=已结束的过去时期
- `ever` 本身 = "曾经/有过"
- 有 `ever` 和没有的区别：`Do you work?`（问习惯） vs `Do you ever work?`（问有无此可能性）

| 轮次 | 中文 | 学生答案 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 🟢 肯定陈述（`Do you ever`） | 你现在在家办公，会有时候工作到很晚吗？ | `Do you ever work very late? given the situation that you work from home.` | 🏆 `Do you ever` 核心结构正确；`given the situation that` → `given that`/`now that` |
| 🟢 反义疑问句（否定+`ever`） | 你从没在周末工作过，对吧？ | `You didn't work on the weekends, did you?` | ❌ 未用 `ever`；`haven't ever worked`/`never worked` 才对应"从没……过" |

### LP 7: `not really` 委婉否定

> 课文：*Is there anything else you miss? — No, not really.*

**概念理解**: ✅ `not really` = 不完全是/也谈不上（不完全否定），比 `No` 更委婉

| 轮次 | 中文 | 学生答案 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 🟢 陈述句使用 | 我也不算真的很忙，只是手头有几件事要处理 | `I'm not really busy. Just need to deal with a couple of things now.` | 🏆 满分 |
| 🟢 独立回答 | 你很喜欢远程办公吗？也谈不上特别喜欢 | `Not really. I just think it's convenient.` | 🏆 满分 |
| 🟢 Quick Fire | 同事邀你去 hiking，不太想去但不想直接拒绝 | `Well, not really, because I got something to do.` | ✅ `Not really` 正确；`got` → `have`/`'ve got` |

### LP 8: 不定式表目的 `to do` vs `and do`

> 课文：*I finish work at 2:30 to get the children.*

**概念理解**: ✅ `to do` = 目的（为了接孩子才下班），`and do` = 先后顺序（下班然后接孩子，两件独立的事）

**学生要求跳过练习** ⏭

### LP 9: `that side of` 抽象用法

> 课文：*I miss that side of going into work every day.*

**概念理解**: ✅ 学生理解正确——"抽象的那一面"，不是具体可见的东西

| 轮次 | 中文 | 学生答案 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 🟢 肯定陈述 | 我很怀念和同事一起喝咖啡聊天的那一面 | `I really miss that side of going to have a coffee with my colleagues.` | ⚠️ `that side of` 结构正确；`going to have a coffee` 略绕 → `that side of things — grabbing coffee and chatting with colleagues` |
| 🟢 否定陈述 | 我不太喜欢项目管理的那个方面 | `I don't really like that side of project management.` | 🏆 满分（同时复用了 `not really`）|
| 🟢 Quick Fire | 吐槽新流程（文档流程繁琐，但 code review 还行） | `Well, I think the part about the document process is very complicated. However, that side of the review part looks good to me.` | ✅ `that side of` 用上；`document process`（文档流程/文件流程）→ `documentation process`（文档工作流程）区分；`that side of the review part` 中 `side`+`part` 冗余 |
