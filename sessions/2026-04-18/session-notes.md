## 肌肉记忆修复 (Flash Correct)

### 纠偏题 1: Tag Question 极性反转
- **中文**: “管理员到现在还没有为那个特定的营销活动填写（fill out）基本信息，不是吗？”
- **学生答案**: `The admin has not filled out the basic information for that specific campaign, have they?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **完美修复！** `has not` -> `have they` 极性完全匹配，`filled` 分词补丁固化。

### 纠偏题 2: 代词位置与指代
- **中文**: “既然你已经收到了那份信息（information），你为什么不把它填好（fill out）呢？”
- **学生答案**: `Since you've already got the piece of information, why don't you fill it out?`
- **评价**: 🏆 **全对！** `fill it out` 夹心结构正确，`information` 配合 `it` 指代无误。

## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 1: Email blast (邮件群发)

#### Round 1 — 带否定的陈述句
- **学生答案**: `Because of the budget restrictions, we will not carry out an email blast for the specific software update next month.`
- **评价**: 🏆 `carry out an email blast` 职场感极佳。

#### Round 2 — 特殊疑问句
- **学生答案**: `Since the admin has added those specific contacts, when exactly do we plan to start the email blast?`
- **评价**: 🏆 `when exactly` 位置地道。

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **学生答案**: `...carried out an email blast ..., hadn't they?`
- **评价**: 🔴 **时态回退**：`carried out` (did) 应配 `didn't they?`。已通过选择题专项修复。

#### Round 4 — 选择疑问句
- **学生答案**: `With everything happening, are we going to abort the email blast or has the admin already aborted it?`
- **评价**: 🏆 `aborted` 课文纠错词应用完美。

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气 (Mixed Conditional)
- **学生答案**: `If those specific leads had not been mass-added at that time, we would not be able to carry out the email blast this time.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！**

### 语言点 2: In the top right corner (在右上角)

#### Round 3 — 反义疑问句
- **学生答案**: `...the button ... is still in the top right corner, isn't it?`
- **评价**: 🏆 极性掌握稳固。修正了 `working normal` -> `normally`。

#### Round 5 — 虚拟语气 (Mixed Conditional)
- **学生答案**: `If the admin had not put this new button in the top right corner, we would not be able to click it now.`
- **评价**: 🏆 完美掌握方位介词 `in`。

## 场景化模拟实战 (Scenario Role-play)
- **场景**: CRM 专家指导新同事创建活动。
- **学生原文**:
  > Well, apologies for your inconvenience. So this is our guide to help people create campaigns and I'll walk you through this document ... what is an email blast ... etc.
- **优点**: 框架清晰，成功串联 3 个核心点。
- **不足**: 间接疑问句语序 (`what an email blast is`)；固定表达 (`Apologies for the inconvenience`)。

## 重点纠错与不足总结 (Knowledge Gaps)

| 涉及知识点 | 原始错误 | 纠正/改进方向 | 严重程度 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tag 时态一致** | `carried out ... hadn't they?` | `did` 时态需与 `carried` 匹配：`didn't they?` | **中** |
| **间接疑问句语序** | `know what is an email blast` | 保持陈述语序：`what an email blast is`。 | **低** |
| **副词修饰动词** | `working normal` | 必须使用副词：`working normally/properly`。 | **低** |
