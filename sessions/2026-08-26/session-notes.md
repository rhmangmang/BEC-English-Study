## 语言点 1：`I would suggest + doing`

- 肯定句：`I would suggest arranging a meeting, and then decide the project timeline.` 目标结构正确；修正并列为 `arranging ... and then finalizing ...`，并说明 `decide on / finalize the timeline`。
- 否定句第一次：`I would not suggest the second meeting before finalizing the project timeline.` 漏掉“安排/召开”这一动作；加练。
- 否定句加练：`I would not suggest finalizing the launch day before finalizing the project scope.` 结构通过；`launch date` 比 `launch day` 更常见。
- 一般疑问句：`Would you suggest confirming the timeline with the person in charge first?` — 🏆
- 反义疑问句：`You would suggest preparing the backup proposal first, wouldn't you?` — 🏆；`backup plan` 更常见。
- 选择疑问句：`Would you suggest preparing a backup proposal or directly finalizing the timeline?` — ✅ 两个动名词平行。

**随堂辨析**：`directly` 与 `go straight to` 都可以包含“跳过中间步骤”。真正差异在句法视角和作用域：`directly finalizing` 直接修饰动作，`go straight to finalizing` 把重点放在从当前状态转入下一环节；在给定语境中两者都成立。

## 语言点 2：`I suppose + clause / Do you suppose...`

- 肯定句：`I suppose it's the kind of issues that the person in charge would consider.` 目标结构正确；单一所指更自然为 `this is the kind of issue...`。
- 否定句：`I suppose this proposal would not give the person in charge a professional impression.` 修正搭配为 `make a professional impression on...`，或更自然地说 `I don't suppose this proposal would come across as professional.`
- 一般疑问句：`Do you suppose that the person charge has confirmed the timeline with the team?` 结构正确；固定短语漏 `in`，应为 `person in charge`。
- 反义疑问句：`I suppose the meeting has been arranged and is ready to start, isn't it?` — 🏆
- 选择疑问句：`Do you suppose we should sync the progress first, or go straight to the meeting?` 句型通过；更自然为 `sync up on progress` / `do a progress check-in`。

**随堂辨析**：系统讲清了物作主语的 `align with requirements`、人际协作的 `align on goals / align with sb on goals`，以及 `check with sb on/about sth` 与 `check sth with sb` 的信息焦点差异。

## 语言点 3：`a tangled mess of + noun`

- 肯定句：`The original prepared plan became a tangled mess of conflicted dates and dependencies.` 修正为 `The plan we had lined up has now become a tangled mess of conflicting dates and dependencies.`；历史 `line up` 成功复现。
- 否定句：`These small adjustments have not led to a tangled mess of conflicting priorities and deadlines.` — 🏆
- 一般疑问句第一次：`Do this kind of temporary change cause...` 主谓一致错误，`Do` 应为 `Does`；加练。
- 一般疑问句加练：`Does this kind of thing usually cause a tangled mess of tasks and deadlines?` — 🏆；历史 `kind of thing` 成功复现。
- 反义疑问句：`The tasks we lined up have become a tangled mess of deadlines and dependencies, haven't they?` — 🏆
- 选择疑问句第一次：`Will this change cause a tangled mess of approval process and dependencies, or will make the proposal cater to business requirements?` 第二个分句漏主语 `it`；`approval processes` 用复数；商务语境更自然为 `align the proposal with business requirements`。
- 选择疑问句加练：`Should we sort out this tangled mess of tasks and dependencies, or should we prepare another plan?` — 🏆

**教学纠偏**：学生指出原选择题题干逻辑不平行，反馈成立，随后更换题干。应继续确保两个选择项处于同一决策层级。

## 语言点 4：`give sb time to do sth`

- 肯定句：`We should give the person in charge a little bit time to confirm the timeline with the team.` 修正为 `a little time` 或 `a little bit of time`。
- 否定句：`We should not give the team more time to confirm the finalized timeline with the stakeholders.` — 🏆
- 一般疑问句：`Should we give the team more time to prepare the backup plan?` — 🏆；`the` 表示已有特定计划，首次引入通常用 `a`。
- 反义疑问句：`We have given the team enough time to prepare everything, haven't we?` — 🏆
- 选择疑问句：`Should we give the team some time to improve the existing plan or prepare another backup plan?` — 🏆；`another` 暗示再增加/换一份备用方案。

**随堂辨析**：`give sb time to get everything ready` 中不定式表示给予时间的目的/机会，本身不宣称“已经准备好”；要表达结果应说 `Everything is ready now` 或 `The team has finished preparing everything.`

## 本轮新暴露或复发的缺口

- **主语—助动词一致**：`Do this kind...` → `Does this kind...`。
- **并列疑问分句漏主语**：`or will make...` → `or will it make...`。
- **并列结构与选项逻辑平行**：形式平行之外，还要保证两个选项属于同一决策层级。
- **固定结构小词遗漏**：`a little bit of time`、`person in charge`。
- **词形与搭配**：`conflicting dates`，`make an impression on`，`launch date`。
- **不定式目的与结果的区分**：`time to get ready` 不蕴含任务已完成。
