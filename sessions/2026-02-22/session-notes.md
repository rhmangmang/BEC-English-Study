## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 1: be inclined to do sth. (倾向于做某事)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "I was inclined to group some things under vulnerability management..."
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "I'm not inclined to add new features in this release" — 满分
  2. ✅ 反义疑问句: "You are inclined to reuse the components of the old version, aren't you?" — `of` → `from`（来源用from）；`old version` → `previous version` 更专业
  3. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "Why are you inclined to group these tasks under one category?" — 满分！跨语言点复用 `group under` 🌟
  4. ⚠️ 选择疑问句: "Are you inclined to deal with this issue yourself or we together as a team?" — 平行结构不完整，`or` 后缺动词
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If we had been inclined to reuse the contents from the previous version, we would not have to develop again" — `had been inclined to` 完美；`contents` → `content`（不可数）；`develop again` → `redevelop`

### 语言点 2: all the way along (自始至终、全程)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "...there were tiny little MVCs all the way along."
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 中上
- **详细记录**:
  1. ⚠️ 否定陈述句: "There are no serious technical issues at all with this project all the way along" — 🔴 时态错误！`are` → `were`（叙述时态问题再次出现）
  2. ⚠️ 反义疑问句: "Someone has been following up on this requirement all the way along, hasn't they?" — `hasn't they` → `haven't they`（tag助动词跟代词走）
  3. ⚠️ 特殊疑问句: "How many people attended this project all the way along?" — `attended` → `were involved in`（attend 不搭配 project）
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "Have you been following up on this all the way along by yourself or with other colleagues' help?" — 平行结构满分 🏆；上一语言点的错误已修复
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Type 3): "If someone had monitored the status of the system all the way along, the crash would not have lasted so long" — `had monitored` → `had been monitoring`（持续动作用进行时）；`crash` → `outage`（持续性故障）

### 语言点 3: plug sth. in (把...整合进去)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "Can we reuse some of those things and plug them in here?"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ❌ 否定陈述句: "We have not plug those old components into the new system" — 🔴 `plug` → `plugged`（过去分词形式！）
  2. ✅ 反义疑问句: "You have already plugged those modules in, right?" — `plugged` 已修复！`right?` → `haven't you?`（标准tag）
  3. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "when do you plan to plug these features into the new version?" — 满分
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "Do you want to directly plug them in or test them before plugging them in?" — 代词 `them` 位置全部正确 🏆
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If we had plugged those components earlier, this version would not be delayed now" — Mixed满分；`earlier` 已修复 🏆；但漏掉 `in`

### 语言点 4: over the course of (在...期间/过程中)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "...over the course of the year, we went from this to this."
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "Over the course of the last quarter, the team did not make significant progress" — 满分！`make progress` 搭配修复成功 🏆
  2. ✅ 反义疑问句: "Over the course of the last half year, your products have improved a lot indeed, haven't they?" — `half year` → `six months`；`indeed` 放助动词和主动词之间更好
  3. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "Over the course of the last year, how many technical issues have your team solved?" — `issues` → `challenges`（难题强调难度）
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "Over the course of the past two months, did you focus on fixing online issues or developing new features?" — 平行结构满分 🏆；`online` → `production`（行业术语）
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If we had kept on optimizing the system over the course of the last year, we would not have so many technical debt now" — `kept on optimizing` 出色 🌟；`many` → `much`（technical debt 不可数）

### 语言点 5: it makes more sense to do A than B (做A比做B更有意义)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "It makes more sense to me to highlight that than any individual little thing."
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "It makes no sense to spend time fixing these little issues" — `no sense` → `not much sense`（程度偏差）；`spend time fixing` 搭配正确 🏆
  2. ⚠️ 反义疑问句: "it makes more sense to me to focus on the whole progress than any individual task" — 🔴 漏掉 tag `doesn't it?`；`whole` → `overall`
  3. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "Why do you think it makes more sense to show the whole result than individual features?" — `do you think` + 陈述语序满分 🏆；`whole` → `overall`
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "do you think it makes more sense to optimize the core features first or to extend the market first?" — `extend` → `expand into`（扩展市场的标准搭配）
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If we had thought it made more sense to refact than to patch, the code quality would not be so bad" — `it made`（时态回退/backshift）精彩 🌟；`refact` → `refactor`（拼写）；`bad` → `poor`

## 涵盖的关键概念

- **`be inclined to` 否定/虚拟变形**: `not inclined to` / `had been inclined to`
- **`group A under B`**: 把A归到B类目下 vs `group A`（把A归为一组）
- **`all the way along` = throughout**: 自始至终，贯穿全程
- **`plug A into B` vs `plug A in`**: 有目的地用 into；无目的地用 in；代词必须放中间
- **`over the course of + 时间段`**: 在某段期间，汇报进展高频表达
- **`it makes more sense to A than B`**: 做A比做B更合理，比较结构
- **时态回退 (Backshift)**: `thought it **made** more sense`（从句随主句回退为过去时）
- **`from` vs `of`**: 来源（复用自某版本）用 from；归属用 of
- **`overall` vs `whole`**: 整体/宏观用 overall；全部/完整用 whole
- **`attend` vs `be involved in`**: 出席活动用 attend；参与项目用 be involved in
- **`expand into` vs `extend`**: 扩展新市场用 expand into；延长时间用 extend
- **tag 助动词规则**: 跟 tag 代词走（they→haven't），不跟主句主语走
