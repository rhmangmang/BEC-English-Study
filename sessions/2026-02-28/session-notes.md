## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 1: before I forget (趁我还没忘)
- **来源**: 30BEC-23s 原文 "before I forget, I need to add you too"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "Before I forget, don't think this bug won't affect this iteration" — 双重否定强调到位。
  2. ⚠️ 特殊疑问句: "Before I forget, I'd like to ask when **you will** add..." — 🔴 **纠偏**：间接疑问句需用陈述语序 (知识缺口 #257)。
  3. ✅ 反义疑问句: "Before I forget, they've already started the next iteration, haven't they?" — 🏆 **反义疑问句大获全胜**，时态极性全对。
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "Should we get the tickets passed to the development team or the testing team?" — 跨语言点复用出色 🌟。
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Type 3): "If I had not forgotten it before last week's meeting, I would have added these leads..." — 🏆 **肌肉记忆稳固**。

## 重点错题记录

| 涉及知识点 | 原始错误句子 | 纠正后的正确表达 | 错误类型 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **词号辨析** | Because of its **emergency** | Because of the **urgency** | `Emergency` 是突发事件（名）；`Urgency` 是紧迫性（名）。 |
| **间接疑问句** | ask **when will you** | ask **when you will** | see/ask/know 后的从句需用陈述语序。 |
| **冠词细节** | to the Teams | to Teams / to the Teams group | 软件名直连或加 group。 |
