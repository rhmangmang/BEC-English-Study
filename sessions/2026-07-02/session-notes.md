## 📚 造句训练详情

### 语言点 1: `boost` (52BEC)

**来源**: `we have boosted sales... we also have boosted technical service`
**核心**: 商务义——提升、加强、拉升（拉规模/力度/数量）。搭配：boost sales/efficiency/service/support
**状态**: 🟡 复用中（新入池）

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误/反馈 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 这个新工具提升了效率，但大家还没察觉到区别。 | `This new tool has boosted efficiency. However, nobody has picked up on the difference.` | 🏆 | `boosted` ✅；`pick up on` 跨 session 结构正确(`on + the difference`名词短语)；`However` 句首断开 ✅ |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 新系统没有提升效率，而且也不是全员上阵的情况。 | `The new system didn't boost efficiency, and it's not all hands on deck.` | 🏆 | `didn't boost` ✅；`all hands on deck` 隔>14天唤醒成功即将升级；Comma Splice 无 |
| R3 | 一般疑问 | 这次推广有没有提升销量？ | `Did this campaign boost sales? Or did it bring no effect?` | ✅ | `Did...boost` ✅；`bring no effect` ❌→`didn't really make a difference` / `no real lift` 更自然。`effect` 不与 `bring` 搭配 |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 这个流程提升了协作效率，不是吗？而且你上手还挺快的。 | `This process has boosted collaboration efficiency, hasn't it? and you got onboard with it very quick.` | 🏆 | `has boosted...hasn't it?` 极性/时态/代词全部正确；`get onboard with` 隔15天成功复用 🏆；`very quick`→`very quickly`(副词) |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 你觉得是促销活动还是新设计提升了销量？ | `Do you think the sales campaign or the new design boost sales?` | 🔴 | **关键性错误：主谓不一致**。`A or B` 二选一主语视为单数→`boosts` ✅ |
| R5+ | 加练选择 | 你觉得是团队培训还是新流程提升了协作？ | `Do you think it was the team training or the new process that boosted the collaboration?` | 🏆 | 用 `it was...that...` 强调句绕开了主谓一致问题 → 结构满分 |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | 要是当初团队顶住了那些杂七杂八的需求，新功能早就能提升效率了。 | `If the team had pushed back on those non-critical requirements, the new feature would have boosted efficiency.` | 🏆 | Type3 `had pushed back...would have boosted` 完美；`push back on` 隔10天成功复用 🏆 |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖
**加练轮次**: 1 轮（选择疑问句主谓一致）
**植入历史词组**: `pick up on`🟢(R1) / `all hands on deck`🟡→🟢(R2) / `get onboard with`🟡→🟢(R4) / `push back on`🟡→🟢(R6)

### 语言点 2: `enhance` (52BEC)

**来源**: 52BEC 师生对话中辨析 `boost vs enhance`
**核心**: 提升品质/优化/使更好——与 `boost`(拉规模)相对。搭配：enhance quality/experience/reputation/value
**状态**: 🟡 复用中（新入池）

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误/反馈 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 我们精简了流程，也提升了用户体验。 | `We streamlined the process and enhanced user experience as well.` | 🏆 | `enhanced user experience` ✅；`streamline` 隔10天成功复用 🏆；平行结构 `streamlined...and enhanced` ✅ |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 这次改版没有提升体验，也不值得花那么多时间。 | `The modification didn't enhance experience, and it was not worth spending so much time.` | ✅ | `didn't enhance`✅；`be worth doing` 隔16天(14-21天艾宾浩斯高危窗口)成功唤醒 🏆；`experience`→`the experience` 缺冠词；`so much time`→`that much time on it` |
| R3 | 一般疑问 | 你觉得加那个动画提升了用户体验吗？ | `Do you think it has enhanced the user experience by adding the animation?` | ✅ | `has enhanced` ✅；结构偏绕→更自然：`Do you think adding the animation has enhanced the experience?` |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 新界面跟旧的一样没提升体验，是吧？ | `This new interface is the same as the old one. they didn't enhance the experience, didn't they?` | 🔴🔴 | **双关键性错误**：① Tag 极性回退——否定主句+否定Tag❌→`did it?`✅；② 代词 `they`→`it`❌(interface 单数)；`the same as` 比较对象一致性 ✅ |
| R4+ | 加练反义 | 这个改动没提升品质，不是吗？ | `This modification didn't enhance the quality, did it?` | 🏆 | Tag 极性修复✅；代词匹配✅；`enhance the quality` 搭配地道 |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 你觉得是培训提升了一线团队的技能，还是他们本来就很强？ | `Do you think the training enhanced the skills in the field, or they are good enough already?` | ✅ | `enhanced`✅；`in the field` 预暴露(位置偏→修饰 skills 不自然，应是 field team's skills)；`they are`→`they were`(时态平行) |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | 要是当初招了那个UX设计师，整个产品的品质早就提升了。 | `If you had hired the UX designer, the quality of the whole product would have been enhanced already.` | 🏆 | Type3 被动 `had hired...would have been enhanced` 完美 |
| Micro RP 1 | 整合 boost + enhance | 产品复盘会，改版效果汇报 | `Well, the modification made a very good difference. it has boosted the sales and enhanced the user experience.` | ✅ | `boosted sales` + `enhanced user experience` 两词全命中；`made a very good difference`→`really paid off`/`made a real difference`；`it`→`It` |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖（含 Micro RP）
**加练轮次**: 1 轮（Tag 极性+代词修复）
**植入历史词组**: `streamline`🟡→🟢(R1) / `be worth doing`🟡→🟢(R2) / `the same as`🟡(R4)

### 语言点 3: `in the field` (52BEC)

**来源**: `about 130 engineers in the field` + 学生对 `in the field` 提问后辨析 `field engineer`
**核心**: 在现场/外勤/一线（对比 office，不在办公室而在客户现场）。`field engineer` = 外勤工程师/职位名
**状态**: 🟡 复用中（新入池）

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误/反馈 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 外勤工程师发现了一个故障，我正在跟团队确认具体时间线。 | `An engineer in the field spotted a bug and I'm checking with the team on the concrete timeline.` | 🏆 | `engineer in the field` ✅；`check with sb on sth` 隔16天后成功唤醒 🏆；`bug`→`fault`(设备维修)；`concrete`→`specific` |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 我们在一线的工程师不多，所以碰到问题的时候响应比较慢。 | `We don't have many engineers in the field, so when we run into issues, the response to it will be slow.` | 🏆 | `engineers in the field` ✅；`run into issues` 隔约1个月成功唤醒 🏆；`response to it will be slow`→`response time tends to be slow` |
| R3 | 一般疑问 | 你们有多少工程师在外勤？够不够覆盖那些诊所？ | `How many engineers in the field are there? are they enough to cover those clinics?` | ✅ | `engineers in the field` ✅；语序更自然：`How many engineers do you have in the field?`；`are they enough`→`Are there enough of them` |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 你们有一线工程师在处理这事儿，不是吗？ | `We have engineers in the field dealing with this thing, don't we?` | ✅ | `engineers in the field` ✅；`have...don't we?` Tag 极性正确(实义动词→do)；`this thing`→`this issue`/`this` |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 是在场的工程师还是远程支持的人先发现了这个问题？ | `Did the engineer in the field or the one doing the remote supporting first brought up this issue?` | 🔴 | `engineer in the field` ✅；`bring up` 选词正确(无 `put up with` 混淆) 🏆；**动词形式错误** `Did...brought`❌→`Did...bring`✅；`the one doing remote supporting` 偏绕→`the remote support person` |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | 要是当初我们有一线工程师在现场，那个问题就不会拖那么久没人管。 | `If we had had engineers in the field, this issue would have been stepped in already.` | 🔴🔴 | **关键性错误：不及物动词被动化**。`step in`(介入)不及物→不能 `be stepped in`❌→`someone would have stepped in`✅ |
| R6+ | 加练Type3 | 要是当初有同事介入了，那个问题就不会拖那么久没人管。 | `If someone had stepped in, this issue would not have been waited so long.` | ⚠️ | `step in` 用人作主语修复 ✅；但 `waited` 又被动化了→`would not have dragged on for so long` / `been sitting unresolved`(wait也是不及物) |
| QF | 场景回应 | 大客户设备停机，问同事谁在现场能过去 | `Who is in the field and who can immediately go over and deal with that?` | 🏆 | `in the field` 自然嵌入问句；`go over and deal with`→`head over right away` 更口语化 |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖
**加练轮次**: 1 轮（不及物动词被动化 `step in`，同轮又暴露 `wait` 同类型错误，未完全修复）
**植入历史词组**: `check with sb on sth`🟡→🟢(R1) / `run into`🟢(R2) / `bring up` 首次植入(R5) / `step in`🟢→🔴(R6)

### 语言点 4: `a flashing light` (52BEC)

**来源**: `if the machine has a problem, if it has, you know, a flashing light...`
**核心**: 闪烁的警示灯/故障灯。设备维修场景实用词汇。`flashing`(adj)=一闪一闪的 vs 动词 `the light is flashing`
**状态**: 🟡 复用中（新入池）

| 轮次 | 句式 | 中文 | 学生回答 | 结果 | 关键错误/反馈 |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| R1 | 肯定陈述 | 这台机器的故障灯在闪，但这个修复应该能赶上下周的发布。 | `The machine's flashing light is flashing, and this fix should make it into next week's release.` | ✅ | `flashing light` ✅；`make it into` 隔8天成功复用 🏆；`flashing light is flashing` 冗余(名词短语+动词重复) → `the flashing light is on` |
| — | 学生提问 | 学生对 `The flashing light is on` 提出追问：灯"亮着"不一定表示它在一下亮一下暗 | → 辨析：`flashing light`(adjective→type of light) vs `the light is flashing`(verb→action)；学生理解后继续 |
| R2 | 否定陈述 | 机器连故障灯都没亮，但我们忍这个小毛病已经很久了。 | `The flashing light on the machine wasn't even on. We have put up with this glitch for a long time.` | 🏆 | `wasn't even on` ✅（`on`=亮着，回答前面讨论的理解正确）；`put up with` vs `bring up` 零混淆 🏆（知识缺口修复成功） |
| R3 | 一般疑问 | 那个灯的闪烁让你分心了吗？ | `Did the flashing of the light distract you?` | ✅ | 用名词化形式 `flashing of the light` 替代物件的 `flashing light`，语法正确但偏离目标名词短语 |
| R4 | 反义疑问 | 故障灯在闪，但这个工单已经被关掉了，不是吗？ | `The flashing light is on. However, this ticket has been closed off, hasn't it?` | 🏆 | `flashing light` ✅；`close off` 隔8天成功复用 🏆；Tag 完成时 `has been...hasn't it?` 正确 |
| R5 | 选择疑问 | 你是觉得闪绿灯还是闪红灯更紧急？而且我们已经不能再等了。 | `Do you think the green flashing or the red flashing is more urgent, and we can not wait any longer?` | ✅ | `flashing`→`flashing light`/`flashing one`(缺light)；`any longer` 隔约1个月成功唤醒 🏆；`and`前句误用选择/陈述并列→应拆两句 |
| R6 | 虚拟Type3 | 要是那盏红灯被标记为紧急的话，我们早就派人过去了。 | `If that red light had been flagged as urgent, we would have already sent people over.` | 🏆 | Type3 `had been flagged...would have sent` 完美；`flag as` 一次性修复(之前 `flagged emergency`❌→`flagged as urgent`✅) 🏆 |
| QF | 场景回应 | 远程排查设备故障，问客户故障灯有没有在闪 | `Could you see if the flashing light is on?` | 🏆 | `flashing light` 自然嵌入；间接疑问句语序 `if the light is on`✅；更自然: `Can you check if the flashing light is on?` |

**覆盖句式**: 6/6 全部覆盖
**加练轮次**: 0 轮（零关键性错误 🏆）
**植入历史词组**: `make it into`🟡→🟢(R1) / `put up with`🔴(R2, 知识缺口) / `close off`🟡→🟢(R4) / `any longer`🟡→🟢(R5) / `flag as`🔴(R6, 知识缺口修复)

**概念辨析穿插**: `flashing light`(adj+noun=灯的类型) vs `the light is flashing`(verb=正在闪的动作) / `on`=亮着 vs `flashing`=在闪

### 结构层面
1. **`boost` R5 (选择疑问句·主谓一致)**: `Do you think the sales campaign or the new design boost sales?` → `A or B` 二选一的主语用单数动词 `boosts`。这是本轮最典型的基础语法回退。加练轮用强调句式绕开，但加练也没直接面对主谓一致本身。

2. **`enhance` R4 (反义疑问句·Tag 极性+代词)**: 同轮两个错误：① `didn't enhance...didn't they?` 否定主句+否定Tag；② `they` 指 `the interface`(单数)。两个都是在 `enhance` 这个新语言点的造句压力下暴露的反义疑问句不稳定性。
