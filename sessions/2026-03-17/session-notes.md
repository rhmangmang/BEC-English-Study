## 语言点造句测试记录 (SENTENCE CONSTRUCTION)

### 1. the superset of (超集 / 总集)
- **结果**: 5/5 全通关 🏆
- **亮点**: `consider A B` 结构运用及其丝滑（无需 is）。
- **不足**:
  - 🔴 **拼写**: `superet` (漏掉 s)。
  - ⚠️ **语境**: `such a broader confusion` (a 冗余)。

### 2. specifically (具体地 / 特指地)
- **结果**: 5/5 全通关 🏆
- **亮点**: 成功组合 `Which ... exactly` 与 `specifically`，逻辑严密。
- **不足**:
  - ⚠️ **名词修饰**: `underlying logic defect` 略生硬，建议用 `defect in the underlying logic`。

### 3. go in & kind of / kinda (点进去 / 稍微)
- **结果**: 5/5 全通关 🏆
- **亮点**: 成功使用了口语连读 `kinda`，并在虚拟语气中灵活运用 `after going in` 简化句子。
- **不足**:
  - 🔴 **拼写**: `subdirecties` (拼写错误)。
  - ⚠️ **时态匹配**: 在复盘语境下，`once we go in` 宜统一为 `once we went in`。

## 场景化模拟实战 (Scenario Role-play)

### 场景: 全局搜索功能复盘 (Global Search Retrospective)
- **任务**: 解释搜索框让用户困惑的原因，串联 5 个核心点。
- **学生原文**:
  > "Well, it's always been a challenge to develop such a huge feature in a short time and I believe the issue goes back to the broader confusion of the concept of global search and the search window right now is actually the supeset of all the data, it's not specifically a type of data and I want to tell you, once you go in, you could kind of filter."
- **核心点集成**:
  - ✅ `it's always been a challenge`
  - ✅ `goes back to`
  - ✅ `broader confusion of`
  - ✅ `the superset of` (拼写微瑕 supeset)
  - ✅ `specifically`
  - ✅ `once you go in`
  - ✅ `kind of`
- **评价**: 🏆 **统治级输出**。逻辑异常清晰（挑战 -> 根源 -> 现况 -> 方案），从单句翻译跨越到了“整体组织叙述”。
