## 场景化模拟实战 (Scenario Role-play)

### 场景: 新功能 Beta 版复盘会 (Beta Launch Review Meeting)
- **任务**: 作为 Tech Lead，向 PM Cindy 复盘新功能 MVC 发布后漏掉的用户体验细节，提出深度讨论方案，并明确会议定性。
- **核心语言点集成**:
  - ✅ `missed some thoughts on user interactions in trying to replicate the user experience`: 精准运用了 `in trying to replicate`，且逻辑通顺（过程中遗漏）。
  - ✅ `go deeper on this issue`: 正确搭配 `on`（针对主题深入讨论）。
  - ✅ `this meeting is more of a learning lecture rather than defining whose problem`: 成功将 `more of A rather than B` 融入整段叙述，展示了本质定性能力。
  - ✅ `shift into a targeted planning mode`: 非常职场的收尾表达。
  - ✅ `Well, we have some thoughts on the beta launch.`: 主动使用了 `Well...` 缓冲词开场，非常地道。
- **不足**:
  - **🔴 `learning lecture` 用词不当**: `lecture` 是"课堂讲座"，在职场语境中描述"成长/学习机会"时更地道的是 **`learning opportunity`** 或 **`learning experience`**。这暴露出中文"学习"一词在英文中有多个映射，需要根据语境区分。
  - **🔴 `defining whose problem` 不够地道**: 虽然意思能传达，但在职场中表达"非追责"有更高级的固定搭配：**`assigning blame`**（分配责任/追责）或 **`pointing fingers`**（指责）。改为 `rather than assigning blame` 更具职场专业性。
  - **🔴 `give the better experience to users` 结构不自然**: 更简洁地道的表达是 **`provide a better user experience`** 或直接 **`improve the user experience`**。注意 `better` 前面不需要 `the`（比较级一般不加定冠词）。
  - **🔴 `make some improvement in it` 介词错误**: `improvement` 的搭配通常是 **`to`** 而非 `in`。改为 **`make some improvements to it`**。
  - **🔴 平行结构缺失**: `more of a learning lecture (名词短语) rather than defining whose problem (动名词短语)` → A 和 B 的语法结构不对称。应保持一致：**`more of a learning opportunity rather than a blame session`**（两边都是名词短语）。

## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 1: in + doing sth. (在……的过程中/行为中)
- **结果**: 5/5 全通关 🏆
- **关键亮点**:
  - **Round 1 否定陈述句**: `We didn't consider those potential pinch points in trying to improve our recruitment process.` → 语序非常自然，`in trying to` 放在句末作为"背景补丁"，是典型的职场口语习惯。跨模块复用了 `potential pinch points` 🌟。
  - **Round 3 反义疑问句**: `We have to report the progress to the tech lead in replicating our onboarding experience, don't we?` → **`don't we?`** 助动词转换满分 🏆。
  - **Round 5 虚拟语气**: `If we had solved those potential pinch points in replicating onboarding experience, we would not have to have a bi-weekly cadence meeting.` → 混合虚拟语气运用完美，跨模块复用了 `bi-weekly cadence meeting` 🏆。
- **不足**:
  - **🔴 Round 2 漏译"究竟"**: 原文中明确要求翻译"究竟"并建议复用 `exactly`，但学生给出的答案 `What type of work is most confusing in improving the system?` 中完全遗漏了 **`exactly`**。这说明在非强制语境下，学生对 `exactly` 的使用仍然需要提醒，尚未变成"自动扫描"的习惯。**严重程度：中**。
  - **🔴 Round 3 `process` vs `progress` 再次混淆**: `report the process` ❌ → 应为 **`report progress`**（汇报进度）。`process` = 流程/过程，`progress` = 进度/进展。这一混淆点在 `progress-tracker.md` 中已有记录（2026-02-21），时隔近三周再次复发，说明该知识点尚未形成肌肉记忆，**需要专项对抗训练**。**严重程度：高**。
  - **🔴 Round 5 冠词遗漏**: `in replicating onboarding experience` → 特指的那个入职体验，应为 **`the onboarding experience`**。这与 03-08/03-09 建立的冠词意识稍有矛盾，说明在虚拟语气的高认知负荷下，冠词的自动挂载能力仍会偶尔松动。**严重程度：低**。

### 语言点 2: go deeper (on/into) (深挖/深入讨论)
- **结果**: 5/5 全通关 🏆
- **关键亮点**:
  - **Round 2 特殊疑问句**: `About the project progress, at what timing exactly should we go deeper on that?` → `Exactly` 位置精准。`About...` 句首作为背景铺垫 (Topic Fronting) 是很高级的口语策略 🌟。
  - **Round 3 反义疑问句**: `On tomorrow's meeting, we have to go deeper into the concrete reasons for the project delay, don't we?` → **`don't we?`** 再次满分 🏆。`concrete reasons` 用词非常专业。
  - **Round 5 虚拟语气**: `If we had gone deeper on the core proposal, we would not be confused about the project progress.` → 混合虚拟语气完美，且成功区分了 `progress`（进度）与 `process`（流程），**当日修复** 🏆。
- **不足**:
  - **🔴 Round 1 时态不匹配 (Conditional Tense)**: `If we don't get any input... we could not go deeper` → `don't` (现在时) 搭配 `could not` (过去式助动词) 时态不一致。正确版本：`If we don't get... we **won't** be able to...`（真实条件）或 `If we **didn't** get... we **could** not...`（虚拟假设）。这属于**条件句时态匹配**的新暴露点。**严重程度：中**。
  - **🔴 Round 3 会议介词 `on` vs `in`**: `On tomorrow's meeting` → 应为 **`In tomorrow's meeting`**。会议是一个"场景/容器"，通常搭配 `in`。`On` 通常用于具体的某一天（如 `On Monday`），而非活动/事件本身。**严重程度：中**。
  - **Round 4 再次漏译 `exactly`**: 原文"究竟"对应 `exactly`，但学生翻译 `which one is more important` 中未包含 `exactly`。连续两次漏掉，说明 **`exactly` 在选择疑问句中的自动扫描意识仍弱于在特殊疑问句中的表现**。**严重程度：中**。

### 语言点 3: shift into (切换到/转变为/进入)
- **结果**: 5/5 全通关 🏆
- **关键亮点**:
  - **Round 2 特殊疑问句**: `During the current recruitment peak, when exactly should you shift into a more strategic role?` → **`When exactly`** 位置满分，教科书级别 🏆。
  - **Round 3 反义疑问句**: `In order to avoid more pinch points, we have to shift into a more automated system, don't we?` → **`don't we?`** 连续满分，**`automated`** 用词正确（成功戒掉 `automatic`）🏆。
  - **Round 5 虚拟语气**: `If we had solved those potential pinch points earlier, we would have already shifted into a more effective coaching role.` → `would have already shifted into` 时态层次处理精准。
- **不足**:
  - **🔴 Round 1 代词与不可数名词不匹配**: `those awkward wording` → **`wording`** 是不可数名词，不能搭配复数代词 `those`。应为 **`the/that awkward wording`** 或者改为可数名词 **`those awkward phrases/expressions`**。这是"中文无数量直觉→英文忘记标注可数/不可数"的典型干扰。**严重程度：中（新发现）**。
  - **Round 4 平行结构违规**: `which one exactly is more suitable: will you shift into... or should I stick to...` → 冒号后面需要的是两个平行的**动名词短语**（Gerund Phrases），而非两个完整的独立问句。正确：`shifting into A or sticking to B?`。**严重程度：中（强化了平行结构的已知缺口）**。

### 语言点 4: more of A rather than B (更多是 A，而不是 B)
- **结果**: 5/5 全通关 🏆
- **关键亮点**:
  - **Round 1 否定陈述句**: `This improvement is more of a huge win for the whole team rather than an individual effort.` → 结构满分，跨模块复用 `huge win` 和 `the whole team` 🏆。
  - **Round 3 反义疑问句**: `We have to see this agenda as more of an improvement to the visibility rather than just a process change, don't we?` → `have to` Tag 助动词已完全固化 🏆。
  - **Round 5 虚拟语气**: `If we had seen that agenda item as more of a huge win for the whole team rather than someone's credit, we would have already got the head nods from the architect, wouldn't we?` → 🏆 **本日最佳句子！** 融合了 `more of A rather than B` + 混合虚拟语气 + Tag Question + 39BEC 的 `head nods`，四重叠加零失误。Tag Question 极性完全正确。
- **不足**:
  - **🔴 Round 2 再次漏用 `exactly`**: `In terms of this proposal, what exactly will make the role more like a facilitator...` → 虽然实际上用了 `exactly`，但未使用目标结构 `more of ... rather than`，而是替换为 `more like`（更像）。**`more of` 用于本质定性**（实际上是），**`more like` 用于类比**（看起来像）。在讨论角色本质定义时，`more of` 更精准。**严重程度：低**。
  - **Round 3 `agenda` vs `agenda item`**: `this agenda` 指整个会议日程表。课文原文是 `Agenda item No.4`，具体到某一项讨论点应为 **`this agenda item`**。**严重程度：低**。
  - **Round 3 介词微调**: `improvement to the visibility` → 改为 **`improvement in visibility`** 更自然。`improvement in + 抽象名词` 是更常见的搭配。**严重程度：低**。
