## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 6: group sth. under (把…归到某类目下)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "I was inclined to group some things under vulnerability management"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "We didn't group these requirements under the same category" — 满分
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "Under which module do you plan to group these tasks?" — 满分！介词前置 `Under which` 非常正式 🌟
  3. ✅ 反义疑问句: "The product team have grouped these features under the category of user experience, haven't they?" — `haven't they` 完美匹配（02-22 知识缺口已修复）🏆；`under the category of user experience` 稍冗长，可简化为 `under user experience`
  4. ⚠️ 选择疑问句: "Do you want to group these issues under performance optimization or system vulnerability?" — 🔴 `vulnerability`(漏洞) ≠ `stability`(稳定性)，词汇混淆
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If we had grouped these tasks under the same category, we would not have to reprocess them" — Mixed Conditional 满分 🏆

### 语言点 7: give sb fodder to do sth. (给某人素材/弹药去做某事)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "give them fodder to go out with"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ⚠️ 否定陈述句: "This update did not give any fodder to the market team" — `market team` → `marketing team`（market 是名词，marketing 才是修饰语）
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "What fodder did this version give to the PR team?" — 满分
  3. ⚠️ 反义疑问句: "This new feature did give the sales team fodder, didn't it?" — `did give` 强调用法出色 🌟；但漏译"不少" → 缺 `quite a bit of`
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "Do you want to give the team the technical fodder or the business fodder?" — 平行结构正确；可简化为 `fodder on the technical side or the business side`
  5. ⚠️ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If we had prepared more fodder, the PR team would have things to say" — Mixed 结构满分；`say` → `promote`（宣传≠说）；漏译"现在"

### 语言点 8: input into (给…提供素材/参考/支撑)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "This is kind of like input into our PR team"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "The development team didn't give any input into the product decision on the technical side" — 满分！`on the technical side` 跨语言点复用 🌟
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "What input did you provide on the product plan this time?" — `input on`（关于主题）vs `input into`（注入流程），两者区别已讲解
  3. ⚠️ 反义疑问句: "The testing team also provided quite a bit input into the releasing process, didn't they?" — 缺 `of`（quite a bit **of** input）；`releasing process` → `release` / `release process`
  4. ⚠️ 选择疑问句: "Do you want to provide input on data or customers feedback into the team?" — `customers` → `customer`（修饰语不加s）；介词混搭（on + into 结构混乱）→ 应用 `provide the team with input on data or customer feedback`
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If each team had provided input, the plan now would not have so many issues." — Mixed 满分 🏆；`now` 放句尾更自然

### 语言点 9: hit (覆盖/触达)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "we can hit kind of different areas of press"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ✅ 否定陈述句: "Last quarter's promotion didn't hit all the target markets" — 满分
  2. ⚠️ 特殊疑问句: "How many media did this release hit?" — `media` → `media channels`（漏译"渠道"）
  3. ✅ 反义疑问句: "The features of this release almost hit all the user groups, don't they?" — tag正确；`almost hit all` → `hit almost all`（almost 修饰 all 更精准）
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "Do you want to hit the domestic market first or the overseas one?" — 满分！`the overseas one` 代词替换优雅 🌟
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If this promotion had hit more channels, the results would be much better now" — 满分！`much better` 翻译"好得多"精准 🏆

### 语言点 10: from this to this (从之前到现在的对比)
- **来源**: 26BEC-12s 原文 "we went from this to this"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 中上
- **详细记录**:
  1. ❌ 否定陈述句: "This project has not went from the initial phrase to the mature phrase over the course of the last year" — 🔴 `went` → `gone`（过去分词错误！复发!）；`phrase` → `phase`（拼写混淆）
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "How did your product go from a small tool to a complete platform?" — 满分 🏆
  3. ⚠️ 反义疑问句: "Your team have gone from a squad of three people to a cross-team one, haven't you?" — `gone` 已修复 🏆；`haven't you` → `haven't they`（tag代词替换主语核心名词）；`cross-team` → `cross-department/cross-functional`
  4. ⚠️ 选择疑问句: "Did you go from the manual process to the automatic one or start from the automatic process in the beginning?" — `automatic` → `automated`（流程用automated）；`in the beginning` → `from the start`
  5. ⚠️ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If we had not started from a small project, we would not develop to such a scale now" — Mixed结构基本正确；`develop`（动作）→ `be at`（状态），现在的规模是状态不是动作

## 涵盖的关键概念

- **`group A under B`**: 把A归到B类目下，工作归类高频表达
- **`Under which + N`**: 介词前置构成正式特殊疑问句
- **`give sb fodder`**: 给某人宣传素材/弹药，PR场景常用
- **`marketing` vs `market`**: marketing team (修饰语), market (名词)
- **`input into`（流程/团队）vs `input on`（主题）**: 两种介词搭配的语义区分
- **`a bit of` / `quite a bit of`**: of 不可省，固定搭配
- **复合名词修饰语**: 用原形不加 s（customer feedback, release process）
- **`provide sb with sth on topic`**: 同时指明对象和主题时的最佳结构
- **`hit` 引申义**: 覆盖/触达（非"打击"），宣传/目标场景高频
- **`go from A to B`**: 从A发展到B，口语用 this to this，正式用具体描述
- **`almost` 位置**: almost hit all（差点达到全部）vs hit almost all（覆盖了大部分）
- **`phase` vs `phrase`**: 阶段=phase /feɪz/；短语=phrase /freɪz/
- **`automatic` vs `automated`**: 流程/系统用 automated；机械/设备用 automatic
- **tag代词替换规则**: 替换主语核心名词（Your team→they），不是所有格（your→you）
- **虚拟语气：状态vs动作**: "处于某规模/状态"用 be at，不用 develop to
