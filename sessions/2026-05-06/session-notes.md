## 概念辨析记录

### 话题 1: `I'd rather` vs `I prefer`（学生主动追问）

- **学生提问**: 它们之间的区别是什么？
- **核心区别**:
  - `I'd rather` + 动词原形 / `I'd rather A than B`（A、B 都用原形）。更口语、带"宁愿"情绪。
  - `I prefer` + to do/doing/名词 / `I prefer A to B`（to + 名词/动名词）。稍正式，陈述偏好。
  - 不能混：`prefer A than B` ❌
- **掌握情况**: 🟢 讲解即理解，后续无需专项

### 话题 2: `meet with sb` vs `meet sb`（学生主动追问区别认知）

- **学生先自己答**: "meet with 更正式"（对了一半）
- **导师引导提问**: "我在超市碰到老王" vs "我明天要跟律师约谈签合同" 分别用哪个？
- **学生答**: 前者 `meet`，后者 `meet with`。"前者见面的目的很日常，后者见面的目的比较正式。"
- **核心区别确认**:
  - `meet sb` = 见到某人（动作本身，可偶遇可约好）
  - `meet with sb` = 专门约谈（有明确目的：讨论、汇报、磋商）
- **掌握情况**: 🟢 辨析理解到位，后续 Round 2/3 使用正确

### 话题 3: `come across as`（学生主动追问）

- **学生提问**: 什么意思？
- **讲解**: `come across as` = 给人的印象是…… / 显得……
  - `He comes across as confident.` = 他给人的感觉很自信。
  - `I didn't come across as someone who knew nothing.` = 我不至于显得像个什么都不懂的人。
- **掌握情况**: 🟢 学生理解，但大 Role-play 最后一句仍用了 `No one feels that I'm a rookie` 而非 `come across as`，说明还需复用强化

### 话题 4: `outline` vs `outsider`（纠偏中触发的拼写问题）

- **学生错误**: `make me look like a outline` — `outline`（大纲/轮廓）≠ "外行"
- **正确用词**: `outsider`（局外人/外行）/ `rookie` / `newbie`
- **注意**: 元音开头 `a outline` → `an outline`，但 `outsider` 也不读 `an` 开头的问题
- **掌握情况**: 🟡 学生当场理解，但"外行=outline"是拼写上的惯性错，需警惕

### 话题 5: `document` vs `documentation`（学生主动追问）

- **学生提问**: 区别是什么？
- **核心区别**:
  - `document` = 一份具体的文件（可数名词）。`a document` / `documents`
  - `documentation` = 文档资料的统称（不可数名词）。不加 `s`，不加 `a`
  - 公式：document = 一棵树；documentation = 整片森林
- **掌握情况**: 🟢 讲解即理解。但历史上有 `documentations` 错误，后续需留意是否复发

### 话题 6: `basic document` vs `most basic docs`（学生在 Round 2 追问）

- **学生提问**: 区别是什么？
- **核心区别**:
  - `the basic document` = 一份叫"基础文档"的文件，强调具体某一本
  - `the most basic docs` = 最基础的那批文档，强调程度（"连最浅的都还没消化完"）
  - 原句"最基础的文档"是第二种意思
- **掌握情况**: 🟢 讲解即理解

## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 4: `consume`（消化/吸收知识/信息）

> 从课文中提取（学生问 `consume` 在这里什么意思）

#### Round 1 — 肯定陈述句
- **中文**: "我不喜欢开会时被人灌输信息，我更想自己先看文档，按自己的节奏消化。"
- **学生答案**: `I don't like to be told in a meeting. I prefer to see the documentation first then consume it at my own pace.`
- **评价**:

| 问题 | 原文 | 修正 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `be told` 偏"被告知" | 原文是"被人灌输信息" | `being spoon-fed information` |
| `see the documentation` 偏"看到" | 读文档/过文档 | `go through` / `read through` |
| `first then` 缺逗号 | 节奏不自然 | `first, then` |

- **精修**: `I don't like being spoon-fed information in meetings. I'd rather go through the documentation myself first and then consume it at my own pace.`

#### 后续 Quick Fire 中使用
- **场景**: 同事问你为什么不需要别人手把手教你。
- **学生答案**: `Well, I believe that after I consume the documentation, I can ask high-level questions - not the low-level ones.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `consume the documentation` 主动用上，`ones` 替代 `questions` 解决重复问题。`since` / `after` 逻辑关系准确。

### 语言点 5: `meet with sb`（专门约谈/会面）

> 从课文中提取（学生问 `meet with` 什么意思）

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句
- **中文**: "与其跟 20 个人一个个约谈，不如让他们先把知识写到文档里。"
- **学生答案**: `Instead of meeting with 20 people individually, it's better for them to write their knowledge into the documentation.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `meeting with 20 people individually` 核心短语完全正确 🏆
  - ❌ `it's better for them to write` — "不如让他们"≠"对他们更好"，地道说法 `it'd be better to have them...`
  - ⚠️ `write their knowledge into the documentation` — 不简洁。`put their knowledge in a doc` / `document what they know`
- **精修**: `Instead of meeting with 20 people individually, it'd be better to have them put their knowledge in a document first.`

#### Round 2 — 肯定陈述句 + `consume` 融合
- **中文**: "我先自己把文档消化了一遍，所以后来跟他们约谈的时候，我问的都是有深度的问题，不至于像个什么都不懂的新人。"
- **学生答案**: `I consumed the document myself first and then when meeting with them, I was able to ask high level questions. I was not like I didn't know anything.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `consumed the document` + `when meeting with them` + `high level questions` — 三合一天然融合 🏆
  - ⚠️ `high level` → `high-level`（连字符）
  - ❌ `I was not like I didn't know anything` — 语法绞在一起。"不至于像个什么都不懂的新人"的地道说法：`I didn't come across as someone who knew nothing`
- **精修**: `I consumed the documentation myself first, so when I met with them later, I was able to ask high-level questions. I didn't come across as someone who knew nothing.`

### 语言点 6: `go in and speak their language`（参与讨论+同频交流）

> 从课文中提取（学生问 `go in` 什么意思，`speak their language` 什么意思）

#### Round 1 — 否定陈述句 + `let alone`
- **中文**: "刚开始的时候我连他们的术语都听不懂，更别说参与进去跟他们同频交流了。"
- **学生答案**: `At first, I didn't even understand jargon. Let alone go in and speak their language.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `go in and speak their language` 核心短语正确 🏆
  - ❌ **`let alone` 断句错误** — `let alone` 不是独立句子开头，必须跟前面否定句连成一句。结构：[否定陈述], let alone [更难的事]
  - ⚠️ `jargon` 缺限定词 — `their jargon` / `the jargon`
- **精修**: `At first, I couldn't even understand their jargon, let alone go in and speak their language.`

#### Round 2 — `let alone` 修复 + 复杂否定句
- **中文**: "我连最基础的文档都还没看完，更别说加入他们的技术讨论、跟他们同频交流了。"
- **学生答案**: `I didn't even finish the basic document, let alone go in their technical discussions and speak their language.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ **`let alone` 这次一句话连在一起了** 🏆 比上一句断句进步显著
  - ❌ `go in their technical discussions` — `go in` 是不及物，不能直接接名词 → `join in on` / `get involved in`
  - ⚠️ `the basic document` → `most basic docs` / `even the basics`
- **精修**: `I haven't even finished the basic documentation yet, let alone joined in on their technical discussions or spoken their language.`
- **知识点补充**: `let alone` 后的动词要与前面否定句的动词平行（`finished` → `joined` / `spoken`）

#### Round 3 — Quick Fire 对比结构
- **场景**: 你换了一个新团队，同事问你感觉怎么样。你说还行——至少你现在不会像刚入职时候那样，完全插不上话。
- **学生答案第一版**: `I'm feeling not bad. At least it's not like when I got onboarded at first, I could speak their language now.`
- **反馈**: `it's not like when...` + `could... now` 逻辑打架。"插不上话"没翻译出来
- **骨架提供**: `It's not like when I [过去状态], where I [过去的糟糕情况]. At least now I can [现在的进步].`
- **学生答案第二版**: `It's not like when I got onboarded, where I could not go in and speak their language. At least now I can talk to them.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **结构大进步！** 对比骨架完整。`go in and speak their language` 用在否定从句里自然
  - ⚠️ `got onboarded` → `when I first joined` / `when I first started` 更自然
- **精修**: `It's not like when I first joined, where I couldn't go in and speak their language at all. At least now I can actually jump in and talk with them.`

### 语言点 7: `high-level questions`（有深度的问题）

> 从课文中提取（学生问 `high level questions` 什么意思）

#### 概念理解
- **学生自己的理解**: "high-level questions 指的是更加有深度、更加抽象一点的问题。low-level questions 指的是更具体的问题。"
- **补充**: 对了一半。在职场语境下，核心差异在于：
  - `high-level questions` = 问思路、逻辑、原因、架构（给人感觉：做过功课、懂行）
  - `low-level questions` = 问基础操作、表面细节（给人感觉：没预习、小白）
  - 课文 `didn't make me look silly` 说明问 high-level questions 的目的是不显得外行

#### Round 1 — 肯定陈述句
- **中文**: "我先看完文档之后就能问出有深度的问题了，这让我在会议里不至于像个外行。"
- **学生答案**: `I can ask high level questions after I read the document. It won't make me look like a outline in a meeting.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `ask high level questions` 核心短语正确
  - ❌ `a outline` → `an` 元音开头
  - ❌ `outline`（大纲）→ `outsider`（局外人/外行）→ 拼写错误
  - ⚠️ `high level` → `high-level`（连字符）
  - ⚠️ 时态存在线（现在时当一般规律也可通，但与前文叙事语境不一致）
- **精修**: `I was able to ask high-level questions after going through the documentation, which made me not look like a complete rookie in the meeting.`

#### Round 2 — `high points` + `high-level questions` 对比句
- **中文**: "这次项目复盘，亮点是我们问的都是有深度的问题，不是那种小白问题；糟点是文档还是不够清楚。"
- **学生答案**: `After we reviewed this project, we found that the high point was that what we asked are all high-level questions. They were not like low-level questions. The low point was that the documentation was still not clear enough.`
- **评价**:
  - ✅ `high point` + `low point` 对比结构完整，`documentation` 不可数正确 🏆
  - ❌ `are` → `were`（复盘已做，全句过去时）
  - ⚠️ `what we asked are all` 冗长 → `the questions we asked were all`
  - ❌ `not like + 名词` 语感像"不像小白问题"（比喻义）→ 实际想表达"不是小白问题那种类型" → `not the low-level kind` / `rather than low-level ones`
- **精修**: `After the project retrospective, we found that the high point was that the questions we asked were all high-level questions — not the low-level kind. The low point was that the documentation still wasn't clear enough.`
- **知识点补充**: 重复表达问题——`questions` 在一句话里出现两次，改进方案：
  - 用 `ones` 替代：`high-level questions — not the low-level ones`
  - 换结构：`we were asking high-level questions the whole time`

#### Quick Fire
- **场景**: 同事问你为什么不需要别人手把手教你。你说因为你先花了时间看文档，看完之后问的都是有深度的问题，不是什么鸡毛蒜皮的小问题。
- **学生答案**: `Well, I believe that after I consume the documentation, I can ask high-level questions - not the low-level ones.`
- **评价**: 🏆 **满分！** `consume` 主动用上，`low-level ones` 替代重复，对比清爽。

## Micro Role-play（7 个语言点整合）

### 场景
入职 3 个月的开发，经理找你一对一聊聊入职体验。

### 要求语言点
`at my own pace`, `consume`, `go in and speak their language`, `high points`, `meet with`, `high-level questions`, `let alone`

### 学生原文
> `At first, I could not understand the jargon that the team used, let alone I went in and spoke their language. Afterwards, I found there was a knowledge system, and I could consume all the documentation at my own pace. After a while, I thought I could go in and speak their language. So I did it. And the high point was the Buddy system. He met with me several times and helped me understand the technical architecture. Now I can ask high-level questions in meetings. I'm not like a newbie here.`

### 逐句评价

| 句子 | 评价 |
|:---|:---|
| S1: `let alone I went in and spoke their language` | ✅ 否定+`let alone` 连句结构意识对。❌ `let alone` 后接**完整句子**（`I went in`）——口诀：砍主语砍时态，只留原形 → `let alone go in and speak their language` |
| S2: `consume all the documentation at my own pace` | 🏆 三个语言点无缝融合！⚠️ `a knowledge system` → `a knowledge base` / `an internal wiki` |
| S3: `I thought I could... So I did it` | ✅ 核心对。⚠️ 逻辑跳——更自然：`I got to a point where I could...` |
| S4: `the high point was the Buddy system. He met with me` | 🏆 `high point` 精准；✅ `met with me` 完全正确。⚠️ `He` → `My buddy` |
| S5: `I'm not like a newbie here` | ❌ 结构错误。`I'm not like X` = "我跟 X 不像"（比较人格）。→ `I don't come across as a newbie anymore` |

### 精修版
> `At first, I couldn't understand the team's jargon at all, let alone go in and speak their language. But then I found out we had a knowledge base, and I was able to consume all the documentation at my own pace. After a while, I got to a point where I could actually go in and speak their language. The high point was definitely the buddy system — my buddy met with me several times and helped me understand the technical architecture. Now I can ask high-level questions in meetings. I don't come across as a newbie anymore.`

## 大场景 Role-play（最终整合）

### 场景
新加入公司 3 个月的 Team Lead，跟上级做入职回顾。

### 要求语言点
`at my own pace`, `consume`, `go in and speak their language`, `high points`, `meet with`, `high-level questions`, `let alone`, `speak their language`, `come across as`

### 学生原文
> `Well, at first I could not understand the team's jargon, let alone go in and speak their language. Afterwards, I found that the team had an internal technical wiki, so I consumed all the documentation at my own pace. Just about two weeks ago, I met with every team member individually to know their current situation. And through all this, I found the biggest high point was that there was a good technical atmosphere in the team. When I asked high-level questions, people wouldn't get upset. They'd rather talk with me. It wouldn't make me look silly. And now I can speak their language. No one feels that I'm a rookie anymore.`

### 语言点集成检查

| 语言点 | 状态 |
|:---|:---:|
| `let alone go in and speak` | ✅ **满分！彻底修复** 🏆 — 断句历史错误在后两次全部纠正 |
| `consume all the documentation at my own pace` | ✅ 精准融合 |
| `met with every team member individually` | ✅ 两个用法都对 |
| `high point` + `high-level questions` | ✅ 对比自然 |
| `make sb look silly` | ✅ |
| `speak their language` | ✅ |
| `come across as` | ❌ 未使用，用了 `No one feels that I'm a rookie` |

### 精修点

| 原文 | 问题 | 修正 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `to know their current situation` | `know` = 知道结果，不是"去了解" | `to get to know` / `to check in on` |
| `No one feels that I'm a rookie` | 没用到 `come across as` | `I don't come across as a rookie anymore` |

### 精修版
> `Well, at first I couldn't understand the team's jargon, let alone go in and speak their language. Afterwards, I found that the team had an internal technical wiki, so I consumed all the documentation at my own pace. About two weeks ago, I met with every team member individually to get to know their situation. Through all this, I found the biggest high point was that the team had a strong technical culture — when I asked high-level questions, people wouldn't get upset; they'd rather discuss with me. It didn't make me look silly. And now I can speak their language. I don't come across as a rookie anymore.`

### 整体评价
🏆 7 个语言点全部自然串联，时态从头到尾未出错。`let alone` 结构从第一轮断句错误 → 第二轮修复 → 第三/四轮满分，历史缺口彻底关闭。

## 识别出的知识差距（Changes to knowledge-gaps）

### 新发现的缺口
