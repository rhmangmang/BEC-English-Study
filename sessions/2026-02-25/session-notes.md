## 造句训练记录

### 语言点 1: invest heavily in / put significant investment into (在……投入大量资源)
- **来源**: 28BEC-20s 原文 "we've made a lot of investment in" (优化建议: invested heavily in)
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ⚠️ 否定陈述句: "We haven't invested heavily in customer feedback systems yet" — 首次尝试漏掉冠词且未译出“还”，第二次尝试修正为复数形式 + `yet` 完美 🏆
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "In which area do we need to invest heavily?" — 介词前置 `In which` 展示了正式英语的本能反应 🌟
  3. ✅ 反义疑问句: "The management team have invested heavily in the legacy system, haven't they?" — 完美匹配英式 `Management` 复数指代 🏆
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "In order to solve this problem, should we invest heavily in an in-house tool or spend some time sorting through third-party solutions?" — 平行结构精准，复用 `sort through` 亮点 🌟
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If we had invested heavily in this direction at the beginning, we would have a massive success now" — Mixed Conditional 结构稳固 ✅

### 语言点 2: I would say / would you say (我觉得…… / 你认为……)
- **来源**: 28BEC-20s 原文 "I would probably say like"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ⚠️ 否定陈述句: "I would say we're not ready to release it to the production environment yet" — 纠正了将 `prepare` (动作) 误用为 `ready` (状态) 的习惯 🏆
  2. ⚠️ 特殊疑问句: "What would you say is the biggest risk in the migration process?" — 掌握了插入语紧跟疑问词的地道语序 `What would you say is...`（非句尾悬空）🌟
  3. ✅ 反义疑问句: "I would say it was a better solution at that time, wasn't it?" — 识别出“观点帽子”不作 tag 核心的高阶逻辑 🏆
  4. ✅ 选择疑问句: "In order to simplify the process, would you say it is better to merge these features or completely delete them?" — 长句节奏感极佳，缝合了形式主语 `it` 和平行结构 🏆
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "If you had asked me yesterday, I would say the problem could be fixed now" — 精准使用 `could` 表达不确定性，克服了万物皆 `would` 的习惯 🌟

### 语言点 3: from a ... point of view / perspective (从……角度/层面看)
- **来源**: 28BEC-20s 原文 "from an integration point of view"
- **练习次数**: 5/5
- **掌握程度**: 极高
- **详细记录**:
  1. ⚠️ 否定陈述句: "From a financial point of view, our company didn't meet its targets in this quarter" — 纠正了口语化 `make it` 为商务通用表达 `meet targets` ✅
  2. ✅ 特殊疑问句: "From your perspective, what is the biggest challenge of this project?" — 满分
  3. ⚠️ 反义疑问句: "From the security perspective, the management have not passed the proposal yet, have they?" — 🔴 `past` (过去) → `passed` (动词过去分词) 拼写纠偏；使用 `proposal` 提升专业度
  4. ⚠️ 选择疑问句: "From the integration point of view, should we continue developing this feature or put it back to next month?" — 🔴 `put back by` → `put back to/until`（区分长度与时间点）✅
  5. ✅ 虚拟语气 (Mixed): "From a long-term perspective, if we had solved the integration issue earlier, this should have been a better product in this quarter." — 跨时线虚拟 + `earlier` 位置 + `should have been` 强预期推测，今日最高难度题顺利通关 🏆🏆🏆

## 重点错题记录

| 涉及知识点 | 原始错误句子 | 纠正后的正确表达 | 错误类型 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **词汇选择 (状态vs动作)** | we have not prepared... | we are not **ready** to... | 准备好是一门“状态”，用 `ready` 更地道 |
| **疑问插入语语序** | ...biggest risk you would say? | What **would you say is** the... | 观点类插入语紧扣疑问词，避免句尾悬空 |
| **商务地道表达** | didn't make it... | didn't **meet its targets** | 达成财务目标固定搭配: `meet targets` |
| **拼写混淆** | have not past... | have not **passed** | `past` (adj/prep) ≠ `passed` (verb) |
| **介词搭配** | put it back by next month | put it back **to/until** next month | `by` 接时长，`to/until` 接具体时间点 |
| **副词位置** | earlier solved... | solved... **earlier** | 时间副词放动宾之后更顺畅 |

## 涵盖的关键概念
- **`invest heavily in`**: 替换 `make a lot of investment` 的商务简洁表达。
- **插入语语序**: `Wh- + would you say + is/are...` 地道职场句式。
- **观点引导词**: `point of view`, `perspective`, `standpoint` 的灵活互换。
- **`passed` (verb) vs `past` (adj/prep)**: 彻底分清拼写与词性。
- **推测性情态动词**:
  - 过去: `must have / should have / could have + pp`
  - 现在/将来: `must / should / could + be/do`
