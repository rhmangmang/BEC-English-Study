### 2. 📝 核心语言点训练

#### LP 1: `with` 载体结构 (06BEC 提取) — 新学
> 结构：`[主语 + 动作] + with + [载体/工具/途径]`，描述"通过什么方式来实现目标"。

- **Round 1 (肯定陈述)**: "我们最终通过详细的数据分析来说服管理层采纳这个方案。"
  - **学生**: `We finally persuaded the management to adopt this proposal with detailed analysis.`
  - **评价**：✅ `persuaded...with detailed analysis` 用上了目标结构。遗漏了"数据"→ `data analysis`。
  
- **Round 2 (否定陈述)**: "光靠猜，我们是没法准确预估这次上线的工作量的。"
  - **学生**: `We cannot accurately estimate the workload for this launch with just guessing.`
  - **评价**：⚠️ `with just guessing` 语法正确但不够地道。母语者在这个结构中更倾向说 `by just guessing` / `on guesswork alone`。否定句中 `with` 载体结构容易产生歧义。

- **Round 3 (特殊疑问)**: "你们打算用什么工具来监控新系统的上线过程？"
  - **学生**: `With what tool do you monitor the launch process for the new system?`
  - **评价**：✅ 结构正确，但语序过于正式。日常更自然：`What tool are you using to monitor the launch process?`

- **Quick Fire**: 项目经理在 kick-off 会议上说："我们这次要依靠 R&D 部门的性能报告来说服客户。"
  - **学生**: `we need to persuade clients with performance reports that R&D department provides.`
  - **评价**：✅ 目标结构用对了。`marketing plan` → 更符合场景是 `rollout plan` / `launch plan`。

- **状态更新**：🟡 复用中 (基础结构掌握，否定句中存在地道性问题)

#### LP 2: `need to like` 结构 (08BEC 提取) — 新学
> 含义："要做好做某事的心理准备/能接受某事"，用在建议、前提条件场景。注意主语必须是"人"。

- **Round 1 (肯定陈述)**: "如果你想进咨询行业，你得能接受频繁出差。"
  - **学生**: `If you want to enter the advice industry, you need to like going on trip for business frequently.`
  - **评价**：⚠️ 目标结构 `you need to like` 用对了。但 `advice industry` ❌ → `consulting industry`；`going on trip` → `frequent business travel`。

- **Round 2 (否定陈述)**: "做这份工作，你不需要非得喜欢管人，但你得能接受干活出错后被人指出来。"
  - **学生**: `You don't need to manage people to do this job but you need to like being pointed out after you make something wrong.`
  - **评价**：🔴 前半句丢失了 `like`！`You don't need to manage` ❌ → `You don't need to **like** managing`。原意是"不需要喜欢管人"，不是"不需要管人"。后半句 `need to like being pointed out` 用对了方向，但 `being pointed out` 补充完整是 `having your mistakes pointed out` / `being corrected`。

- **Round 3 (一般疑问)**: "你觉得做一个项目经理，需要能接受各种不确定性吗？"
  - **学生**: `Do you think being a project manager needs to like undetermined things?`
  - **评价**：🔴 主语错误！`being a project manager needs to like` ❌。`need to like` 的主体**必须是"人"**，不能用岗位/活动作主语。正确：`Do you think **a project manager** needs to like dealing with uncertainty/ambiguity?`

- **Quick Fire**: Tech Lead 面试中对候选人说："做我们这行，得能接受不清楚的需求和不断变动的优先级。"
  - **学生**: `If you want to do the job, you need to like unclear requirements and continuous changed priorities.`
  - **评价**：✅ 目标结构正确，自然融入。`continuous changed priorities` → `continuously changing priorities` (进行时 vs 过去分词)。

- **状态更新**：🟡 复用中 (结构方向正确，但主语必须是人、不能丢失 `like`，需重点巩固)

#### LP 3: `take + [vehicle] + straight + to + [place]` (09BEC 提取) — 新学
> 描述"直接搭乘某交通工具前往某地，中间不停/不换乘"。

- **Round 1 (肯定陈述)**: "团队决定直接坐公司大巴去机场，这样就不用担心停车的问题了。"
  - **学生**: `The team decided to take the company bus straight to the airport, so we don't have to worry about parking.`
  - **评价**：✅ `take the company bus straight to the airport` 完美。前后主语略有不一致 (the team → we)，口语中可接受。

- **Round 2 (否定陈述)**: "上周我们没直接打车去会场，而是先去了公司取材料。"
  - **学生**: `Last week, we didn't take a taxi straight to the place. However, we went to the company to get materials first.`
  - **评价**：✅ 否定式 `didn't take a taxi straight to` 正确。`the place` → `the venue`（更精准）；`However` 语意偏转折 → 替代关系用 `Instead` 更自然（↔ `however` vs `instead` 辨析练习，已通过）。

- **Round 3 (选择疑问)**: "你今天早上的火车是直达会场的，还是中间需要换乘？"
  - **学生**: `Did you take the train straight to the conference center or did you change to another train in the middle?`
  - **评价**：✅ 结构正确。`change to another train in the middle` → `transfer`（更简洁地道）。

- **Quick Fire**: 安排出差，告诉团队成员已订好车从机场直达客户办公室。
  - **学生**: `Don't worry, I just placed an order to book a car, so it can take us straight to the client's place from the airport.`
  - **评价**：⚠️ 使用了 `take sb straight to` 变体（以人为宾语，非交通工具）。目标结构要求 `take + [vehicle]`，更精准：`I've booked a car to take us straight to the client's office`。`placed an order to book` 冗余 → `booked`。

- **状态更新**：🟢 已固化 (3轮核心+QF基本正确，结构简单不易出错)

### 3. 🔴 不足与待强化 (Improvement Areas)

1. **`need to like` 结构的两个关键陷阱**：
   - 主语必须是**"人"**，不能是岗位/活动 (`being a PM needs to like` ❌)
   - 否定句中**不能丢失 `like`** (`You don't need to manage` ❌ → `You don't need to **like** managing` ✅)
   - 这两个错误在本课中分别出现，需下轮 Flash Correct 拦截。

2. **不规则动词过去分词**：今日零复发 ✅，但需继续保持警惕。

3. **主谓一致 (S-V Agreement)**：`complaints **has** gone down` ❌ → `have`。复数主语的动词语态扫描仍需养成习惯。

4. **词汇精准性**：
   - `advice industry` ❌ → `consulting industry`
   - `undetermined things` ❌ → `uncertainty / ambiguity`
   - `stucked` ❌ → `stuck` (stick-stuck-stuck)

5. **`with` 载体结构否定句中的地道性**：`with just guessing` 在否定句中不如 `by just guessing` / `on guesswork alone` 自然。
